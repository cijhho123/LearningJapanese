"Classical Japanese: A Grammatical Compendium" - an open-courseware
reference guide to bungo (classical Japanese) grammar, aimed at students who
already have a working command of modern Japanese grammar. Covers the
classical conjugation system (the six bases, the auxiliary verbs, the
particles that differ from or vanished in modern Japanese), with worked
examples.

This is the first classical-Japanese grammar resource in this folder. The
Culture and Reading folders elsewhere in this repo hold classical texts
(Hojoki, Tosa Nikki) that assume this kind of grammar as background.

PDF, ~4.2 MB. Retrieved 2026-09-17.
