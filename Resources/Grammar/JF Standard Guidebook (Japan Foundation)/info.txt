"JF Standard for Japanese-Language Education: Guidebook for Users [New
Edition]", The Japan Foundation, JF Nihongo Kyoiku Standard project.

The Japan Foundation's own can-do-based framework for teaching, learning and
assessing Japanese, built around a "language competence tree" (linguistic,
sociolinguistic and pragmatic competences) and six CEFR-aligned levels
(A1-C2). This is the framework the Marugoto textbook series is designed
around, and the JF's institutional answer to "what should a learner be able
to do at each level", as opposed to a grammar-point curriculum.
