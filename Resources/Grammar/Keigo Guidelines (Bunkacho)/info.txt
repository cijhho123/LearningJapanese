"Keigo no Shishin" (Keigo Guidelines), Bunka Shingikai (Council for Cultural
Affairs) report, Bunkacho (Agency for Cultural Affairs), 2 February 2007 (Heisei 19).

The official, government-issued account of how modern keigo works and how it
should be used: the three-way sonkeigo / kenjougo / teineigo classification
(replacing the older two-way split), a chapter of concrete usage problems in
Q&A form (mixing sonkeigo and kenjougo, over-using keigo, "uchi/soto",
requests, regional variation), and reference material (the ministerial
referral that commissioned it, deliberation history, an index).

82 pages, PDF (permissions-restricted: printable and copyable, not editable).
