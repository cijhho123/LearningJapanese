"Nihongo Kyoiku no Sanshowaku" (Japanese-Language Education Reference
Framework), Bunka Shingikai Kokugo Bunkakai (Council for Cultural Affairs,
Japanese Language Subdivision) report, 12 October 2021 (Reiwa 3).

Japan's official, CEFR-modelled framework for teaching, learning and
assessing Japanese as a foreign/second language: the A1-C2 six-level scale
across the five language activities (listening, reading, spoken interaction,
spoken production, writing), roughly 500 Can-do descriptors, and guidance on
how proficiency tests map onto the framework. The Japan Foundation's own
JF Standard (see the neighbouring folder) predates this and JF Standard is
the one actually used to structure the Marugoto coursebooks; this is the
newer government (Bunkacho) reference that the field is now converging on.
