# Kansai-ben Primer (kansaibenkyou.net) - offline mirror

Source: <https://kansaibenkyou.net/> ("A learning resource for Kansai-ben (関西弁)").

A learner-facing primer on Kansai dialect, structured around parallel-track example dialogue: each
line of a conversation is given in Kansai-ben, in a grammar-annotated Kansai-ben form with inline
notes, in standard Japanese, and in English, so a reader can move between the dialect form and its
standard-Japanese equivalent line by line. The site also carries grammar-point articles, a
pronunciation/phonology section, a vocabulary word list, and a "real conversations" section of
recordings of native speaker pairs.

## Contents

682 files, 9.6 MB total:

- 12 standalone `.html` files at the top level (0.38 MB), one per example conversation, saved with
  human-readable English/Japanese titles (e.g. `01 - With the Landlord (大家さんと).html`) - an
  earlier, individually-saved copy of the same 12 conversations found under
  `kansaibenkyou.net/example-conversations/` below.
- `kansaibenkyou.net/` (670 files, ~9.2 MB), a mirror of the site:
  - `example-conversations/` - the same 12 scripted conversations (landlord, welcome party, shops,
    fish stall, directions, karaoke, train, neighbour, work, lunch break, izakaya, okonomiyaki),
    each page carrying all four parallel tracks (Kansai-ben, annotated Kansai-ben, standard Japanese,
    English) in one HTML file.
  - `grammar-points/` (45 pages), `phonology/` (16 pages), `words/` (280 pages) - the site's
    grammar, pronunciation and vocabulary reference sections.
  - `real-conversations/` (36 pages) - short English summaries and vocabulary notes for recordings
    of native-speaker pairs; these pages are not full transcripts of the recordings, the audio is
    the primary content and is not part of this mirror.
  - `introduction/`, `about/`, `feedback/`, `copyright/`, `bibliography/`, `development/`,
    `common-use-list/`, `resources/`, plus the site's CSS/JS/image assets.

Audio referenced from the example-conversation and grammar/phonology pages (`assets/audio/*.mp3`)
was excluded from the mirror; only the text tracks are present.
