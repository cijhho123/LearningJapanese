      大家さんと / With the Landlord

      Description:

      Atsushi leaves his apartment for work in the morning and meets his landlord cleaning out front.

      Characters:

      Mori Atsushi   The Landlord

      • Yeah, I am. My dog gets noisy if I don't take her for a walk. Since I'm out at around 6 anyways, I'm able to get the cleaning done while it's still not hot.
