      朝、コーヒー飲む？

      Shinichi and Sachiko talk about what they drink in the morning.

      Tags: male, female, middle age, casual, Sachiko, Shinichi

      Useful expressions:

      お茶の方がええと思えへん？
      Don't you think tea is better?

      朝はコーヒーやろ。
      In the morning, coffee is better.
