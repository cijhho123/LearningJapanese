      Particle “な”

      Particle “na”

      Common uses

      • Reminder
      • Soliciting and demonstrating agreement

      Grammar elements

      • な

      The standard Japanese final particle ね can become な in Kansai-ben.

      Kansai-ben vs. Standard

      Standard＿＿＿ね
      Kansai-ben＿＿＿な

      Examples

      Demanding agreement
      Standardいい天気だね。
      Kansai-benええ天気やな。
      EnglishThe weather is nice, isn't it?
      Request confirmation
      Standard明日、車貸してね。
      Kansai-ben明日、車貸してな。
      EnglishTomorrow, you're going to let me use the car, right?

      Commentary

      Strong requests using Vてぇな

      Extending the final え sound of the て-form of the verb makes the request stronger.

      Vてえな (strong request)
      Standard明日、車貸してよね(ー)。
      Kansai-ben明日、車貸してぇな(あ)。
      EnglishWould you please let me use your car tomorrow?
