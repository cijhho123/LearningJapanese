      Te-form and ta-form (verbs)

      Te-form and ta-form (verbs)

      Common uses

      • Non-functional

      Grammar elements

      • Te-form (verbs)
      • Ta-form (verbs)

      Looking from standard Japanese to Kansai-ben, there are some differences in the final vowels of the te-form? and ta-form? when the verb ends with [au], [ou], or [uu] sounds.

      The first case is for verbs where the dictionary form? is just two characters (かう, すう, etc.). In this case the sound before the う extends (vowel extension).

      The second case is for the other verbs. In this case the sound before the う can extend (vowel extension), but not necessarily.

      Examples

      The two character case
      -au-ou-uu
      StandardKansai-benStandardKansai-benStandardKansai-ben
      dictionary form?かうかう
      (kau)      		ようよう
      (you)      		すうすう
      (suu)
      te-form?かってこうて
      (koote)      		よってようて
      (yoote)      		すってすうて
      (suute)
      ta-form?かったこうた
      (koota)      		よったようた
      (yoota)      		すったすうた
      (suuta)

      -au-ou-uu
      StandardKansai-benStandardKansai-benStandardKansai-ben
      dictionary form?うたううたう

      (sasou)      		ふるうふるう
      (furuu)
      te-form?
      (long)      		うたってうとうて
      (utoote)      		さそってさそうて
      (sasoote)      		ふるってふるうて

      te-form
      (short)      		うとて
      (uto_te)      		さそて
      (saso_te)      		ふるて
      (furu_te)
      ta-form?
      (long)      		うたったうとうた
      (utoota)      		さそったさそうた
      (sasoota)      		ふるったふるうた
      (furuuta)
      ta-form
      (short)      		うとた
      (uto_ta)      		さそた(saso_ta)ふるた
