      会社で / At a Company

      Description:

      Mori has a presentation, but isn't feeling well. His coworker notices that he's pushing himself too hard and probably shouldn't be at work...

      Characters:

      Mori Atsushi   Kishimoto   Sugimoto Natsuko   Oshima
