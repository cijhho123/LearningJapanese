Some ~的 Entries

In a discussion on the mailing list the possibility of expanding the coverage of ~的 entries by including those covered in GG5 was discussed. I have looked at the ~的 headwords, and of the ~400 there 96 are not yet in JMdict. I have analyzed these and listed them below in three groups:

  • those where the XX的 entry is in another dictionary/glossary: LSD, JWN, Eijiro, etc.
  • those where the portion without the 的 is a JMdict entry;
  • the rest

The three block are below. If you want to create entries from them, an easy way is to paste them into the Add Multiple Entries page. Let me know what ones you enter and I will mark them off on this page.

Jim Breen
