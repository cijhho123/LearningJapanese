拘留(こうりゅう) する

put in custody ☆☆☆☆☆ NP
拘 (put in custody) + 留 (absent / stopped) = 拘留 (put in custody )

拘束(こうそく) する

restrain/restrict ☆☆☆☆☆ NP
拘 (put in custody) + 束 (bundle of sticks) = 拘束 (restrain/restrict)

to physically restrain (handcuffs / strait-jacket) to legally restrict (carbon emissions / immigration quotas / freedom of speech)
