辛 spicy / painful

立 (stand up) + 十 (ten)
★★★☆☆
7 strokes
JERKSTRONG

Onyomi

SHIN

When you eat the spicy peppers, your upper lip gets SHINY with sweat.

Mnemonic

This spicy sauce is hotter than ten peppers standing end to end.

Kunyomi

から*い spicy hot, not fresh-from-the-oven hot (BOOBOO)
★★★☆☆
つら＊い something is tremendously difficult or painful to do - (the life of a coal miner is hard!).

Not only does つらい have nothing to do with からい, but they both LOOK the same: 辛い、辛い. - so there's no way to tell which is which! (although usually つらい is written in hiragana, and からい is written with kanji ) 1/2 KANA
★★★☆☆

Jukugo

香辛料(こうしんりょう)

spices ★☆☆☆☆
香 (good smell) + 辛 (spicy / painful) + 料 (ingredients / fees) = 香辛料 (spices)

spices - not just hot ones. This is the only word you'll ever need the onyomi for. Sorry.

Lookalikes

Meaning Hint Radical

幸 luck, happiness TEN 十
宰 manager CROWN

Luck has a TEN on top, spicy does NOT. So you can say,

Good luck will bring you TEN TIMES AS MUCH happiness as good spices .

The man with the CROWN manages the country.
