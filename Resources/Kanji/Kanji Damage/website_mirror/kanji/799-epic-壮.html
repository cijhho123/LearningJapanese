壮 epic!!!

(guy so macho he has two dicks) + 士 (samurai radical)
★☆☆☆☆
6 strokes
SIDEKICK
Most dictionaries say this means 'robust', as 'in good health.' But more often, it means epic, magnificent, grandiose.
