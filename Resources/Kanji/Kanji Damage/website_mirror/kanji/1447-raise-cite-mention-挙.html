挙 raise / cite/ mention

(crab) + (handleft)
★★★☆☆
10 strokes
JERKDUPE

Onyomi

KYO

Keep Your Opinions to yourself until I ask you to cite or mention something.

Mnemonic

Raise your hand to grab the crab. Yum!

Kunyomi

( を ) あ＊げる To metaphorically bring up something (an objection, a point) to give an example of , to cite or mention something. But also - irritatingly - to literally raise one's hand (Oo! Oo! I know ! I know!) However, if you're raising anything else BUT your hand, you have to use 上げる instead of 挙げる. Yeah, I know, I know. What can you do?? NP
★★☆☆☆
( が ) あ＊がる something gets cited or mentioned in a report or article.
★★☆☆☆

Jukugo

選挙(せんきょ) する

an election ★★☆☆☆
選 (choose) + 挙 (raise / cite/ mention) = 選挙 (an election)
