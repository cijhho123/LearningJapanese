描 depict

Onyomi

BYOU

Sketch sort of wavy lines coming off of the bum, to depict his B.O. Flies also help.

Mnemonic

Use your hand to depict a flower growing in the middle of the rice field .

Kunyomi

か*く to sketch - a picture, as opposed to the other かく　(書く） which means to write words

描 (depict) + 写 (copy) = 描写 (depiction )

a depiction or representation of something (literally, as in 'this film contains graphic depictions of violence' or figuratively, as in 'Describe his personality to me.')
