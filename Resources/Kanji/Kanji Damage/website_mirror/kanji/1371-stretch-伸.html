伸 stretch

(personleft) + 申 (humbly say/god radical) Same-ON
★★★☆☆
7 strokes
DUPESOLO

Onyomi

SHIN

Mnemonic

A person tries to stretch up to the SHINing light of God, but always falls short.

Kunyomi

( を ) の*ばす to intentionally stretch , to extend.

Unlike the OTHER のばす　（延ばす）、伸ばす is more used with things: stretch your back, extend a collapsible umbrella/telescope/ admantium claw.
★★★★☆

( が ) の＊びる something automatically lengthens or grows - like hair or grass. Something extends further- a forest that extends all the way to the ocean, or days of summer that get longer.
★★★☆☆

Jukugo

背伸び(せのび) する

to stretch your body ☆☆☆☆☆
背 (stature / back of the body) + 伸 (stretch) = 背伸び (to stretch your body)
