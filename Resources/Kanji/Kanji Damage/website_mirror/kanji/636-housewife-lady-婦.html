婦 housewife / lady

女 (woman) + (snout) + (swordswallower)
★★★☆☆
11 strokes
BETAFRILL PAIR
Is this a Fucked Radical? There's nothing inherently bad about the right-hand radicals "snout + swordswallower" (remember, they're just names I made up) - but the only other two kanji that have that particular combo are "go home" and "clean house." So you be the judge. . .

Also, as a suffix, it means 'lady' or 'female version of xxx.'

Onyomi

FU

Mnemonic

That is FUCKED up that while you're out working , your housewife is fucking a pig-snouted sword-swallower.
