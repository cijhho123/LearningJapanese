現 reality

王 (king) + 見 (look)
★★★★☆
11 strokes
DUPE
Is this one a JERK? The kun-yomi meanings don't quite match the jukugo meanings. But . . . if something appears or is displayed for you, that means you're aware that it's real now. So maybe not a JERK after all!

Onyomi

GEN

Reality happens AGAIN every day.

Mnemonic

Only the king can see what's really going on in the kingdom. That's why he is king!

Kunyomi

( を ) あらわ＊す to display something, to make something manifest.

Second meaning: something becomes clear.
★★★☆☆

( が ) あらわ＊れる something appears - symptoms, your soul-mate, attackers, rappers that bite my style crop up all over the place since I blew up.
★★★☆☆

Jukugo

figure of speech ★★★★☆

現実(げんじつ)

reality ★★★☆☆
現 (reality) + 実 (truth) = 現実 (reality)

現場(げんば)

the current place ★★★☆☆ KUN ON
現 (reality) + 場 (place) = 現場 (the current place)

the current place. (this one is used on the 'YOU ARE HERE' part of maps)
現在(げんざい)

the present time ★★☆☆☆
現 (reality) + 在 (be real) = 現在 (the present time)

the present time - but also can mean "the present situation" or "the staus quo."
