向 turn to face

(goatee) + 丶 (dot)
★★★★☆
6 strokes

Onyomi

KOU

Mnemonic

Let's say the dot is a dot of cocaine.

So now we can say . . .

When your moms turns to face the mirror, she realizes she's got a dot of cocaine stuck on the upper portion of her goatee. Uncomfortable!!

Kunyomi

む*こう over there (it's also a slang, street term for 'those guys', the other side)
★★★★☆
( を ) む＊く I turn this way, turn that way.
★★★☆☆
( を or に ) む＊け to be made for XXX (i.e. shoes for women is 女性向けのshoes) .
Second meaning: make X face Y. If I train a firehose on a dog, it's hoseをdogに向ける。
★★★☆☆
( に ) む＊かう something turns this or that way.
★★☆☆☆

Jukugo

方向(ほうこう)

direction ★★★★☆
方 (direction / method / person) + 向 (turn to face) = 方向 (direction)

転向(てんこう)

do an about-face ★★★☆☆ CCW
転 (roll over) + 向 (turn to face) = 転向 (do an about-face)

convert to new ideology, do an about-face. This is important because most of the freaky looking kids you see on the street will all become housewives and salarimen the exact second they turn ２3. - making TENKOU what we call a "cultural code word."
