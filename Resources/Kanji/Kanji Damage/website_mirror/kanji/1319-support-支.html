支 support

十 (ten) + 又 (again/crotch radical)
★★★★★
4 strokes

Onyomi

SHI

SHE supports abortion and the death penalty.

Mnemonic

The pimp supports ten crotches, and he is responsible for their care, feeding, financial management, and discipline.

Kunyomi

ささ＊える support - usually literal: a crutch supports a heavy tree limb or an injured person.
★★★★☆

Jukugo

支援(しえん) する

support ★★☆☆☆ NP
支 (support) + 援 (assist) = 支援 (support)

support - usually used with support groups like "adult children of alcoholics" or "a heartwarming show of support for a city which has had a natural disaster". It's not limited to victims but it has that sort of nuance to it.
