残 remain behind

(shhh) + (shish kebab)
★★★★☆
10 strokes

Onyomi

ZAN

After all the people have died (or been eaten) nothing but ZANbies will remain behind.

Mnemonic

Ssssh!! Don't tell him I left his shish kebab behind at the picnic.

残念(ざんねん)

That's too bad!! ★★★★★
残 (remain behind) + 念 (concern) = 残念 (That's too bad!!)

"That's too bad." - kind of informal ; say this when someone didn't enjoy a movie they went to, or couldn't come to your party because they got the flu.
