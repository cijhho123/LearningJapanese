嬉 stoked

女 (woman) + 喜 (rejoice) Same-ON
★★★★☆
15 strokes
SOLOILL PAIR

Onyomi

KI

Mnemonic

I'm stoked that my woman KEEPS on rejoicing in me.

Kunyomi

うれ*しい to be really happy - but it also has a connotation of gratitude. You get 嬉しい when you get a present from someone, but not when the sun comes up and it's a rad-weather day. (BOOBOO: unlike "よろこぶ," you usually use 'ureshii' about yourself.) 1/2 KANA
