Onyomi

Mnemonic

The more I detest (insert name of personal enemy), the more my heartbeat gains speed.

Kunyomi

にく*らしい hateful, just odious, terribly immoral.
★★☆☆☆
にく＊む to hate or detest! Not like "I hate sports" or "I hate Insane Clown Posse," but like "I detest Hitler and female genital mutilation."
