印 stamp

(Eazy) + (fingerprint)
★★★☆☆
6 strokes

Onyomi

IN

You have to really push the stamp IN the paper to leave a mark.

Mnemonic

The capital "E" on eazy-E's stamp is like his fingerprint: a unique signature which signifies that he has notarized the documents.

Kunyomi

しるし a logo or symbol - as in a check mark on a list, or a cross, or the black flag logo


★★☆☆☆

Jukugo

印鑑(いんかん)

chop / seal ★★★☆☆
印 (stamp) + 鑑 (expert opinion) = 印鑑 (chop / seal)

one's seal or stamp with one's name on it, which the Japanese use instead of signatures.
第一印象(だいいちいんしょう)

first impression. ★★★☆☆
第一 (first or primary) + 印象 (impression) = 第一印象 (first impression.)
