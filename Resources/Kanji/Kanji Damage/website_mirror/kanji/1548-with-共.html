共通点(きょうつうてん)

common point ★★★☆☆
共 (with) + 通 (pass) + 点 (point) = 共通点 (common point)

common point (between two things or people)
公共(こうきょう)

public ★★☆☆☆
公 (public) + 共 (with) = 公共 (public)

public, as in "public toilet" or "public telephone"
共産主義(きょうさんしゅぎ)

communism ★★☆☆☆
共 (with) + 産 (childbirth, production of things) + 主義 (- ism) = 共産主義 (communism)

共有(きょうゆう)

jointly owned ★★☆☆☆
共 (with) + 有 (exist) = 共有 (jointly owned)

shared / communal / jointly owned things.
