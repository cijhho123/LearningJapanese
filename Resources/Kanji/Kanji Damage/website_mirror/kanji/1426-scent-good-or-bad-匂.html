匂 scent (good or bad)

(wrap) + (heel)
★★★☆☆
4 strokes
DUPE
A little synonym-tell-'em-apart is in order;

匂い　(におい) = Smell, good or bad.

臭い　(におい) = Bad smell.

香り　 (かおり) = good smell.

匂い emphasizes less the particular KIND of smell, and more SMELL as opposed to taste, touch, sound, etc.

Onyomi

Mnemonic

The socks which wrap your heel (and toes) have a great scent recently.

Kunyomi

にお* う Smell this! Does this milk smell fresh?
☆☆☆☆☆
にお*い a smell. Sometimes bad, sometimes, good. Usually not so good.

Can also be used metaphorically, like "This reeks of conspiracy!" (陰謀の匂い！) 1/2 KANA
