覚 bear in mind

(mohawk) + 見 (look)
★★★★☆
12 strokes

Onyomi

KAKU

Mnemonic

Please bear in mind that the mohican is looking at your COCK right now.

Kunyomi

( を ) おぼ*える to memorize something. (i.e. for a test, or because you're a spy and you need to remember the password) or bear in mind (to be nicer to Grandma)
★★★★☆
( を ) さ＊ます to metaphorically wake someone up - to dissilusion them or make them realize something
★☆☆☆☆
( が ) さ＊める someone comes to their senses, wakes up, gets sober, realizes something they thought was not, in fact, true.
★☆☆☆☆

Jukugo

幻覚(げんかく)

hallucination ★★☆☆☆
幻 (illusion) + 覚 (bear in mind) = 幻覚 (hallucination)

a visual hallucination
感覚(かんかく)

physical sense ☆☆☆☆☆
感 (feeling) + 覚 (bear in mind) = 感覚 (physical sense)

a feeling or sense (touch / taste / smell / hearing, etc.)
