横 side / arrogant

木 (tree) + 黄 (yellow) Same-ON
★★★★☆
5 strokes
JERKPN
When it uses the onyomi, it means 'arrogant', but when it uses the kunyomi, it means 'side.'

Onyomi

OU

Mnemonic

OH SHIT, the side of the tree is yellow with dog piss. Those arrogant dogs!

Kunyomi

よこ side (BOOBOO: not left or right side, that's "gawa". Yoko is like I'm standing beside him, or, she's lying on her side, sideways.)
★★★☆☆
