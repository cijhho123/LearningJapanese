Onyomi

BOU / MOU

Mnemonic

Kunyomi

のぞ*む to wish or desire. (the possible as well as impossible)
★★★☆☆
のぞ＊み an almost-impossible or impossible wish: a faint and idle dream. "Someday I'll marry Tom Cruse." "Someday I'll be President of Moldovia."
★★☆☆☆

Jukugo

希望(きぼう) する

request ★★★☆☆
希 (request / uncommon) + 望 (desire) = 希望 (request)

A common-place, easily possible wish: "I'd like an aisle seat." "I'd like to fly nonstop."　希望 might be better translated as 'request': 'Customers wishing to request a child-seat for their infant please see the waiter.'
高望み(たかのぞみ) する

aim too high ★☆☆☆☆
高 (tall) + 望 (desire) = 高望み (aim too high)

to aim too high, to be over-ambitious.
