程 extent

(wheat) + 口 (mouth/small box radical) + 王 (king)
★★☆☆☆
12 strokes

Onyomi

TEI

TAKE off your clothes to the extent that you cannot be shown on broadcast tv.

Mnemonic

This wheat is popular to the extent that it is even fed to the mouth of the king!

Kunyomi

ほど an extent.- You shouldn't joke to that extent. You shouldn't drink to that extent. He's not good at ska-bashing to the extent his father was. And so on. KANA
★★★☆☆

Jukugo

程度(ていど)

a degree or extent ★★★☆☆
程 (extent) + 度 (times) = 程度 (a degree or extent)

成程(なるほど)

oh, I see! ★★★☆☆ KUNKUNKANA
成 (become) + 程 (extent) = 成程 (oh, I see!)

"ohhhh, now I see!" (very similar to a hearty 分かった！！　（わかった）, but it also has the nuance of, understanding AND agreeing whole-heartedly)
過程(かてい)

a process ★★☆☆☆
過 (surpass / too much) + 程 (extent) = 過程 (a process)

程々(ほどほど) にｘｘｘｘ

don't do XXX too much! ★☆☆☆☆ KUNKUN
