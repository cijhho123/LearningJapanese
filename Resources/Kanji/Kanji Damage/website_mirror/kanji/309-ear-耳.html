耳 ear

★★★★☆
6 strokes
SOLOPK

Onyomi

Mnemonic

This one looks like eye (目) but with a bunch of lines sticking out from it. Right? OK, so let's try to remember it like this: The ear is a sense organ like the eye, and the lines sticking out are way too many piercings.
