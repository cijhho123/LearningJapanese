整 arrange

束 (bundle of sticks) + (director) + 正 (correct)
★★★☆☆
16 strokes

Onyomi

SEI

Mnemonic

When the DIRECTOR SAYS, you gotta arrange these sticks into a bundle, you do it, no questions asked, because she's always CORRECT.

Kunyomi

( を ) ととの＊える to arrange or prepare - usually used with "prepare a hairstyle", or "clean up a sentence on a re-write of a paper", and not used anywhere else. Sometimes books will refer to someone who is pretty as having a 整った顔 (totono-ta kao) (literally: a well-arranged face!)
★★☆☆☆
( が ) ととの＊う something is arranged in a certain formation.
