散 scatter

(hellbunny) + 月 (moon/organ) + (director)
★★★☆☆
12 strokes

Onyomi

SAN

SANTA Claus scatters presents all over the world.

Mnemonic

The hellbunnies tear the director apart and scatter his organs in all directions.

Kunyomi

ち＊らす to scatter, but in a GOOD way - seeds, information, sprinkles on a cupcake (also sometimes means, to distract someone's attention, which seems a little arbitrary).
★☆☆☆☆
ち＊らかす to scatter, in a negative sense: Yo Mama's thong is scattered all over the room! (and half of the adjoining room as well)
★★★☆☆

Jukugo

散歩(さんぽ) する

a stroll ★★★☆☆
散 (scatter) + 歩 (walk) = 散歩 (a stroll)

a stroll, to walk with no particular destination
解散(かいさん) する

band breaks up ★☆☆☆☆
解 (solve / untie) + 散 (scatter) = 解散 (band breaks up)
