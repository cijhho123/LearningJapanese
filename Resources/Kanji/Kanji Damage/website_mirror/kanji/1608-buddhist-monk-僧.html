僧 buddhist monk

(personleft) + 曽 (gain) Same-ON
★☆☆☆☆
13 strokes
F
This is a somewhat formal kanji. Most people just say お坊さん　（おぼうさん） for monk in casual conversation.

Onyomi

SOU

The monk is SO wise.

Mnemonic

A monk is a person who gains enlightenment

Kunyomi

そう a somewhat formal, official way to refer to a monk LAZYF
☆☆☆☆☆

Jukugo
