至 until

(decapitated cow) + 土 (earth)
★★☆☆☆
6 strokes
SOLO
There's no really useful jukugo but you'll sometimes see 至 as a PREFIX meaning, 'the most'.

Onyomi

SHI

Mnemonic

SHE decapitated the cow until its head fell down and hit the earth.

Kunyomi

( に ) いた＊る to get to, to arrive. To extend to. Until the very top/bottom/end. (至るところ= everywhere, wall-to-wall) (xxx至るまで= all the way to xxx, even to the extent of xxx)
