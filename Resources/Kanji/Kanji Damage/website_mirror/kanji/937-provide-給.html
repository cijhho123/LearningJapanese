給 provide

糸 (string) + 合 (to suit)
★★☆☆☆
12 strokes
BETA
Sorry! The top 2 jukugo mean the same exact thing and are even 99% same-SOUNDING but you need both of them.

Onyomi

KYUU

Mnemonic

If you provide me with the string, I'll make a CUTE suit that suits you.

Jukugo

給料(きゅうりょう)

wages ★★★☆☆
給 (provide) + 料 (ingredients / fees) = 給料 (wages)

wages - a bit more useful than the related word 給与. For instance, 'payday' is 給料日 (きゅうりょうび)、 not 給与日.
供給(きょうきゅう)

supply ★★☆☆☆
供 (follower) + 給 (provide) = 供給 (supply)

to supply (supply and demand / supply the troops with guns / supply your kid with a school lunches / the feed chute supplies grain to the conveyor belt at the cereal factory)
給与(きゅうよ)

salary, wages ★☆☆☆☆
給 (provide) + 与 (bestow) = 給与 (salary, wages )

Synonyms

provide or supply
与える    供給   
salary, wages
