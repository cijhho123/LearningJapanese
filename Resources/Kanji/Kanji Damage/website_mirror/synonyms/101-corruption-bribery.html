   corruption, bribery

堕落(だらく) Corrupt in one’s personal life – not usually political corruption. Drugs, lies, trife behavior.
 
汚職(おしょく) Political corruptions – graft, kickbacks, sweetheart deals, bribery, cronyism, etc.   NP
 
賄賂(わいろ) Bribery
 
横領(おうりょう) Embezzlement
 
使込む(つかいこむ) Embezzlement. 横領 and 使い込む mean the same thing, and are both used in casual conversation, but only 横領 is used in newspapers or at legal trials.
