#  氏 mr. /speculum radical

★★★★☆

4 strokes

[PK](../tags/18.html)[STRONG](../tags/24.html)[JERK RADICAL](../tags/29.html)[ILL PAIR](../tags/10.html)

JERK: this is a legit kanji, meaning 'Mr.', BUT as a radical, the keyword is 'speculum.' 

Although 氏 is pronounced SHI, it is STRONG for TEI!!!! 

## Onyomi

SHI |  SHE is a member of the speculum family.
