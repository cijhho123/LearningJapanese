## Jukugo

希望(きぼう) する |  request ★★★☆☆   
[希](1095-request-uncommon-希.html) (request / uncommon) + [望](449-desire-望.html) (desire) = 希望 (request)  A common-place, easily possible wish: "I'd like an aisle seat." "I'd like to fly nonstop." 希望 might be better translated as 'request': 'Customers wishing to request a child-seat for their infant please see the waiter.'  
---|---  
希少(きしょう) な |  rare animals or plants ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[希](1095-request-uncommon-希.html) (request / uncommon) + [少](193-a-little-amount-少.html) (a little (amount)) = 希少 (rare animals or plants)  useless, academic word for rare animals or plants.
