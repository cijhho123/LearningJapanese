## Jukugo

突然(とつぜん) |  suddenly! ★★★☆☆   
[突](404-thrust-突.html) (thrust) + [然](411-nature-然.html) (nature) = 突然 (suddenly!)  suddenly! Like 急に, but you can also use 突然 if you're sending someone an unexpected mail, or making a last-minute plan.  
---|---  
衝突(しょうとつ) |  impact / conflict ★☆☆☆☆   
[衝](1241-collision-衝.html) (collision) + [突](404-thrust-突.html) (thrust) = 衝突 (impact / conflict)  means impact (car crash) but also a conflict (India and Pakistan are on a collision course) or mental conflict (his communism conflicts with his love for the sexy CIA agent).
