## Jukugo

以上(いじょう) |  more than / that's all ★★★★☆ [JERK](../tags/11.html)   
[以](1721-compared-to-以.html) (compared to) + [上](123-above-上.html) (above) = 以上 (more than / that's all)  ２ meanings. usually said when you finish ordering something (food, copies, condoms) ； 'That'll be all.' It also means, "on top of all that" or "more than" (used like xxx以上、or sometimes also 以上 のｘｘｘ）  
---|---  
上手(じょうず) な |  skilllfully done! ★★★★☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[上](123-above-上.html) (above) + [手](293-hand-手.html) (hand) = 上手 (skilllfully done!)  skillfully done! Usually used to praise one's friends' efforts (at art, music, cooking, etc.)  
上級(じょうきゅう) の |  advanced-level ★★★☆☆   
[上](123-above-上.html) (above) + [級](627-level-級.html) (level) = 上級 (advanced-level)  advanced level. When you finish this book, you'll still be a beginner of kanji but you'll have advanced level skills at making people aware of problems in life that their Moms might face.  
目上の人(めうえ) のひと |  higher person ★☆☆☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) + [上](123-above-上.html) (above) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 目上の人 (higher person)  someone who out-ranks you - at work, school, or in society.
