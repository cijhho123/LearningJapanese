## Jukugo

忠実(ちゅうじつ) な or に |  faithful ★★☆☆☆   
[忠](490-loyalty-忠.html) (loyalty) + [実](964-truth-実.html) (truth) = 忠実 (faithful)  someone who is faithful in doing his/her duties. Also means like a movie which is faithful to the book, or a translation which is faithful to the original. Or even a forgery which copies the original dollar bill completely well.  
---|---  
忠告(ちゅうこく) する |  admonish ★☆☆☆☆   
[忠](490-loyalty-忠.html) (loyalty) + [告](807-inform-告.html) (inform) = 忠告 (admonish)  admonishing negative advice: "Don't open that door!" "Don't swim naked!"
