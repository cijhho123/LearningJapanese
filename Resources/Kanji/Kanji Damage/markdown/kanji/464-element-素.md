## Jukugo

素晴らしい(すばらしい) |  the best! ★★★☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[素](464-element-素.html) (element) + [晴](470-weather-get-good-now-晴.html) (weather get good now) = 素晴らしい (the best!)  the best! Usually used about art or theatrical performances.   
A way to remember the pronunciation is that the ON-yomi of 素 is 'SU', while the KUN-yomi of 晴 is 'hareru'. . . which, because 晴 is the second kanji in the word, the 'ha' turns to 'BA'.   
So maybe KUN-ON is a more accurate tag, but even then it's hard to remember, so FP it is!  
---|---  
素材(そざい) |  raw material ★★☆☆☆   
[素](464-element-素.html) (element) + [材](586-raw-materials-材.html) (raw materials) = 素材 (raw material)  raw material - both literally and figuratively (as in, the raw data that you base your essay or report or novel on) ("I'm working on materal for my novel!")  
酸素(さんそ) |  oxygen. ☆☆☆☆☆ [FR](../tags/9.html)   
[酸](619-sour-酸.html) (sour) + [素](464-element-素.html) (element) = 酸素 (oxygen.)  oxygen - which is kind of a Fucked Roots if you think about it - "sour element?" What have they got against oxygen?!? What SHOULD we be breathing?  
二酸化 炭素(に さんか たんそ) |  carbon dioxide ☆☆☆☆☆   
[二](2-two-二.html) (two) + [酸](619-sour-酸.html) (sour) + [化](64-transform-化.html) (transform) + [炭](832-carbon-炭.html) (carbon) + [素](464-element-素.html) (element) = 二酸化 炭素 (carbon dioxide)
