## Jukugo

御飯(ごはん) |  rice ★★★★☆ [KANA](../tags/12.html)   
[御](1470-the-honorific-o-御.html) (the honorific "o") + [飯](924-rice-meal-飯.html) (rice / meal) = 御飯 (rice)  two meanings - 1) cooked rice, 2) a meal  
---|---  
御手洗い(おてあらい) |  toilet, man. Toilet!!!!!!! ★★★☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[御](1470-the-honorific-o-御.html) (the honorific "o") + [手](293-hand-手.html) (hand) + [洗](811-wash-洗.html) (wash) = 御手洗い (toilet, man. Toilet!!!!!!!)  a shitter  
御覧(ごらん) |  please look sir ★★☆☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[御](1470-the-honorific-o-御.html) (the honorific "o") + [覧](689-view-覧.html) (view) = 御覧 (please look sir)  a formal way of asking your superior to view something. either 'ごらん ください' or 'ごらん に なる.'  
御免なさい(ごめんなさい) |  sorry ★☆☆☆☆ [KANA](../tags/12.html)   
[御](1470-the-honorific-o-御.html) (the honorific "o") + [免](1211-exemption-license-免.html) (exemption / license) = 御免なさい (sorry)  sorry (usually KANA, but it's worth learning to see the roots of the expression: 'Honorably exempt me (from your wrath!)')
