## Jukugo

兵士(へいし) |  infantryman, low-ranking soldier ★★☆☆☆   
[兵](556-soldier-兵.html) (soldier) + [士](245-samurai-radical-士.html) (samurai radical) = 兵士 (infantryman, low-ranking soldier)   
---|---  
兵器(へいき) |  arms ★★☆☆☆   
[兵](556-soldier-兵.html) (soldier) + [器](403-instrument-器.html) (instrument) = 兵器 (arms)  arms: unlike the related term 武器(ぶき）, 兵器 generally means things that are very modern and big: bazookas, missiles, rocket launchers, howitzers.
