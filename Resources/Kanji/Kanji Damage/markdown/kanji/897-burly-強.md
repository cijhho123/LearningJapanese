## Jukugo

勉強(べんきょう) する |  To study ★★★★★   
[勉](1215-try-hard-勉.html) (try hard) + [強](897-burly-強.html) (burly) = 勉強 (To study)  To study. Used about learning a whole subject, such as Irish History or Thermodynamics. (BOOBOO: 勉強 takes months or years, as opposed to 学ぶ （まなぶ） which means to learn one thing, and can take a second.)  
---|---  
強調(きょうちょう) する |  emphasize ★★★☆☆   
[強](897-burly-強.html) (burly) + [調](1636-check-out-調.html) (check out) = 強調 (emphasize)   
強制(きょうせい) |  compulsory ★★☆☆☆ [ABU](../tags/1.html)   
[強](897-burly-強.html) (burly) + [制](805-system-制.html) (system) = 強制 (compulsory)  compulsory, forced. "Do it, slave!"  
強面(こわもて) の |  rugged, thuggy ★☆☆☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[強](897-burly-強.html) (burly) + [面](813-front-surface-face-面.html) (front surface / face) = 強面 (rugged, thuggy)  ：someone who is rugged, thuggy, street-looking.
