#  陰 shady

[![](../assets/radsmall/town-5b6cedad9082f836d90ce4150a4c12639ea53c5e5b033e84df6ee78a96d1dddb.jpg)](782-town.html) (town) \+ [今](943-now-今.html) (now) \+ [![](../assets/radsmall/order-rule-32b89b9bd28ad2086b01311815f5483fce223ba87fe0919020f69732725e9b5c.jpg)](1244-decapitated-cow.html) (decapitated cow)

[DUPE](../tags/6.html)

Unlike the OTHER shadow kanji (影) which is literal, this shadow kanji (陰) is more figurative: things that are shady, dark, secret, or hidden. 

陰 is also the kanji used for naughty bits.
