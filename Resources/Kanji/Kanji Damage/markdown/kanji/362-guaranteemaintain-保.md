保守主義(ほしゅしゅぎ) |  conservative ★★☆☆☆ [NEO](../tags/15.html)   
[保](362-guaranteemaintain-保.html) (guarantee/maintain ) + [守](991-protect-守.html) (protect) + [主](454-mastermainly-主.html)[義](877-righteousness-義.html) (- ism) = 保守主義 (conservative)  politically or morally conservative.  
健康保険(けんこうほけん) |  health insurance ★★☆☆☆   
[健](1156-healthy-健.html)[康](1663-health-康.html) (health) + [保](362-guaranteemaintain-保.html)[険](792-steep-risky-険.html) (insurance) = 健康保険 (health insurance)   
保護(ほご) する |  conserve, protect ★☆☆☆☆   
[保](362-guaranteemaintain-保.html) (guarantee/maintain ) + [護](269-defend-護.html) (defend) = 保護 (conserve, protect)  conserve, protect : used about protecting nature and the environment. But usually used with police protection (for snitches) or if you're escorting a lost child or drunk to the police box. (BOOBOO: conservative person is 保守的な （ほしゅてきな） 人)  
保証人(ほしょうにん) |  a guarantor ★☆☆☆☆   
[保](362-guaranteemaintain-保.html)[証](284-proof-証.html) (guarantee) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 保証人 (a guarantor)  a guarantor. This may not sound like a useful word to know, but if you ever come to Nippon, you'll need a Japanese guarantor. For pretty much everything- getting an apartment, a visa, etc. Usually it's your boss. So if you lose your job, your boss can kick you out of your house AND the country. It's a sort of share-cropper system!
