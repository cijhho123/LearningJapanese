## Jukugo

納得(なっとく) |  consent ★★★☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[納](216-to-supply-納.html) (to supply) + [得](1237-bargain-obtain-得.html) (bargain / obtain) = 納得 (consent)  give your consent. Often used in documents.  
---|---  
収納(しゅうのう) |  storage ★☆☆☆☆   
[収](96-get-収.html) (get) + [納](216-to-supply-納.html) (to supply) = 収納 (storage)  storage - similar to the word 倉庫 (そうこ）, but 収納 is in the same building: the broom closet at your fast food restaurant is 収納 , but the shack in your backyard is 倉庫.  
を or の 納入(のうにゅう) する |  deliver/pay ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[納](216-to-supply-納.html) (to supply) + [入](60-putgo-in-入.html) (put/go in) = 納入 (deliver/pay)  to pay, supply or deliver - more formal than 配達（はいたう）
