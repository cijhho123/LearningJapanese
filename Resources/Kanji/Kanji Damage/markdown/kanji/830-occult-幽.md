## Jukugo

幽霊(ゆうれい) |  ghost ★★★☆☆   
[幽](830-occult-幽.html) (occult) + [霊](1395-ghost-霊.html) (ghost) = 幽霊 (ghost)  a vengeful ghost - unlike 悪霊（あくりょう） and 妖怪 （ようかい）, 幽霊 were once human and driven by revenge. 幽霊 are more popular in horror movies (like The Ring), whereas 妖怪 are more traditional.
