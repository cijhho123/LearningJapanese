#  判 judgement

[半](186-half-半.html) (half) [Same-ON](../tags/63.html) \+ [刀](164-sword-刀.html) (sword) 

★★★☆☆

7 strokes

This used to mean "Official seal or stamp which stamps a document, making that document official." I guess a lot of those documents were the judgements of the ruler, because nowadays the kanji just means "to judge".

## Jukugo

批判(ひはん) する |  criticism ★★★☆☆   
[批](298-call-bullshit-on-批.html) (call bullshit on) + [判](188-judgement-判.html) (judgement) = 批判 (criticism)  criticism, like "I call bullshit on the government!" or "I call bullshit on your opinion of an athlete!"  
---|---  
判断(はんだん) する |  judge ★★★☆☆   
[判](188-judgement-判.html) (judgement) + [断](387-decisionjudgementrefuse-断.html) (decision/judgement/refuse) = 判断 (judge)  to judge, to deliver a verdict - but also judge like 'I wouldn't wear that skirt if I was her.'  
判子(はんこ) |  seal ★★★☆☆ [KUN ON](../tags/36.html)   
[判](188-judgement-判.html) (judgement) + [子](5-child-子.html) (child) = 判子 (seal)  The little stamps that people here use instead of signatures on official documents.  
評判(ひょうばん) |  reputation ★★☆☆☆   
[評](189-art-or-literary-criticism-評.html) (art or literary criticism) + [判](188-judgement-判.html) (judgement) = 評判 (reputation)
