## Jukugo

無料(むりょう) |  free ★★★★☆   
[無](264-without-無.html) (without) + [料](1585-ingredients-fees-料.html) (ingredients / fees) = 無料 (free)  free (not like Martin Luther King, I mean free like a free magazine full of articles on how to become a stripper)  
---|---  
料理(りょうり) つくる |  cuisine/cooking ★★★★☆   
[料](1585-ingredients-fees-料.html) (ingredients / fees) + [理](445-reason-理.html) (reason) = 料理 (cuisine/cooking)  ２ meanings.   
1： cooking , as in, "My hobby is cooking. I'm cooking right now, I can't talk."   
2： cuisine, as in, "My favorite cuisine is Mexican. My favorite cuisine is Indonesian."   
(BOOBOO: You don't say, though, "I'm going to buy some ryouri." Or "I'm eating ryouri right now." )  
料金(りょうきん) |  a fee, also, bills. ★★★☆☆   
[料](1585-ingredients-fees-料.html) (ingredients / fees) + [金](909-gold-金.html) (gold) = 料金 (a fee, also, bills.)   
給料(きゅうりょう) |  wages ★★★☆☆   
[給](937-provide-給.html) (provide) + [料](1585-ingredients-fees-料.html) (ingredients / fees) = 給料 (wages)  wages - a bit more useful than the related word 給与. For instance, 'payday' is 給料日 (きゅうりょうび)、 not 給与日.  
香辛料(こうしんりょう) |  spices ★☆☆☆☆   
[香](377-good-smell-香.html) (good smell) + [辛](149-spicy-painful-辛.html) (spicy / painful) + [料](1585-ingredients-fees-料.html) (ingredients / fees) = 香辛料 (spices)  spices - not just hot ones. This is the only word you'll ever need the onyomi for. Sorry.
