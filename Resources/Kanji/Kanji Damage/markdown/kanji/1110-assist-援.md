## Jukugo

を or に 援助(えんじょ) する |  to assist ★★★☆☆   
[援](1110-assist-援.html) (assist) + [助](1490-save-助.html) (save) = 援助 (to assist)  to compensate someone for a favor they have done you, to aid someone with money or material things. When high schoolers date salarimen for money, it's enjo kousai.(援助交際）  
---|---  
支援(しえん) する |  support ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[支](1319-support-支.html) (support) + [援](1110-assist-援.html) (assist) = 支援 (support)  support - usually used with support groups like "adult children of alcoholics" or "a heartwarming show of support for a city which has had a natural disaster". It's not limited to victims but it has that sort of nuance to it.  
応援(おうえん) する |  cheeer on ★☆☆☆☆ [KANA](../tags/12.html)   
[応](1128-react-応.html) (react) + [援](1110-assist-援.html) (assist) = 応援 (cheeer on)  to cheer for or root for someone at a game.
