## Jukugo

政治(せいじ) |  politics ★★★★☆ [FR](../tags/9.html)   
[政](708-politics-政.html) (politics) + [治](140-cure-治.html) (cure) = 政治 (politics)  politics. (FR：literally "administer and cure",which is a bit of a laugh)  
---|---  
治癒(ちゆ) |  healing ☆☆☆☆☆   
[治](140-cure-治.html) (cure) + [癒](1355-cure-癒.html) (cure) = 治癒 (healing)  healing in general : (the healing process / an unhealed wound / taking too long to heal)  
治療(ちりょう) |  therapy ☆☆☆☆☆   
[治](140-cure-治.html) (cure) + [療](1364-medical-therapy-療.html) (medical therapy) = 治療 (therapy)  medical therapy - for instance, cancer 治療 would be chemo. Unlike the related term 医療 （いりょう）、治療 refers to ongoing, repeated-once-a-day/week processes for specific illnesses)
