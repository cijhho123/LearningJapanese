## Jukugo

機械(きかい) |  machine ★★★★★   
[機](675-machine-機.html) (machine) + [械](1531-contraption-械.html) (contraption) = 機械 (machine)  A machine, particularly something with a motor in it which moves when activated. A computer is not a 機械 , but a robot arm is.  
---|---  
機械化(きかいか) |  mechanization ★☆☆☆☆   
[機](675-machine-機.html)[械](1531-contraption-械.html) (machine) + [化](64-transform-化.html) (transform) = 機械化 (mechanization )  the mechanization of a society or of an industry.
