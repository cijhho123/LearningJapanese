## Onyomi

|  Though it's used in several jukugo, you don't need to learn the ONyomi: 込む is strictly KUNKUN.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  When a lot of people move and go in a place it gets crowded.  
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

( を ) こ＊める |  I put something (usually feelings or emotions or passion, strength) into something (a present, an effort). Or I just put bullets into a fuckin' gun. Seriously, my J-dictionary uses both examples.   
★★★☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

申し込む(もうしこむ) |  application ★★★☆☆   
[申](1367-humbly-say-god-radical-申.html) (humbly say) + [込](331-get-crowded-込.html) (get crowded) = 申し込む (application)  an application (for a loan or to go to college, etc)  
---|---  
人込み(ひとごみ) |  crowd of people ★★☆☆☆   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [込](331-get-crowded-込.html) (get crowded) = 人込み (crowd of people)  a crowd of people- (BOOBOO : not necessarily all people doing the same thing(i.e. 'the crowd at the Sinatra show'). It means 'crowds in general,' as in, 'It gets crowded after 4 PM.' )  
詰め込む(つめこむ) |  to cram, jam, pack. ★☆☆☆☆   
[詰](249-cram-in-詰.html) (cram in) + [込](331-get-crowded-込.html) (get crowded) = 詰め込む (to cram, jam, pack. )
