## Jukugo

時間(じかん) |  time ★★★★★   
[時](1002-time-時.html) (time) + [間](162-a-period-of-time-間.html) (a period of time) = 時間 (time)   
---|---  
間違い(まちがい) |  a mistake ★★★★★ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[間](162-a-period-of-time-間.html) (a period of time) + [違](1577-different-and-therefore-wrong-違.html) (different and therefore wrong) = 間違い (a mistake)  Verb form; 間違える！  
ｘｘｘ 間(かん) |  xxx amount of time ★★★★★ [SUF](../tags/37.html) xxx amount of time. You can't just say "one year" like in English. You have to add 'kan', the equivalent of saying, in English, 'one year's time.'  for instance:  一年間 (いちねんかん）:one year's time  三週間 (さんしゅうかん）: three weeks' time  With months, it gets even more complex: you have to add 間 at the end AND ヶ (pronounced 'ka') in the middle, like this:  三ヶ月間 (さんかげつかん）: three months' time  
人間(にんげん) |  human ★★★★☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [間](162-a-period-of-time-間.html) (a period of time) = 人間 (human)   
に 間に合う(まにあう) |  make it in time ★★★☆☆   
[間](162-a-period-of-time-間.html) (a period of time) + [合](936-to-suit-合.html) (to suit) = 間に合う (make it in time)  to make it in time - catching your train or not being late for class (this is the main time you use the "MA" pronunciation)
