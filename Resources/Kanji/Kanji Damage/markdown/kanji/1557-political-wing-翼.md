#  翼 political wing

[羽](1532-feathers-羽.html) (feathers) \+ [異](1556-to-differ-異.html) (to differ) 

★★☆☆☆

17 strokes

[PN](../tags/19.html)

Originally this meant a literal wing, but nowadays it's more common to see it used about politics. 

## Onyomi

YOKU |  The right wing wants to place the YOKE of oppression around the necks of foreigners.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  In English we talk about left and right wings of politics. In Japanese, apparently they use left and right FEATHERS!!!  So you can say, "The left and the right feather differ in their opinions."  
---|---
