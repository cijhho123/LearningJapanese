保護(ほご) する |  conserve, protect ★☆☆☆☆   
[保](362-guaranteemaintain-保.html) (guarantee/maintain ) + [護](269-defend-護.html) (defend) = 保護 (conserve, protect)  conserve, protect : used about protecting nature and the environment. But usually used with police protection (for snitches) or if you're escorting a lost child or drunk to the police box. (BOOBOO: conservative person is 保守的な （ほしゅてきな） 人)
