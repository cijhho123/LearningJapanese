## Jukugo

自転車(じてんしゃ) |  bicycle ★★★★★   
[自](78-my-own-自.html) (my own) + [転](1266-roll-over-転.html) (roll over) + [車](1058-car-車.html) (car) = 自転車 (bicycle)   
---|---  
運転(うんてん) する |  drive a vehicle ★★★★☆   
[運](1071-carry-luck-運.html) (carry / luck) + [転](1266-roll-over-転.html) (roll over) = 運転 (drive a vehicle)   
転向(てんこう) |  do an about-face ★★★☆☆ [CCW](../tags/4.html)   
[転](1266-roll-over-転.html) (roll over) + [向](1640-turn-to-face-向.html) (turn to face) = 転向 (do an about-face)  convert to new ideology, do an about-face. This is important because most of the freaky looking kids you see on the street will all become housewives and salarimen the exact second they turn ２3. - making TENKOU what we call a "cultural code word."  
転勤(てんきん) |  transfer ☆☆☆☆☆   
[転](1266-roll-over-転.html) (roll over) + [勤](423-be-employed-at-勤.html) (be employed at) = 転勤 (transfer)  when a salariman gets transferred to another office, that's tenkin.  I only mention this because most of the Japanese you meet who speak good English, probably one of their parents got tenkin-ed to America / UK, so it tends to come up in conversation.
