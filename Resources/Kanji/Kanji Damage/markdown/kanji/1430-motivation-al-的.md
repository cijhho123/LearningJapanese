## Jukugo

XXX 的(てき) |  -al ★★★★★ [SUF](../tags/37.html) Mostly this is used as a SUFFIX which turns a noun into an adjective. Like how politics becomes 'political,' or how education becomes 'educational.' or economics becomes 'economical.' In Japanese, 政治 becomes 政治的な.  
---|---  
目的(もくてき) |  motive, not only for crimes. ★★★★☆   
[目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 目的 (motive, not only for crimes.)   
自動的(じどうてき) な |  automatic ★★★★☆   
[自](78-my-own-自.html) (my own) + [動](1078-movement-動.html) (movement) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 自動的 (automatic)   
理想的(りそうてき) な |  ideal. ★★★☆☆   
[理](445-reason-理.html) (reason) + [想](352-ideaimagination-想.html) (idea/imagination) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 理想的 (ideal.)   
基本的(きほんてき) な |  the basics ★★★☆☆   
[基](1705-basis-基.html) (basis) + [本](338-book-the-real-本.html) (book / the real) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 基本的 (the basics)  the basics (the emphasis is: the minimum knowledge)  Also means fundamentally (emphasizing the essential bits that various things or ideas have in common)  
合理的(ごうりてき) な |  rational OR reasonable ★☆☆☆☆   
[合](936-to-suit-合.html) (to suit) + [理](445-reason-理.html) (reason) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 合理的 (rational OR reasonable )  rational OR reasonable (i.e. get up at a reasonable hour or a reasonable compromise - like 'logical,' but used even if there is not a mathematical proof involved. Also has the nuance of efficient - the boss says to the worker 仕事合理的に進めなさい！ （しごと ごうりてき に すすめ なさい！）, meaning, 'Stop wasting our time and money! Work in a more efficient, rational way.'
