## Jukugo

根本(こんぽん) |  the fundamentals ★★★☆☆   
[根](781-root-根.html) (root) + [本](338-book-the-real-本.html) (book / the real) = 根本 (the fundamentals)  the fundamentals (how is it different from 基本？ Well, with 根本, the emphasis is: the fundamental, deep, organizing principles, not the most easy 'for dummies' bits). The essential and underlying - as opposed to on the surface and easily apparent) Also handy:こんぽんてきな (the adjective form)  
---|---  
屋根(やね) |  roof ★★☆☆☆ [KUN ON](../tags/36.html)   
[屋](1260-store-屋.html) (store) + [根](781-root-根.html) (root) = 屋根 (roof)  roof . That's right, not root. Roof!  
根気(こんき) のよい / のわるい |  patience ★★☆☆☆   
[根](781-root-根.html) (root) + [気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) = 根気 (patience)  patience. You need 根気 to be able to 我慢 (がまん）。   
Often used like 根気がいい（she's very patient!) or, 根気よくｘｘｘした！ (you did xxx very patiently!)  
根性(こんじょう) |  balls ★★☆☆☆   
[根](781-root-根.html) (root) + [性](475-sex-essential-nature-性.html) (sex / essential nature) = 根性 (balls)  guts /balls (for instance, giving yourself cigarette burns to prove some kind of point about being a man)
