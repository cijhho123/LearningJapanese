## Jukugo

一応(いちおう) |  for now, for the time being, ★★★☆☆   
[一](1-one-line-radical-一.html) (one) + [応](1128-react-応.html) (react) = 一応 (for now, for the time being,)   
---|---  
反応(はんのう) |  a reaction ★★☆☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[反](739-anti-反.html) (anti) + [応](1128-react-応.html) (react) = 反応 (a reaction )  a reaction - despite the 反 kanji, 反応 is not neccesairly a bad reaction. As a noun, it's value-free. (careful! This is pronounced NOU, not OU)  
応援(おうえん) する |  cheeer on ★☆☆☆☆ [KANA](../tags/12.html)   
[応](1128-react-応.html) (react) + [援](1110-assist-援.html) (assist) = 応援 (cheeer on)  to cheer for or root for someone at a game.
