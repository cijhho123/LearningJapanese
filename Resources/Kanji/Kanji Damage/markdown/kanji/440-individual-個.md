## Jukugo

個人(こじん) の |  personal ★★★☆☆   
[個](440-individual-個.html) (individual) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 個人 (personal)  personal, single-serving (one-to-one session with a teacher, personal pizza)(BOOBOO: don't confuse this with private - private is こじんてきな 個人的な)  
---|---  
一個(いっこ) |  small thing ★★★☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)[COUNTER](../tags/38.html)   
[一](1-one-line-radical-一.html) (one) + [個](440-individual-個.html) (individual) = 一個 (small thing)  one small, usually roundish, thing - orange, apple, ipod  
個室(こしつ) |  private room ★★☆☆☆   
[個](440-individual-個.html) (individual) + [室](1261-room-suffix-室.html) (room suffix) = 個室 (private room)
