## Jukugo

教師(きょうし) |  teacher ★★☆☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[教](710-teach-教.html) (teach) + [師](1619-master-teacher-師.html) (master, teacher) = 教師 (teacher)  teacher - more fomal than 先生。  
---|---  
医師(いし) |  doctor ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[医](1047-doctor-医.html) (doctor) + [師](1619-master-teacher-師.html) (master, teacher) = 医師 (doctor)  a more formal word for doctor -(you wouldn't say "I need to see the 医師!" - you'd say, "I need to see the 医者（いしゃ）！”）  
師匠(ししょう) |  master craftsman ★☆☆☆☆   
[師](1619-master-teacher-師.html) (master, teacher) + [匠](692-artisan-匠.html) (artisan) = 師匠 (master craftsman)  master craftsman, who teaches disciples and apprentices. If he's awesome but doesn't have apprentices, you don't use this one.
