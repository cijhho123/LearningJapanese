## Jukugo

目的(もくてき) |  motive, not only for crimes. ★★★★☆   
[目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 目的 (motive, not only for crimes.)   
---|---  
目立つ(めだつ) |  to stand out in a crowd. ★★★☆☆   
[目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) + [立](148-stand-up-立.html) (stand up) = 目立つ (to stand out in a crowd.)   
XXX 番目(ばんめ) のYYY |  thing in a row ★★★☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)[COUNTER](../tags/38.html)   
[番](395-number-番.html) (number) + [目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) = 番目 (thing in a row)  this is a term used in counting things in a row. For instance, in English we'd say "Second book from the right" or "Third shelf from the top" in Japanese, they'd say: 2番目のbook OR 4番目のshelf.  
駄目(だめ) |  no way! ★★★☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)[KANA](../tags/12.html)   
[駄](414-bad-quality-駄.html) (bad quality) + [目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) = 駄目 (no way!)  this has so many meanings: useless, bad, 'stop!' or just 'no way!'  
目次(もくじ) |  table of contents ★★☆☆☆   
[目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) + [次](956-next-次.html) (next) = 目次 (table of contents)   
目撃者(もくげきしゃ) |  a witness to a crime. ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) + [撃](1300-charge-撃.html) (charge) + [者](255-professional-者.html) (professional) = 目撃者 (a witness to a crime.)
