## Jukugo

災害(さいがい) |  natural disaster ★★★☆☆   
[災](1733-disaster-災.html) (disaster) + [害](462-damage-害.html) (damage) = 災害 (natural disaster)  natural disaster - earthquake, fire, flood, your mother's flattus, etc.  
---|---  
火災(かさい) |  disaster fire ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[火](47-fire-火.html) (fire) + [災](1733-disaster-災.html) (disaster) = 火災 (disaster fire)  a slightly more newspaper-ish word for a harmful blaze.  
災難(さいなん) |  disaster ★☆☆☆☆   
[災](1733-disaster-災.html) (disaster) + [難](422-difficult-難.html) (difficult) = 災難 (disaster)  this means disaster - not in the sense of earthquakes or tsunami. Disaster in the sense of 'How was your trip to Tahiti?' 'Oh my god, it was a total disaster!!! First we missed the plane, then I got an infection, then on top of all that our hotel was run by zombies!' 災難 carries the sense of a bunch of random unconnected misfortunes. It never rains, but it pours, kind of thing.
