#  論 make a case for

[言](11-say-言.html) (say) \+ [![](../assets/radsmall/barbershop-f2d6b0b4528060c82d9e052b36951f2ee619c37044023e15fa08b015b079da6b.jpg)](971-bookstore.html) (bookstore)

[BETA](../tags/2.html)

Unlike the related term 議 (which also means to discuss)、論 is not reciprocal. I.e. you need two people to 議, but only one to 論.

## Jukugo

論文(ろんぶん) |  essay ★★☆☆☆   
[論](972-make-a-case-for-論.html) (make a case for) + [文](92-culture-sentence-文.html) (culture/ sentence) = 論文 (essay)  an essay, like a senior thesis.  
---|---  
世論(よろん) |  public opinion ★★☆☆☆   
[世](1029-society-世.html) (society) + [論](972-make-a-case-for-論.html) (make a case for) = 世論 (public opinion)   
理論(りろん) |  theory ★☆☆☆☆   
[理](445-reason-理.html) (reason) + [論](972-make-a-case-for-論.html) (make a case for) = 理論 (theory)  theory, as in science or "theory vs. practice"  
討論(とうろん) |  discussion ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[討](989-attackdiscuss-討.html) (attack/discuss) + [論](972-make-a-case-for-論.html) (make a case for) = 討論 (discussion)
