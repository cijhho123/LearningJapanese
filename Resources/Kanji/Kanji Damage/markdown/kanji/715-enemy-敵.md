## Jukugo

素敵(すてき) |  Wonderful! ★★★☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[素](464-element-素.html) (element) + [敵](715-enemy-敵.html) (enemy) = 素敵 (Wonderful!)  Wonderful! Magnificent! This is usually used only by women, and it is used in reference to things made or done by a third party - you wouldn't say your friend's cooking was すてき, but you'd say a dress your friend wore was すてき.  
---|---  
無敵(むてき) な |  unrivaled ★★☆☆☆   
[無](264-without-無.html) (without) + [敵](715-enemy-敵.html) (enemy) = 無敵 (unrivaled)   
敵対(てきたい) |  rival ★☆☆☆☆   
[敵](715-enemy-敵.html) (enemy) + [対](1010-against-対.html) (against) = 敵対 (rival)
