## Jukugo

全部(ぜんぶ) |  all of it ★★★★★   
[全](919-all-全.html) (all) + [部](795-section-部.html) (section) = 全部 (all of it)   
---|---  
全然(ぜんぜん) |  absolutely! ★★★★☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[全](919-all-全.html) (all) + [然](411-nature-然.html) (nature) = 全然 (absolutely! )  absolutely! (usually used to emphasize the word after it. But sometimes, the word being emphasized is omitted because it's so obvious (to Japanese))  If you hear someone say 'Zenzen!' with no word after it, it might mean 'zenzen daijoubu,' ('Absolutely no problemo.') . . . OR it might mean 'zen zen dame!' ('No effing way, guy!')  
全員(ぜんいん) |  all members ★★★★☆   
[全](919-all-全.html) (all) + [員](544-clerk-員.html) (clerk) = 全員 (all members)  all the members of the group  
全国(ぜんこく) |  nationwide ★★☆☆☆   
[全](919-all-全.html) (all) + [国](438-country-国.html) (country) = 全国 (nationwide)  nationwide,national (as in a national contest)
