## Jukugo

適当(てきとう) な |  appropriate ★★★★☆ [JERK](../tags/11.html)   
[適](716-suitable-適.html) (suitable) + [当](634-hit-the-target-当.html) (hit the target) = 適当 (appropriate)  appropriate, as in an appropriate gift. However, 適当 also means irresponsible! Doing something without considering the consequences. 適当な人間だ！ “He's irresponsible. He'll say anything that pops into his head.”  
---|---  
適齢期(てきれいき) |  a marriageable age ☆☆☆☆☆   
[適](716-suitable-適.html) (suitable) + [齢](1034-stage-of-life-齢.html) (stage of life) + [期](1706-period-of-time-期.html) (period of time) = 適齢期 (a marriageable age)
