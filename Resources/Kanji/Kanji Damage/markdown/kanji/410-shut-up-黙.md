## Jukugo

沈黙(ちんもく) |  bad kind of silence ★★☆☆☆   
[沈](1692-sink-沈.html) (sink) + [黙](410-shut-up-黙.html) (shut up) = 沈黙 (bad kind of silence)  silence. (BOOBOO: not to be confused with 静か （しずか）). 静か means a quiet place. 沈黙 is like a awkward hush that falls over the crowd when your mom's g-string fell off at the superbowl.
