## Jukugo

家族(かぞく) |  family ★★★★★   
[家](1223-home-家.html) (home) + [族](1048-family-族.html) (family) = 家族 (family)  your immediate family - parents, grandparents, siblings.  
---|---  
民族(みんぞく) |  ethnic ★★★☆☆   
[民](1165-folk-民.html) (folk) + [族](1048-family-族.html) (family) = 民族 (ethnic)  two meanings: 1) ethnic group, 2) folk (as in folk customs, folk music, etc)
