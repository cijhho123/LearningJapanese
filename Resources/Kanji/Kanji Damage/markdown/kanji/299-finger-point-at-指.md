## Jukugo

指摘(してき) する |  To point out a shortcoming ★★☆☆☆   
[指](299-finger-point-at-指.html) (finger / point at) + [摘](718-pluck-摘.html) (pluck) = 指摘 (To point out a shortcoming)  To point out a shortcoming - You can use it about your friends. Or "The critic pointed out the shortcomings of the play."  
---|---  
人差し指(ひとさしゆび) |  index finger ★★☆☆☆   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [差](874-discriminate-差.html) (discriminate) + [指](299-finger-point-at-指.html) (finger / point at) = 人差し指 (index finger)  index finger (literally, "the people-pointing finger")  
指示(しじ) |  instructions ★☆☆☆☆   
[指](299-finger-point-at-指.html) (finger / point at) + [示](200-show-altar-radical-示.html) (show) = 指示 (instructions )  instructions ー not like instructions from your boss, but like the instruction manual you get with your new vacuum cleaner.
