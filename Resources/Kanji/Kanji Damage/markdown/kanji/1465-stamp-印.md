## Jukugo

印鑑(いんかん) |  chop / seal ★★★☆☆   
[印](1465-stamp-印.html) (stamp) + [鑑](1440-expert-opinion-鑑.html) (expert opinion) = 印鑑 (chop / seal)  one's seal or stamp with one's name on it, which the Japanese use instead of signatures.  
---|---  
第一印象(だいいちいんしょう) |  first impression. ★★★☆☆   
[第](894-rank-or-number-in-series-第.html)[一](1-one-line-radical-一.html) (first or primary) + [印](1465-stamp-印.html)[象](1220-elephant-phenomenon-象.html) (impression) = 第一印象 (first impression.)
