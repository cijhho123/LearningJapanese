物語(ものがたり) |  tale ★★☆☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)[NOKURI](../tags/43.html)   
[物](1201-animal-thing-物.html) (animal / thing) + [語](1574-language-語.html) (language) = 物語 (tale)  a tale- often used in the title of a book or movie. As in 'the tale of genji' 'tale of two cities.'
