## Jukugo

誕生日(たんじょうび) の日 |  birthday ★★★★☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[誕](1158-birthday-birth-誕.html)[生](473-life-birth-生.html) (birth) + [日](23-sun-day-日.html) (sun, day) = 誕生日 (birthday)  birthday If you want to say Happy Birthday, you'd just say 良い誕生日！ （よいたんじょうび！）.  However, if you're asking someone what day is their birthday, you'd add 'の日’ like this:  
誕生日の日はイツ？ （たんじょうびのひはいつ？）
