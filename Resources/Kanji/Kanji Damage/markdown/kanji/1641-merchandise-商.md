商店街(しょうてんがい) |  shopping district. ★★★★☆   
[商](1641-merchandise-商.html) (merchandise) + [店](1132-shop-店.html) (shop) + [街](1272-shopping-district-街.html) (shopping district) = 商店街 (shopping district.)  shopping district. Not quite a mall, but a street with a tin roof, and all the shops are decorated similarly.Like all the stores on that street formed a kind of merchants' association and tried to give their street some atmosphere.  
商売(しょうばい) |  a business ★★★★☆   
[商](1641-merchandise-商.html) (merchandise) + [売](564-sell-売.html) (sell) = 商売 (a business)  a business, a line of work: (the camera business, the pimp game, the clock business, etc.)
