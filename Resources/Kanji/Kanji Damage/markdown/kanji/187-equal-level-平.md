## Jukugo

平気(へいき) |  ok? ★★★★★ [KIDS](../tags/64.html)   
[平](187-equal-level-平.html) (equal / level) + [気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) = 平気 (ok?)  it's ok with me. Usually phrased as a question, as in, 平気？ "Is that ok with you?" This is only used by young people - more often young women.  
---|---  
平和(へいわ) |  peace ★★★☆☆   
[平](187-equal-level-平.html) (equal / level) + [和](369-peace-japan-和.html) (peace/ Japan) = 平和 (peace)   
平日(へいじつ) |  weekday ★★★☆☆   
[平](187-equal-level-平.html) (equal / level) + [日](23-sun-day-日.html) (sun, day) = 平日 (weekday)   
公平(こうへい) |  fairness (opp. 不公平:ふこうへい) ★★☆☆☆   
[公](137-public-公.html) (public) + [平](187-equal-level-平.html) (equal / level) = 公平 (fairness (opp. 不公平:ふこうへい))   
平等(びょうどう) に |  impartial, even ★☆☆☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[平](187-equal-level-平.html) (equal / level) + [等](1005-equal-etcplural-等.html) (equal /etc./plural) = 平等 (impartial, even)  impartial, even-steven. (to cut the cake into equal pieces, to treat your adopted kids and your biological kids equally)
