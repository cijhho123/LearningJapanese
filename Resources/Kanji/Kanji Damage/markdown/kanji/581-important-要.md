## Jukugo

重要(じゅうよう) な |  essential ★★★★☆   
[重](1059-heavy-overlap-重.html) (heavy / overlap) + [要](581-important-要.html) (important) = 重要 (essential)   
---|---  
要点(ようてん) |  the important points ★★☆☆☆   
[要](581-important-要.html) (important) + [点](126-point-点.html) (point) = 要点 (the important points)  the important points (unlike the related word 重点（じゅうてん）、 要点 is more than one point - more like a summary. Like instead of writing an essay, you'd just do a series of bullet points.   
Example phrase:要点だけ話して！ ("Get to the point!")  
要求(ようきゅう) する |  demand ★★☆☆☆   
[要](581-important-要.html) (important) + [求](725-demandask-求.html) (demand/ask) = 要求 (demand)  demand - more strident than the related word 求める （もとめる）  
需要(じゅよう) |  demand ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[需](1408-demand-需.html) (demand) + [要](581-important-要.html) (important) = 需要 (demand)  demand in the economic sense - supply and demand This does NOT get to turn into a verb. You don't use する. You'd say 需要がある (there's a demand for xxx) or perhaps 需要を 満たす （じゅようをもたす） (there's enough of xxx to meet the demand).
