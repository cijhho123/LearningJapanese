#  句 verse of a poem

[![](../assets/radsmall/wrap-86aa15479e6390b02816855f88f6873be3d5231d32d8588e2e0570221173e67c.jpg)](1411-wrap.html) (wrap) \+ [口](9-mouth-small-box-radical-口.html) (mouth/small box radical) [Same-ON](../tags/63.html)

★★★☆☆

5 strokes

[BETA](../tags/2.html)

Don't confuse 句(verse) with 可! 

可 is straight lines, because it's the nail radical - nails are straight! 

But, 句 uses the 'wrapping' radical, (not hard iron, but soft paper or cloth), so it's round and organic.
