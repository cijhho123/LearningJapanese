#  ![](../assets/radsmall/order-rule-32b89b9bd28ad2086b01311815f5483fce223ba87fe0919020f69732725e9b5c.jpg) decapitated cow

[ム](136-moocow-ム.html) (moocow) \+ [一](1-one-line-radical-一.html) (one/line radical) 

This is basically the lower half of moocow, plus a line on top, as if to say, "OMG, THAT COW GOT THE TOP CUT OFF!" After all, normally cows are quite round on top, or spikey if you include the horns. 

![](../visualaids/decapitatedbefore.jpg)

But if a cow has got a straight line on top, it might go a little something like this: 

![](../visualaids/decpitated.jpg)

And the decapitated cow sometimes is drawn with a little line in the center（![](../assets/radsmall/order-rule-2-top.jpg)）- this is symbolic of a cow that was decapitated quite recently and there's hence still a little blood fountain coming out the neck. Logical? You bet! I think of everything! 

![](../visualaids/decapitatedblood.jpg)
