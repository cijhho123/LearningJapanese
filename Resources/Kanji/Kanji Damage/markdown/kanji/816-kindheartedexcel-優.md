#  優 kindhearted/excel

[![](../assets/radsmall/man-d0fa8d3e87b0dcd06a7777a6693f057bfe7d041f88edfa20c6663c61cf324435.jpg)](61-person-人.html) (personleft) \+ [憂](815-grieve-憂.html) (grieve) [Same-ON](../tags/63.html)

★★★★☆

17 strokes

[JERK](../tags/11.html)[DUPE](../tags/6.html)[PN](../tags/19.html)

JERK: by itself, it means nice, but in a JUKUGO, it means EXCEL, or, for some reason, ACTOR. 

Also this kanji has the distinction of being a DOUBLE dupe: there's two やさしいs AND two すぐれるs!

## Mnemonic

|  YOU are such a kindhearted person to grieve for the loss of my mom's cousin's great-aunt twice removed, whom you've never even met! In other words, a kindhearted PERSON often excels at grief.  
---|---
