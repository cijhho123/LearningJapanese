---|---  
に 感謝(かんしゃ) する |  gratitude ★★★☆☆   
[感](755-feeling-感.html) (feeling) + [謝](1597-apologize-謝.html) (apologize) = 感謝 (gratitude)  The most common, general word for gratitude.  
感動(かんどう) する |  emotionally moved ★★☆☆☆   
[感](755-feeling-感.html) (feeling) + [動](1078-movement-動.html) (movement) = 感動 (emotionally moved)  to be moved by something (a song, a movie, a speech, a pretty poem about asndfisdnf)
