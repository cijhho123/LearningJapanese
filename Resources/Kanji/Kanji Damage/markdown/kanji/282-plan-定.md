## Jukugo

予定(よてい) |  an informal plan ★★★★★   
[予](1329-beforehand-予.html) (beforehand) + [定](282-plan-定.html) (plan) = 予定 (an informal plan)  an informal plan (like to meet your friends for a movie)  
---|---  
設定(せってい) する |  establish ★★★☆☆   
[設](1295-establish-設.html) (establish) + [定](282-plan-定.html) (plan) = 設定 (establish)  to establish a regulation or rule - also it is often seen on the 'setup' or 'preferences' menus of software when you install it.  
決定(けってい) する |  make a serious decision ★★★☆☆   
[決](984-decide-決.html) (decide) + [定](282-plan-定.html) (plan) = 決定 (make a serious decision)  to make a real serious decision； like, "I know I'm always saying I'm going to lose 1２ pounds but this time I really mean it."  
定義(ていぎ) |  definition ★★☆☆☆   
[定](282-plan-定.html) (plan) + [義](877-righteousness-義.html) (righteousness) = 定義 (definition)  a definition (hey! recursion!)
