## Jukugo

印鑑(いんかん) |  chop / seal ★★★☆☆   
[印](1465-stamp-印.html) (stamp) + [鑑](1440-expert-opinion-鑑.html) (expert opinion) = 印鑑 (chop / seal)  one's seal or stamp with one's name on it, which the Japanese use instead of signatures.  
---|---  
図鑑(ずかん) |  a field guide ★★☆☆☆   
[図](1587-diagram-map-図.html) (diagram/ map) + [鑑](1440-expert-opinion-鑑.html) (expert opinion) = 図鑑 (a field guide)  a field guide; a book where each page has one picture and a description of a thing. (birds, gandams, monsters, doinks, whatever).  
鑑賞(かんしょう) |  discriminating eye ★☆☆☆☆   
[鑑](1440-expert-opinion-鑑.html) (expert opinion) + [賞](1628-prize-賞.html) (prize) = 鑑賞 (discriminating eye)  to have a 'discriminating eye' for art. To appreciate art or music on some deep, expert level.  
鑑定(かんてい) |  expert opinion ★☆☆☆☆   
[鑑](1440-expert-opinion-鑑.html) (expert opinion) + [定](282-plan-定.html) (plan) = 鑑定 (expert opinion)  expert opinion. Like an American Idol judge or art critic or some guy in court who says that tobacco does/does not cause cancer.
