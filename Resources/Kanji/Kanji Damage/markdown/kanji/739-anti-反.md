## Jukugo

反対(はんたい) する |  against it!!!!!!!!!! ★★★★☆   
[反](739-anti-反.html) (anti) + [対](1010-against-対.html) (against) = 反対 (against it!!!!!!!!!!)  to be against something  
---|---  
反則(はんそく) |  foul! To break a rule! ★★☆☆☆   
[反](739-anti-反.html) (anti) + [則](546-rule-則.html) (rule) = 反則 (foul! To break a rule!)   
反抗(はんこう) する |  rebel ★★☆☆☆   
[反](739-anti-反.html) (anti) + [抗](1283-oppose-抗.html) (oppose) = 反抗 (rebel)  to be rebellious, defiant. To start a revolt. Unlike the related words 抵抗 （ていこう） and 対抗 （たいこう）、 反抗 has the nuance of 'rebel for the fuck of it,' like a teenager would.  
反応(はんのう) |  a reaction ★★☆☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[反](739-anti-反.html) (anti) + [応](1128-react-応.html) (react) = 反応 (a reaction )  a reaction - despite the 反 kanji, 反応 is not neccesairly a bad reaction. As a noun, it's value-free. (careful! This is pronounced NOU, not OU)
