## Jukugo

展覧会(てんらんかい) |  exhibition ★★☆☆☆   
[展](1541-exhibit-展.html) (exhibit) + [覧](689-view-覧.html) (view) + [会](1269-big-meeting-会.html) (big meeting) = 展覧会 (exhibition)  an art show or art exhibition.  
---|---  
御覧(ごらん) |  please look sir ★★☆☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[御](1470-the-honorific-o-御.html) (the honorific "o") + [覧](689-view-覧.html) (view) = 御覧 (please look sir)  a formal way of asking your superior to view something. either 'ごらん ください' or 'ごらん に なる.'  
博覧会(はくらんかい) |  exposition ★☆☆☆☆   
[博](1017-museum-extensive-博.html) (museum /extensive) + [覧](689-view-覧.html) (view) + [会](1269-big-meeting-会.html) (big meeting) = 博覧会 (exposition)  a GIANT exposition. Not like, "Cezanne's greatest fuckin' paintings all up in here festival" but huger - like a World's Fair. To break it down: 博覧会 (biggest kind of expo ) 展示会（てんじかい＝trade show) 展覧会 （てんらんかい＝art exhibition)
