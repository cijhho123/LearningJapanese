## Jukugo

が 存在(そんざい) する |  to exist ★★★★☆   
[存](1104-be-aware-of-存.html) (be aware of) + [在](1103-be-real-在.html) (be real) = 存在 (to exist)   
---|---  
に 依存(いぞん) する |  depend ★★★☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[依](768-depend-on-依.html) (depend on) + [存](1104-be-aware-of-存.html) (be aware of) = 依存 (depend)  to depend on- usually in a bad way, like "America's dependency on foreign oil"  
保存(ほぞん) |  preserve ★★☆☆☆   
[保](362-guaranteemaintain-保.html) (guarantee/maintain ) + [存](1104-be-aware-of-存.html) (be aware of) = 保存 (preserve)  preserve - usually used with traditional culture or morals more than environmental causes. You'll often see this: 保存員会 （ほぞんいんかい） preservation committee.  
既存(きぞん) のXXX |  the existing XXX ☆☆☆☆☆   
[既](1713-already-既.html) (already) + [存](1104-be-aware-of-存.html) (be aware of) = 既存 (the existing XXX)
