株式(かぶしき) |  stocks ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[株](478-share-stock-株.html) (share, stock) + [式](659-ritual-式.html) (ritual) = 株式 (stocks)  This is the word used in financial terms about stocks IN GENERAL - IPO or 'go public' or 'stock amoritization' and the like  
株式市場(かぶしき しじょう) |  stock market ★☆☆☆☆ [NEO](../tags/15.html)   
[株](478-share-stock-株.html)[式](659-ritual-式.html) (stocks) + [市](73-small-city-dagger-radical-市.html) (small city) + [場](1208-place-場.html) (place) = 株式市場 (stock market)  株式（stocks)＋地場(marketplace)＝the stock market！
