## Jukugo

候補(こうほ) |  candidate ★★☆☆☆   
[候](1049-climatecandidate-候.html) (climate/candidate) + [補](1563-supplement-補.html) (supplement) = 候補 (candidate)   
---|---  
気候(きこう) |  regional climate ☆☆☆☆☆   
[気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) + [候](1049-climatecandidate-候.html) (climate/candidate) = 気候 (regional climate)  climate - not like the weather we're having today, but the general climate of a region - temperate, tropical, etc.  
兆候(ちょうこう) |  a sign ☆☆☆☆☆   
[兆](1652-omen-兆.html) (omen) + [候](1049-climatecandidate-候.html) (climate/candidate) = 兆候 (a sign)  a sign that something will start soon: (Those clouds on the horizon are a sign a taiphoon is coming)
