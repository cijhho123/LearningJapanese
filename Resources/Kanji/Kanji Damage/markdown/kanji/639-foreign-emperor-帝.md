## Jukugo

皇帝(こうてい) |  other country's emperor ★★☆☆☆   
[皇](448-emperor-皇.html) (emperor) + [帝](639-foreign-emperor-帝.html) (foreign emperor) = 皇帝 (other country's emperor)  emperor - like China, not like Japan. (BOOBOO: Japan's emperor is 天皇:てんのう.)  
---|---  
帝国(ていこく) |  empire ★☆☆☆☆   
[帝](639-foreign-emperor-帝.html) (foreign emperor) + [国](438-country-国.html) (country) = 帝国 (empire)  empire - in the literal, non-judgemental sense of 'having an emperor' (England, China, ancient Inca). I don't think 帝国 carries the bad nuance of 'imperialism' (i.e. warlike or colonialist).
