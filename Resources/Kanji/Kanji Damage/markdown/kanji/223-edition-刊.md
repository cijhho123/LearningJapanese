#  刊 edition

[干](222-dry-out-干.html) (dry out) \+ [![](../assets/radsmall/swordright-551f3a37f33610f458edb9494c647afe8490103239e76d8d62616010ab7a9759.jpg)](164-sword-刀.html) (rightsword) 

★☆☆☆☆

9 strokes

[SUF](../tags/37.html)

This a suffix which you can append to ANY time-related word (morning, night,day,month, week, year, etc). 

It means "this is how often this periodical is published"
