## Jukugo

地域(ちいき) |  district, zone ★★★★☆   
[地](1600-area-地.html) (area) + [域](671-region-域.html) (region) = 地域 (district, zone)  a region or district, a zone. (emphasizes a social community moreso than a geographical area) (i.e. you wouldn't use it for the tropics or the Arctic, but you'd use it for Chinatown ,the American South or Kanto/Kansai)  
---|---  
領域(りょういき) |  domain ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[領](948-territory-領.html) (territory) + [域](671-region-域.html) (region) = 領域 (domain)  a territory or domain controlled by someone. Also used in neologisms like "domain name!"
