## Onyomi

ZOU |  An ELEPHANT (象= ZOU) produces other elephants.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  The motion of the factory machines informs you that they are in production.   
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

つく*る |  to mass-produce something in a factory. (although one-of-a-kind things like a giant rocket ship are also 造る)   
★★★☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

製造(せいぞう) |  manufacturing ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[製](806-mass-production-製.html) (mass production) + [造](808-produce-造.html) (produce) = 製造 (manufacturing)  manufacturing, production  
---|---  
改造(かいぞう) |  remodeling, renewal ★★☆☆☆   
[改](720-renew-improve-改.html) (renew / improve) + [造](808-produce-造.html) (produce) = 改造 (remodeling, renewal)  remodeling, renewal, or reorganization of a physical thing ; re-modeling a house, tricking out a car, re-organizing the leadership of an organization.  
創造的(そうぞうてき) な |  creative ★★☆☆☆   
[創](933-originate-creative-創.html) (originate / creative) + [造](808-produce-造.html) (produce) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 創造的 (creative)  creative (BOOBOO: don't confuse it with the OTHER そうぞう：想像, which means 'imagine.') (I know, it's pernicious!)
