## Jukugo

神様(かみさま) |  god ★★★★★ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[神](1498-god-kanji-神.html) (GOD kanji) + [様](879-important-person-様.html) (important person) = 神様 (god)  a more polite term for god or gods  
---|---  
神道(しんとう) |  shinto ★★★☆☆   
[神](1498-god-kanji-神.html) (GOD kanji) + [道](861-street-道.html) (street) = 神道 (shinto)  shinto, the state religion of Japan  
神社(じんじゃ) |  shinto shrine ★★☆☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[神](1498-god-kanji-神.html) (GOD kanji) + [社](1495-company-社.html) (company) = 神社 (shinto shrine)   
神秘的(しんぴてき) な |  occult, mystical, ★☆☆☆☆   
[神](1498-god-kanji-神.html) (GOD kanji) + [秘](370-secret-秘.html) (secret) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 神秘的 (occult, mystical,)  occult, mystical, supernatural-ish. Not like poltergeists or Alistair Crowley, but like a painting with a spooky vibe or a secluded nature spot where it's easy to imagine forest spirits jumpin' out on you.  
神経(しんけい) |  nerve cell ★☆☆☆☆   
[神](1498-god-kanji-神.html) (GOD kanji) + [経](1089-experience-経.html) (experience) = 神経 (nerve cell)   
神聖(しんせい) な |  holy ★☆☆☆☆   
[神](1498-god-kanji-神.html) (GOD kanji) + [聖](450-holy-聖.html) (holy) = 神聖 (holy)
