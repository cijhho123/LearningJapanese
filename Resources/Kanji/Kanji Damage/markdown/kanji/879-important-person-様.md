## Jukugo

皆様(みんなさま) |  everyone ★★★★☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)[F](../tags/26.html)   
[皆](32-everyone-皆.html) (everyone) + [様](879-important-person-様.html) (important person) = 皆様 (everyone)  a more formal way to say "everyone."  
---|---  
様子(ようす) |  the situation ★★★☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[様](879-important-person-様.html) (important person) + [子](5-child-子.html) (child) = 様子 (the situation)  the situation. Usually used in medicine or business: The patient's situation is grave. We'll wait and see if the market situation improves. 様子を見ましょう！ The other main way it's used is: 様子がおかしい！ Which means, 'Something funny is going on here!' or 'There's something funny about that person.' 'You mean the homeless with a stuffed duck on his head?' 'Yes.'  
様々(さまざま) な |  many, various ★★★☆☆ All different kinds of xxx. Very similar to 色々（いろいろ）, but 様々 means more like "all the kinds of" rather than "many kinds of."  
模様(もよう) |  pattern ★★★☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[模](425-model-模.html) (model) + [様](879-important-person-様.html) (important person) = 模様 (pattern)  a pattern which does not have pictures: fractals , mandala, plaid, zeebra skin. wood. do NOT use about representational patterns like flower-print or kids’ pajamas with horsies on them. 模様 also has a more formal meaning: ‘the details of an incident’ : You might hear a train/airport announcement which says "We will explain the details of the delay" ( 遅れるの模様。 )  
同様(どうよう) に |  in the same way ★★☆☆☆   
[同](974-same-同.html) (same) + [様](879-important-person-様.html) (important person) = 同様 (in the same way)  in the same way. For example, "In the same way caffeine perks up the mind, your dad's naked pictures perk up the body."
