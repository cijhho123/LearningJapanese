## Jukugo

仲間(なかま) |  friendship ★★★★☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[仲](492-friendship-仲.html) (friendship) + [間](162-a-period-of-time-間.html) (a period of time) = 仲間 (friendship)  friendship, OR membership in a group (i.e. working for the same company).  You'll also hear things like this: "The squid is the octopus' 仲間."  
---|---  
ｘｘｘ と 仲直り(なかなおり) する |  to make up ★★★☆☆   
[仲](492-friendship-仲.html) (friendship) + [直](1308-correct-a-problem-direct-contact-直.html) (correct a problem / direct contact) = 仲直り (to make up)  to make up with XXX , to restore a friendship  
仲良し(なかよし) |  a good friend ★★★☆☆   
[仲](492-friendship-仲.html) (friendship) + [良](775-good-良.html) (good) = 仲良し (a good friend)
