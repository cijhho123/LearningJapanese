## Jukugo

眺望(ちょうぼう) |  prospect / view ☆☆☆☆☆   
[眺](1655-look-at-for-a-long-time-while-lost-in-thought-眺.html) (look at for a long time while lost in thought) + [望](449-desire-望.html) (desire) = 眺望 (prospect / view)  1) (literally) a view from a high place (unlike related terms 景色 or 風景, 眺望 emphasizes the viewers' location, rather than the thing being looked at) ,  2) (figuratively) outlook or prospect for the future. (i.e. job prospects or, the outlook for 2009's stock market)
