## Jukugo

上司(じょうし) |  upper person ★★☆☆☆

---|---  
司会者(しかいしゃ) |  master of ceremonies ★☆☆☆☆   
[司](978-administer-司.html) (administer) + [会](1269-big-meeting-会.html) (big meeting) + [者](255-professional-者.html) (professional) = 司会者 (master of ceremonies)  a master of ceremonies of a TV show or event. It's not used for 'sucker MCs' which is a darn shame.
