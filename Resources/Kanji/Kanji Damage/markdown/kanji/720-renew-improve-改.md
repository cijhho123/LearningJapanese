## Jukugo

改造(かいぞう) |  remodeling, renewal ★★☆☆☆   
[改](720-renew-improve-改.html) (renew / improve) + [造](808-produce-造.html) (produce) = 改造 (remodeling, renewal)  remodeling, renewal, or reorganization of a physical thing ; re-modeling a house, tricking out a car, re-organizing the leadership of an organization.  
---|---  
改善(かいぜん) する |  improve ★★☆☆☆   
[改](720-renew-improve-改.html) (renew / improve) + [善](1632-morally-good-善.html) (morally good) = 改善 (improve)  improve a thing - your performance in Math class / the economy / the efficiency of the car  
改革(かいかく) する |  reform ★★☆☆☆   
[改](720-renew-improve-改.html) (renew / improve) + [革](514-leatherrevolution-革.html) (leather/revolution) = 改革 (reform)  reform (the welfare system, the campaign-contributions laws, etc.)
