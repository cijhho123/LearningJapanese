#  誘 invite / entice

[言](11-say-言.html) (say) \+ [秀](621-excel-秀.html) (excel) 

★★★☆☆

14 strokes

This has both a good meaning and a bad meaning! Invite someone to a nice dinner or lure them to their doom.

## Jukugo

誘惑(ゆうわく) する |  tempt ★★★☆☆   
[誘](622-invite-entice-誘.html) (invite / entice) + [惑](668-be-misguided-or-tempted-惑.html) (be misguided or tempted) = 誘惑 (tempt )   
---|---  
誘導(ゆうどう) する |  lead ★★☆☆☆   
[誘](622-invite-entice-誘.html) (invite / entice) + [導](998-lead-導.html) (lead) = 誘導 (lead)  to lead or conduct : A teacher leads the class on the field trip. The policewoman conducts traffic. The nuance is, 誘導 means literally going together with the people to the place.
