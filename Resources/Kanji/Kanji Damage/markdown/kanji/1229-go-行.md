## Jukugo

へ or に 旅行(りょこう) する |  vacation ★★★★★   
[旅](758-trip-旅.html) (trip) + [行](1229-go-行.html) (go) = 旅行 (vacation)  a vacation, or just travel in general.  
---|---  
流行(りゅうこう) |  trend ★★★☆☆   
[流](1248-flow-流.html) (flow) + [行](1229-go-行.html) (go) = 流行 (trend)   
行動(こうどう) |  behavior ★★☆☆☆   
[行](1229-go-行.html) (go) + [動](1078-movement-動.html) (movement) = 行動 (behavior)  behavior, conduct. Can be used in a good or bad way.  
XXX 行き(ゆき) |  a train / bus / plane bound for XXX ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)  
行為(こうい) |  deed ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[行](1229-go-行.html) (go) + [為](1740-deed-為.html) (deed) = 行為 (deed)  one's behavior. Basically the same as 行動（こうどう）but a little more formal. Usually used in newspapers or discussions of trials.
