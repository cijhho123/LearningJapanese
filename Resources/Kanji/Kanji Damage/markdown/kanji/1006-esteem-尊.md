## Jukugo

尊敬語(そんけいご) |  formal Japanese ★★★☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[尊](1006-esteem-尊.html)[敬](1419-respect-敬.html) (respect) + [語](1574-language-語.html) (language) = 尊敬語 (formal Japanese)  the formal Japanese you use when you are addressing your superiors.  
---|---  
尊敬(そんけい) する |  respect ★★★☆☆   
[尊](1006-esteem-尊.html) (esteem) + [敬](1419-respect-敬.html) (respect) = 尊敬 (respect)  to look up to or respect someone. (BOOBOO: it's not ok to tell someone directly, 'I 尊敬 you very much!' - you'd say it about a third party)  
男尊女卑(だんそんじょひ) |  sexism ★★☆☆☆   
[男](180-man-男.html) (man) + [尊](1006-esteem-尊.html) (esteem) + [女](6-woman-女.html) (woman) + [卑](1153-despicable-卑.html) (despicable) = 男尊女卑 (sexism)  sexism (literally, and I love this； 'men - esteem - women - despise')
