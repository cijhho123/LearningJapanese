MEAN-A-LIKES: 

龍 - ancient Chinese dragon 

竜 - regular dragon (TURTLE) 

The ancient Chinese one is mostly used in peoples' names. But you also have to learn it in order to learn the next kanji. 

## Onyomi

RYUU |  Same ON-yomi as regular dragon (竜)  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  This looks difficult but it's easy: the left side is 'stand' (立) and the right side really looks like a dragon standing up on its hind legs, facing right!!
