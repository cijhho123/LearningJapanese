#  京 capital

[![](../assets/radsmall/lid-27eb5444db66fa741b5e9033a1c88c54af8d81584c23b0539a1d6da210c43388.jpg)](80-beret.html) (beret) \+ [口](9-mouth-small-box-radical-口.html) (mouth/small box radical) \+ [小](192-small-size-小.html) (small size)

8 strokes

[BETA](../tags/2.html)[PN](../tags/19.html)

BOOBOO:This means 'capital city of Japan,' As opposed to '都' which means 'capital city' in the sense of 'Hollywood is the film capital' or 'Paris is the art capital' or 'San Francisco is the bear capital.' 

## Onyomi

KYOU |  TODAY (今日= KYOU) I am going to the capital.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  The fashion in the capital city is, to wear a beret, with a tiny soul patch below your mouth.

[東](357-east-東.html)[京](197-capital-京.html) (Tokyo) + [都](791-major-city-都.html) (major city) = 東京都 (capital city)  Tokyo -to. Tokyo is the capital of Japan, so it has "-to" on the end of it. Most cities have a 市（し） instead. And most prefectures have a 県 (けん） instead. If you think that's confusing, look at Washington DC - it's not a state, but it's not a city. It's a "district." Huh?
