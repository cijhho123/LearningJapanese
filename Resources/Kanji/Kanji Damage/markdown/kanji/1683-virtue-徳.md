## Jukugo

道徳(どうとく) |  moral principles ★☆☆☆☆   
[道](861-street-道.html) (street) + [徳](1683-virtue-徳.html) (virtue) = 道徳 (moral principles)  morals in general - universal principles like "don't cheat, don't kill" - as opposed to your own personal rules, like "I don't smoke, I don't watch Julia Roberts films." When newspapers talk about "moral education in the classroom," they use 道徳.
