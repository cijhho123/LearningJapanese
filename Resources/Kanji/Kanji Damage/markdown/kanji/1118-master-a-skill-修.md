## Onyomi

SHUU |  Gene Simmons is a master of walking in high-heel SHOES.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  next to 人 is a single vertical line, which let's think of as "a straight razor" the person is holding.  Thus:  That person is a master of shaving hair with a straight razor, so they made him the director of the barbershop!  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

修理(しゅうり) する |  fix ★★★☆☆   
[修](1118-master-a-skill-修.html) (master a skill) + [理](445-reason-理.html) (reason) = 修理 (fix)  to fix a thing- （BOOBOO: you don't 直す a car, or a computer, you 修理 it)  
---|---  
研修(けんしゅう) |  study, training ☆☆☆☆☆   
[研](1189-polish-sharpen-研.html) (polish, sharpen) + [修](1118-master-a-skill-修.html) (master a skill) = 研修 (study, training )
