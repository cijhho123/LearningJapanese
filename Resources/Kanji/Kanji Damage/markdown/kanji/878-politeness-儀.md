## Jukugo

礼儀(れいぎ) |  Formal manners ★★☆☆☆   
[礼](1504-polite-礼.html) (polite) + [儀](878-politeness-儀.html) (politeness) = 礼儀 (Formal manners)  Manners used in formal situations: correct bowing proceedures, sonkeigo, respecting heirarchy.  
---|---  
お 辞儀(じぎ) する |  bow ★☆☆☆☆ [CCW](../tags/4.html)[1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[辞](233-quit-辞.html) (quit) + [儀](878-politeness-儀.html) (politeness) = 辞儀 (bow)  a serious, formal bow - the real bow, not the head-nod.

You _discuss_ things when you SPEAK. 

_Righteousness_ is something you have inside you, but _politeness_ is how you treat other PERSONs. 

That's why righteous has no left radical ! It's an INTERNAL virtue.
