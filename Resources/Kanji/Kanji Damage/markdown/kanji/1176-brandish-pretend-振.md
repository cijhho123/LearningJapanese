## Jukugo

身振り(みぶり) |  gesture ★★☆☆☆   
[身](1595-ones-own-flesh-身.html) ((one's own) flesh) + [振](1176-brandish-pretend-振.html) (brandish / pretend) = 身振り (gesture)  a gesture, conscious or not - a motion of any part of the body - from flipping someone off to waving to shaking one's booty.  
---|---  
知らん振り(しらんぷり) |  feign innocence ★☆☆☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[知](1050-know-知.html) (know) + [振](1176-brandish-pretend-振.html) (brandish / pretend) = 知らん振り (feign innocence )  to pretend you don't know what I am talking about, to feign innocence
