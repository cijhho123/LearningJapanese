## Jukugo

処理(しょり) |  take care of a problem. ★★★☆☆   
[処](1287-dispose-of-or-manage-処.html) (dispose of or manage) + [理](445-reason-理.html) (reason) = 処理 (take care of a problem.)  Often translated as 'management', 処理 is more like,"to take care of a problem. To resolve a complaint." Unlike the related terms 経営(けいえい） or 営む （いとなむ）, 処理 is not a full-time job; more like something you do when an emergency happens.  
---|---  
処女(しょじょ) |  female virgin ★★☆☆☆ [FR](../tags/9.html)   
[処](1287-dispose-of-or-manage-処.html) (dispose of or manage) + [女](6-woman-女.html) (woman) = 処女 (female virgin)   
処分(しょぶん) する |  deal with/ dispose of ★★☆☆☆   
[処](1287-dispose-of-or-manage-処.html) (dispose of or manage) + [分](169-understand-divide-minute-分.html) (understand / divide / minute) = 処分 (deal with/ dispose of)  to dispose of (unwanted stuff),punish, or deal with or manage (a problem)  
処置(しょち) |  temporary fix ★★☆☆☆   
[処](1287-dispose-of-or-manage-処.html) (dispose of or manage) + [置](1309-put-down-on-table-置.html) (put down on table) = 処置 (temporary fix)  temporary, provisional management of a problem, until a lasting solution can be found : usually medical (i.e. stabalize the patient), but sometimes used about jury-rigging a machine. Take care of the radiation leak. Manage the PR crisis.
