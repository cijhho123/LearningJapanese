## Onyomi

TAKU |  I only TALK to selected people.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  When Jack Black selects you with his hand, you must TALK about rock.   
(Jack Black was the teacher in the movie School of Rock)   
(I've never actually seen it, but I assume he doesn't want everyone talking at once).

## Jukugo

選択(せんたく) する |  to choose ★★☆☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[選](1552-choose-選.html) (choose) + [択](294-select-択.html) (select) = 選択 (to choose)  choice- somewhat more formal than 選ぶ - you'd 選ぶ a bagel but 選択 a President.
