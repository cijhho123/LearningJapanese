## Mnemonic

|  It looks like a cross-section of an iron girder. Since this is the kanji for factory (工場), you can think of it like, a girder made at the iron factory. ![](../visualaids/1craft.jpg)  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

工事(こうじ) |  construction ★★★★★   
[工](645-craft-or-industry-工.html) (craft or industry) + [事](1660-action-incident-事.html) (action/ incident) = 工事 (construction)  construction work - buildings or roads, i.e. what guys do outside your window.  
---|---  
工場(こうじょう) |  factory ★★★☆☆   
[工](645-craft-or-industry-工.html) (craft or industry) + [場](1208-place-場.html) (place) = 工場 (factory)   
人工的(じんこうてき) な |  man-made ★★★☆☆ [NEO](../tags/15.html)   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [工](645-craft-or-industry-工.html) (craft or industry) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 人工的 (man-made)   
大工(だいく) |  carpenter ☆☆☆☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[大](397-big-大.html) (big) + [工](645-craft-or-industry-工.html) (craft or industry) = 大工 (carpenter)  carpenter. How awesome is that!???! Seriously, I wouldn't even believe my friend when he told me,I was like, 'Cmon, grow up.' He was like, "What is so funny about a daiku carpenter?" I made him look it up in the dictionary and prove it.
