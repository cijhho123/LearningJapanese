## Kunyomi

はら or おなか |  entrails!   
The kunyomi of his one is tricky because if it's written like this: 腹, it's pronounced はら、 but if it's written with the honorary 'お', like this: お腹、 then it's pronounced おなか。 Thus, this kanji is a COCK.

腹がヘタ(はらがへた) |  I'm hungry! ★★★☆☆ [KIDS](../tags/64.html) (usually said by macho guy/ country child) I'm hungry!  
切腹(せっぷく) する |  seppuku ★☆☆☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[切](165-cut-important-切.html) (cut / important) + [腹](1514-entrails-腹.html) (entrails) = 切腹 (seppuku)  when a samurai commits suicide by slicing his guts out. (切腹 is kind of formal - in casual conversation, you'd say 腹きり (はらきり） instead。)
