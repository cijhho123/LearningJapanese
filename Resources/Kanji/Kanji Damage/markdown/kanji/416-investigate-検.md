## Jukugo

検査(けんさ) する |  checkup ★★★☆☆   
[検](416-investigate-検.html) (investigate) + [査](1488-inspect-査.html) (inspect) = 検査 (checkup)  Medical checkup. Also, investigation, inspection, review: (fiancial audit, smog check, check the condition of goods that roll off the assemby line, etc). Has the nuance of a test where something passes or fails.  
---|---  
検索(けんさく) |  internet search ★★★☆☆   
[検](416-investigate-検.html) (investigate) + [索](215-look-up-索.html) (look up) = 検索 (internet search)   
検討(けんとう) する |  give consideration to ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[検](416-investigate-検.html) (investigate) + [討](989-attackdiscuss-討.html) (attack/discuss) = 検討 (give consideration to)  to give thorough consideration to the topic. To investigate something thoroughly. Often said by politicians who don't want to give a real answer.
