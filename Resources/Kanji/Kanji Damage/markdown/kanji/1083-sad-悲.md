## Jukugo

悲観的(ひかんてき) な |  pessimistic ★☆☆☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[悲](1083-sad-悲.html) (sad) + [観](1054-point-of-view-観.html) (point of view) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 悲観的 (pessimistic)  a somewhat literary word for pessimistic  
---|---  
悲劇(ひげき) |  tragedy ★☆☆☆☆   
[悲](1083-sad-悲.html) (sad) + [劇](1685-play-劇.html) (play) = 悲劇 (tragedy)  tragedy (opp: comedy!)  
悲惨(ひさん) な |  terrible ☆☆☆☆☆   
[悲](1083-sad-悲.html) (sad) + [惨](1119-wretched-惨.html) (wretched) = 悲惨 ( terrible)  terrible, harrowing, tragic. Usually used not about a single person’s misfortune but like the tragic cost of war, the terrible catastrophe that befell the small town in Godzilla’s path, etc.
