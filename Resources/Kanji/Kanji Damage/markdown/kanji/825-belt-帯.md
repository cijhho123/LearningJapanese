## Jukugo

携帯(けいたい) |  cell-phone. ★★★★★   
[携](624-carry-in-hand-携.html) (carry in hand) + [帯](825-belt-帯.html) (belt) = 携帯 (cell-phone.)   
---|---  
地帯(ちたい) |  zone ★★☆☆☆   
[地](1600-area-地.html) (area) + [帯](825-belt-帯.html) (belt) = 地帯 (zone)  a geographical district or zone - emphaizes a physical location: the tropics, the steppes. ちたい is much bigger and broader than 地域.
