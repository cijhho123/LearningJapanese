## Jukugo

---|---  
廃棄(はいき) する |  annul ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[廃](1187-wane-廃.html) (wane) + [棄](1664-discard-棄.html) (discard) = 廃棄 (annul)  dispose of - usually used of specialized garbage: medical waste or nuclear boogers or whatever.
