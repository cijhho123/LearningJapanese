## Jukugo

設定(せってい) する |  establish ★★★☆☆   
[設](1295-establish-設.html) (establish) + [定](282-plan-定.html) (plan) = 設定 (establish)  to establish a regulation or rule - also it is often seen on the 'setup' or 'preferences' menus of software when you install it.  
---|---  
施設(しせつ) |  institution / facilities ★★☆☆☆   
[施](1603-put-into-practice-charity-施.html) (put into practice / charity) + [設](1295-establish-設.html) (establish) = 施設 (institution / facilities)  an institution, some facilities. (a mental hospital / sports stadium / orphanage / water filtration plant)
