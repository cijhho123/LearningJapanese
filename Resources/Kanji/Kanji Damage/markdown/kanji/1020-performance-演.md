## Jukugo

演奏(えんそう) する |  perform ★★★☆☆   
[演](1020-performance-演.html) (performance) + [奏](961-musical-performance-奏.html) (musical performance) = 演奏 (perform)  perform music or a play  
---|---  
講演(こうえん) |  a non-political speech ★★☆☆☆   
[講](1567-lecture-講.html) (lecture) + [演](1020-performance-演.html) (performance) = 講演 (a non-political speech)  any public speech or talk given to a general audience. Authors doing a reading, dudes addressing a club, the graduation speaker at your graduation, are all doing 講演.  
演歌(えんか) |  enka ★☆☆☆☆ [CCW](../tags/4.html)   
[演](1020-performance-演.html) (performance) + [歌](700-sing-歌.html) (sing) = 演歌 (enka)  a genre of really un-hip music Japanese grandparents and hicks listen to - it's been called "the country western music of Japan", but it's much more. When you listen to Enka, or sing it in a karaoke bar, you can cry and express emotion.
