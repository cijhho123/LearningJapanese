## Jukugo

妄想(もうそう) |  A delusion ★☆☆☆☆   
[妄](108-without-reason-or-permission-妄.html) (without reason or permission) + [想](352-ideaimagination-想.html) (idea/imagination) = 妄想 (A delusion)  A delusion - something impossible: "I am currently dating Michael Jackson!" "Jewish aliens are following me in an invisible robot!"  
---|---
