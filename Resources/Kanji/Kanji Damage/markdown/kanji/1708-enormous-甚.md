## Jukugo

甚大(じんだい) な |  fucking huge disaster ★☆☆☆☆   
[甚](1708-enormous-甚.html) (enormous) + [大](397-big-大.html) (big) = 甚大 (fucking huge disaster)  ginormous, gigantic, fucking huge - almost always used about disasters. "The gigantic earthquake killed 1,000 dudes."
