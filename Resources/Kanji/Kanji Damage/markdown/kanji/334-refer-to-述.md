## Onyomi

JUTSU |  The label "infidel enemy of Allah" refers to athiests, christians, and JEWS, TOO!  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  述 is made of motion, plus the katakana 'HO' plus a dot. So you could say,   
While moving towards the hoe, he made a statement about the dot on her lip.   
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

の*べる |  to relate a story of... Describes the speaker's own actions: ('Please allow me to mention or state something') (usually used in a business or formal setting: opening remarks, proposals, extending a warm welcome, all that glad-handing bullshit)   
★★★☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

前述(ぜんじゅつ) |  the aforesaid, the aforementioned ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[前](865-before-前.html) (before) + [述](334-refer-to-述.html) (refer to) = 前述 (the aforesaid, the aforementioned)   
---|---  
記述(きじゅつ) する |  give an in-depth account of ★☆☆☆☆   
[記](723-diary-記.html) (diary) + [述](334-refer-to-述.html) (refer to) = 記述 (give an in-depth account of)  to describe, to give an in-depth account of. Generally means an entire chapter, if not a whole book.
