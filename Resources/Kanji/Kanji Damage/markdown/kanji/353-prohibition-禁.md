## Jukugo

禁煙(きんえん) |  no smoking ★★★★☆   
[禁](353-prohibition-禁.html) (prohibition) + [煙](583-smoky-煙.html) (smoky) = 禁煙 (no smoking)   
---|---  
禁止(きんし) |  forbidden ★★★★☆   
[禁](353-prohibition-禁.html) (prohibition) + [止](271-stop-止.html) (stop) = 禁止 (forbidden)  forbidden by law. Common examples: smoking outdoors, parking your bike here, opening this door, busking on the street, breathing on a grandpa.  
立ち入り禁止(たちいりきんし) |  no entry ★★★☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[立](148-stand-up-立.html) (stand up) + [入](60-putgo-in-入.html) (put/go in) + [禁](353-prohibition-禁.html) (prohibition) + [止](271-stop-止.html) (stop) = 立ち入り禁止 (no entry)  do not cross this line, no entry.  
厳禁(げんきん) |  strictly forbidden. ★★☆☆☆   
[厳](729-strict-厳.html) (strict) + [禁](353-prohibition-禁.html) (prohibition) = 厳禁 (strictly forbidden. )   
禁物(きんもつ) |  taboo or forbidden thing ★☆☆☆☆   
[禁](353-prohibition-禁.html) (prohibition) + [物](1201-animal-thing-物.html) (animal / thing) = 禁物 (taboo or forbidden thing )
