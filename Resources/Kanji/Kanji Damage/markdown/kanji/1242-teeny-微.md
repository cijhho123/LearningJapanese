## Jukugo

微妙(びみょう) な |  meh! ★★★☆☆   
[微](1242-teeny-微.html) (teeny) + [妙](195-odd-妙.html) (odd) = 微妙 (meh!)  a nice way of saying, 'meh.' "How did you like the play?"  "Bimyou." It's not always negative . . . it can also mean subtle and delicate -- which might explain the popularity of the more negative meaning - deniability!
