## Jukugo

時計(とけい) |  watch or clock ★★★★★ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[時](1002-time-時.html) (time) + [計](18-measure-計.html) (measure) = 時計 (watch or clock )   
---|---  
合計(ごうけい) |  total ★★★☆☆   
[合](936-to-suit-合.html) (to suit) + [計](18-measure-計.html) (measure) = 合計 (total)   
計画(けいかく) |  plan ★★★☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[計](18-measure-計.html) (measure) + [画](1039-a-drawing-画.html) (a drawing) = 計画 (plan)  Pretty close to 予定 （よてい） in meaning, but perhaps more elaborate - you'd use 計画 to say "I have a plan to get rich!" or to say "I planned out our whole vacation to 3 countries." . . but you wouldn't use 予定。  
余計(よけい) な |  uncalled-for ★★★☆☆   
[余](928-excess-余.html) (excess) + [計](18-measure-計.html) (measure) = 余計 (uncalled-for)  excessive, uncalled-for, and unnecessary (usually used in the context of 余計なお世話だよ！ （よけいなおせわだよ！）: 'Mind your own business!')  
計算(けいさん) する |  to calculate ★★☆☆☆   
[計](18-measure-計.html) (measure) + [算](1527-calculate-算.html) (calculate) = 計算 (to calculate)
