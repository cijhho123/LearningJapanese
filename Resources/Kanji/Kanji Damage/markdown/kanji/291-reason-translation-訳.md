## Jukugo

言い訳(いいわけ) |  excuse ★★★☆☆

通訳(つうやく) |  simultaneous translation ★☆☆☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[通](1342-pass-通.html) (pass) + [訳](291-reason-translation-訳.html) (reason / translation) = 通訳 (simultaneous translation)  language interpretation, like at the United Nations.- where they listen in one language and talk into a microphone in another language, translating in real-time
