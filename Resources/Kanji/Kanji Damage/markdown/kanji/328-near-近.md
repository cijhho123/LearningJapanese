## Jukugo

最近(さいきん) |  recently ★★★★☆   
[最](311-most-最.html) (most) + [近](328-near-近.html) (near) = 最近 (recently)   
---|---  
に 近寄る(ちかよる) |  get close to ★★☆☆☆   
[近](328-near-近.html) (near) + [寄](400-get-close-寄.html) (get close) = 近寄る (get close to)  to get close to, to approach.(usually used in the negative, on warning signs: danger! keep out! danger! high voltage! 近寄るな!)  
近所(きんじょ) |  around here ★★☆☆☆   
[近](328-near-近.html) (near) + [所](506-area-attribute-所.html) (area / attribute) = 近所 (around here)  in this neighborhood, around here- (BOOBOO: 近所 is strictly relative to where you are standing at the time. You can't say 'Shibuya is a neighborhood in tokyo,' - in that case, you have to use MACHI(町)。)  
に or が 近付く(ちかづく) |  get close to destination ★★☆☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[近](328-near-近.html) (near) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 近付く (get close to destination)  to draw close to a physical destination or a goal.
