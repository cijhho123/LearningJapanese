## Jukugo

著者(ちょしゃ) |  "this author" ★☆☆☆☆   
[著](256-author-著.html) (author) + [者](255-professional-者.html) (professional) = 著者 ("this author")  the way pretentious authors refer to themselves in the book: "As this author has said before, blah blah blah."
