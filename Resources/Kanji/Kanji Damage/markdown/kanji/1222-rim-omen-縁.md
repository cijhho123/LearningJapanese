## Onyomi

EN |  The rim has no END, you can keep going around it forever.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  Tie the string around the rim of the pig's snout, to keep it from biting.  
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

ふち |  rim or edge of table, cup, picture frame (emphasis is that it's not a point on the edge, it's the ENTIRE edge) [KANA](../tags/12.html)

## Jukugo

縁起(えんぎ) のいい or の悪い |  omen ★☆☆☆☆   
[縁](1222-rim-omen-縁.html) (rim / omen) + [起](721-wake-up-occur-起.html) (wake up / occur) = 縁起 (omen)  an omen (can be good or bad)  
---|---  
縁がある(えんがある) |  to have a chance, to be linked by fate ★☆☆☆☆
