## Jukugo

xxx 方(かた) |  way to do xxx ★★★★★ [SUF](../tags/37.html) SUFFIX meaning, 'way to do.' for instance, ギターを弾く； means 'to play the guitar.' so,ギターの弾き方； means 'the way to play the guitar.'  
---|---  
方法(ほうほう) |  method ★★★★☆   
[方](84-direction-method-person-方.html) (direction / method / person) + [法](1258-law-法.html) (law) = 方法 (method )   
仕方(しかた) |  way to do something ★★★★☆   
[仕](246-work-仕.html) (work) + [方](84-direction-method-person-方.html) (direction / method / person) = 仕方 (way to do something)  Most often used in the negative: 'Shikata ga nai.' as in, 'fuck it.'  
方向(ほうこう) |  direction ★★★★☆   
[方](84-direction-method-person-方.html) (direction / method / person) + [向](1640-turn-to-face-向.html) (turn to face) = 方向 (direction)   
一方(いっぽう) に |  one-sided ★★☆☆☆   
[一](1-one-line-radical-一.html) (one) + [方](84-direction-method-person-方.html) (direction / method / person) = 一方 (one-sided)  one-sided, one-way. Often used in the phrase : xxx 一方だ！ (meaning, more and more xxx - i.e. "more and more hot", "more and more popular," etc.)
