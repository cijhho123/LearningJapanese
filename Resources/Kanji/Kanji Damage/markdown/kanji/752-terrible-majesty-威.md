This is one of the most "no-English-equivalent-y" words in the whole Japanese language. Why is majestic stuff scary? Who is scared of Prince? He's like five feet tall. I guess you have to think like a medieval peasant to really get the meaning.

## Jukugo

威厳(いげん) のある |  majestic/scary ★★☆☆☆   
[威](752-terrible-majesty-威.html) (terrible majesty) + [厳](729-strict-厳.html) (strict) = 威厳 (majestic/scary)  majestic , slow and dignified but scary - like a bunch of gangsters slowly walking down the street while the Peter Gunn theme plays. Or a parade float of your mom's neck-beard.  
---|---  
権威(けんい) |  authority ★★☆☆☆   
[権](1056-rights-権.html) (rights) + [威](752-terrible-majesty-威.html) (terrible majesty) = 権威 (authority)  an authority one has based on power alone (parents, cops, etc.) rather than on rights (authority based on rights is 権利（けんり））  
威張ってる(いばってる) |  arrogant ★☆☆☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[威](752-terrible-majesty-威.html) (terrible majesty) + [張](906-stretch-張.html) (stretch) = 威張ってる (arrogant)
