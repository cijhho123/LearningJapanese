## Jukugo

と or を 告白(こくはく) する |  confess ★★★☆☆   
[告](807-inform-告.html) (inform) + [白](31-white-白.html) (white) = 告白 (confess)  to confess- both to a crime and to having a huge crush on me.  
---|---  
広告(こうこく) |  advertisement ★★★☆☆   
[広](1139-wide-広.html) (wide) + [告](807-inform-告.html) (inform) = 広告 (advertisement)  an advertisement - although nowadays people use the Japlish word シーエム (CM, short for 'commercial.')  
報告(ほうこく) |  report ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[報](1482-data-報.html) (data) + [告](807-inform-告.html) (inform) = 報告 (report)  A report - or maybe just 'data' is better?   
Compared to the related term 情報 (news report), 報告 has more of a nuance of a scientific, governmental, or corporate feeling.   
Think about it like this: 報告 are commissioned by private agencies, and then journalists do a 情報 about the 報告.  
忠告(ちゅうこく) する |  admonish ★☆☆☆☆   
[忠](490-loyalty-忠.html) (loyalty) + [告](807-inform-告.html) (inform) = 忠告 (admonish)  admonishing negative advice: "Don't open that door!" "Don't swim naked!"
