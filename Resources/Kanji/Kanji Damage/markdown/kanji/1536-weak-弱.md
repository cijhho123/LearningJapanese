## Onyomi

JAKU |  Michael JAKUson is weak.

## Mnemonic

|  Basically it is the same as feathers (羽 ) but mixed up with the kanji for bow (弓)   
I guess you could say that birds with weak feathers can't fly fast, so they are the ones shot down by the bow.

## Jukugo

弱虫(よわむし) |  weakling ★★☆☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[弱](1536-weak-弱.html) (weak) + [虫](493-insizzect-虫.html) (insizzect) = 弱虫 (weakling)  weakling, gayrod  
---|---  
軟弱(なんじゃく) な |  feeble ★☆☆☆☆   
[軟](1066-soft-軟.html) (soft) + [弱](1536-weak-弱.html) (weak) = 軟弱 (feeble)  this word combines both synonyms for 'soft.' So you know it means really feeble, weak, nerdy.  
弱点(じゃくてん) |  achilles heel ★☆☆☆☆   
[弱](1536-weak-弱.html) (weak) + [点](126-point-点.html) (point) = 弱点 (achilles heel)  Achilles heel, soft underbelly. A built-in weakness that - while not correctable - is not a deal-breaker either.
