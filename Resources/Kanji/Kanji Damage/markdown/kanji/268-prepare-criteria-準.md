## Jukugo

準備(じゅんび) する |  get ready ★★★★★   
[準](268-prepare-criteria-準.html) (prepare / criteria) + [備](734-furnish-備.html) (furnish) = 準備 (get ready)  this is the main word you'll use with this kanji: prepare (packing for a vacation, getting the restaurant ready to open for the day, and putting on makeup are all junbi).  
---|---  
基準(きじゅん) |  a standard ★★☆☆☆   
[基](1705-basis-基.html) (basis) + [準](268-prepare-criteria-準.html) (prepare / criteria) = 基準 (a standard )  criterion - the basis for judging a sex partner, potential job applicant, which songs to buy, etc. Also - unlike the OTHER きじゅん （規準）、THIS きじゅん ALSO has a second meaning: 'standard', as in, standard wages, standard price, the gold standard, standard medical ethics. Metric is our standard of measurement in Japan, whereas in USA they use their own standard.  
規準(きじゅん) |  a criterion ☆☆☆☆☆   
[規](592-criteria-規.html) (criteria) + [準](268-prepare-criteria-準.html) (prepare / criteria) = 規準 (a criterion)
