## Kunyomi

めん |  2 meanings   
1) ENTIRE surface of a 3-dimensional object, as opposed to 表, which is the front surface of a 2-D object (deeeeyammn!!),   
2) the head or face - usually used in the context of expressions like 'meet face to face' or 'dare you to say it to my face!'. In other words, you wouldn't tell a drunk model, 'Hey there, nice 面！’

## Jukugo

面白*い(おもしろい) |  interesting ★★★★★ [SARC](../tags/20.html)[FP](../tags/8.html)   
[面](813-front-surface-face-面.html) (front surface / face) + [白](31-white-白.html) (white) = 面白*い (interesting)  interesting, (in a good way) but also can mean funny (as in, laughing at you!) this deliberate ambiguity being a big thing with Japanese Sarcasm. Although in this case, fully half the time people are being serious.  
---|---  
面倒臭い(めんどうくさい) |  pain in the ass ★★★★★   
[面](813-front-surface-face-面.html)[倒](1256-knock-down-倒.html) (trouble, petty bullshit) + [臭](401-stinky-odor-臭.html) (stinky and dumb xxx!) = 面倒臭い (pain in the ass)  annoying, a pain in the ass.  
画面(がめん) |  screen ★★★☆☆   
[画](1039-a-drawing-画.html) (a drawing) + [面](813-front-surface-face-面.html) (front surface / face) = 画面 (screen)  screen - both movie screen and computer/tv screen  
地面(じめん) |  ground ★★☆☆☆   
[地](1600-area-地.html) (area) + [面](813-front-surface-face-面.html) (front surface / face) = 地面 (ground)  the ground/floor (applies both inside and outside)  
面目を保つ(めんぼくをたもつ) |  save face ★☆☆☆☆ [CCW](../tags/4.html)   
[面](813-front-surface-face-面.html) (front surface / face) + [目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) + [保](362-guaranteemaintain-保.html) (guarantee/maintain ) = 面目を保つ (save face)  to save face (opposite: 面目を失う （めんぼくをうしなう））  
表面(ひょうめん) |  exterior ☆☆☆☆☆   
[表](767-express-表.html) (express) + [面](813-front-surface-face-面.html) (front surface / face) = 表面 (exterior)  the exterior, the surface - unlike 面 (men), 表面 is more technical - usually used by architects or craftspeople. Or, for instance, to describe a "skin" you'd put on a polygon 3d character.
