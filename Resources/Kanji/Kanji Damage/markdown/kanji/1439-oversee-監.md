## Jukugo

監督(かんとく) |  director ★★☆☆☆   
[監](1439-oversee-監.html) (oversee) + [督](203-supervisor-督.html) (supervisor) = 監督 (director)  movie director/ construction foreman / coach.  
---|---  
監視(かんし) する |  to supervise ☆☆☆☆☆   
[監](1439-oversee-監.html) (oversee) + [視](1493-peer-at-視.html) (peer at) = 監視 (to supervise)  to supervise, or watch over a project. Also means, to guard bad people - guards at a prison, a teacher who watches students at test time to keep them from cheating. (BOOBOO: don't mix this with 警備 (けいび), which is someone who is guarding or protecting good people or things).
