## Jukugo

機械(きかい) |  machine ★★★★★   
[機](675-machine-機.html) (machine) + [械](1531-contraption-械.html) (contraption) = 機械 (machine)  A machine, particularly something with a motor in it which moves when activated. A computer is not a 機械 , but a robot arm is.  
---|---  
飛行機(ひこうき) |  airplane ★★★★☆   
[飛](1533-fly-飛.html) (fly) + [行](1229-go-行.html) (go) + [機](675-machine-機.html) (machine) = 飛行機 (airplane)   
機嫌(きげん) |  mood ★★☆☆☆   
[機](675-machine-機.html) (machine) + [嫌](1672-eww-嫌.html) (eww!!) = 機嫌 (mood)  mood (bad mood: 不機嫌!)  
危機(きき) |  social crisis ★★☆☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[危](1475-dangerous-危.html) (dangerous) + [機](675-machine-機.html) (machine) = 危機 (social crisis)  a social crisis (oil crisis, global warming, etc.)  
機会(きかい) |  a chance ★☆☆☆☆   
[機](675-machine-機.html) (machine) + [会](1269-big-meeting-会.html) (big meeting) = 機会 (a chance)  a chance, as in, "I hope I will have a chance to go to Australia someday." or "This could be my only chance to see yo mama's huge collection of Rosie O'donnel pap-smears."
