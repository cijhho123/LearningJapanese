## Onyomi

SHUU |  Your SHOES are so wack it fills me with sorrow.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  When the season turns to fall, my heart is always full of sorrow.  
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

うれ*い |  melancholy- sorrowful - but more subtle than the OTHER うれい （憂い）。 Regular urei is like a homeless guy that is obviously suffering and you can see why. 愁い is like a elegant woman in a kimono going to a temple and carrying with her a subtle poetic aura of sadness. Older Japanese guys go for this.   
★☆☆☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

哀愁(あいしゅう) |  melancholy ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[哀](773-pitiful-哀.html) (pitiful) + [愁](379-sorrow-愁.html) (sorrow) = 哀愁 (melancholy)  an archaic, literary word meaning, "melancholy, pathos, grief." (used about a novel, song, movie, or tone of voice, but not about a person).  
---|---  
郷愁(きょうしゅう) |  nostalgia ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[郷](784-ones-hometown-郷.html) (one's hometown) + [愁](379-sorrow-愁.html) (sorrow) = 郷愁 (nostalgia)  literary word for nostalgia (BOOBOO: most people just say 懐かしい （なつかしい！）)
