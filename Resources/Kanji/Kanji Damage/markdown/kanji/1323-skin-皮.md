## Jukugo

毛皮(けがわ) |  animal fur ★★★★☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[毛](512-fur-毛.html) (fur) + [皮](1323-skin-皮.html) (skin) = 毛皮 (animal fur)  animal fur - on a living animal OR on a fur coat  
---|---  
皮肉(ひにく) |  sarcasm/irony ★★★☆☆ [SARC](../tags/20.html)   
[皮](1323-skin-皮.html) (skin) + [肉](71-meat-肉.html) (meat) = 皮肉 (sarcasm/irony)  sarcasm OR irony. Japanese DO have a sense of sarcasm,(for example, calling the traditional chicken-and-scrambled-eggs dish "parents and child") but it is so different than the Western say-the-opposite-of-what-you-mean style that Japanese don't have a word for western sarc. So they seem to have taken their word for Irony and pressed it into service for sarcasm as well.
