## Mnemonic

|  'thousand' (千) and 'dry out'(干) look the same . ..except the top of thousand is tilted, but the top of _dry out_ is FLAT. So you can recite this fresh rhyme to jog your memory:  'I wear a THOUSAND tilty HATS,  but I got to _dry them out_ on a surface that is FLAT. '  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

千円札(せんえんさつ) |  1,000 yen bill ★★★☆☆ [COUNTER](../tags/38.html)   
[千](229-thousand-千.html) (thousand) + [円](72-yencircle-円.html) (yen/circle) + [札](1506-card-label-bill-札.html) (card / label / bill) = 千円札 (1,000 yen bill)  any money in paper form is a 'satsu' - a ten dollar bill is a $10 satsu, etc.
