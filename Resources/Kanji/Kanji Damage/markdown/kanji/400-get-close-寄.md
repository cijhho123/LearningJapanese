## Jukugo

に 近寄る(ちかよる) |  get close to ★★☆☆☆   
[近](328-near-近.html) (near) + [寄](400-get-close-寄.html) (get close) = 近寄る (get close to)  to get close to, to approach.(usually used in the negative, on warning signs: danger! keep out! danger! high voltage! 近寄るな!)  
---|---  
年寄り(としより) |  old person ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[年](1572-year-年.html) (year) + [寄](400-get-close-寄.html) (get close) = 年寄り (old person)  old person ('70s and up). This isn't rude per se, but if you want to dismiss or belittle what an old fart just said, this is the word you use.
