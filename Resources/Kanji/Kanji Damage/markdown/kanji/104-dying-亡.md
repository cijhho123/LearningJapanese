## Jukugo

滅亡(めつぼう) |  downfall ★★☆☆☆   
[滅](751-annihilate-滅.html) (annihilate) + [亡](104-dying-亡.html) (dying) = 滅亡 (downfall)  the downfall of a civilization or the dying-out of a certain ethnic group. But it doesn't have 'downfall's moral judgement.  
---|---  
未亡人(みぼうじん) |  widow ☆☆☆☆☆ [FR](../tags/9.html)   
[未](381-not-yet-未.html) (not yet) + [亡](104-dying-亡.html) (dying) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 未亡人 (widow)  widow.I'm not including this because it's useful, but because it's one of the most spectacularly non-PC words: the literal translation is：'not yet dead person'!
