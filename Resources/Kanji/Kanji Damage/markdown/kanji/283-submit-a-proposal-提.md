## Jukugo

提案(ていあん) する |  proposal ★★★☆☆   
[提](283-submit-a-proposal-提.html) (submit a proposal) + [案](359-guideproposal-案.html) (guide/proposal) = 提案 (proposal)  a proposal - not just in business but also in everyday life : "Let's go to this movie!"  
---|---  
提出(ていしゅつ) する |  hand over / present ★☆☆☆☆   
[提](283-submit-a-proposal-提.html) (submit a proposal) + [出](1024-pull-out-hand-over-出.html) (pull out / hand over) = 提出 (hand over / present)  to physically present or submit (a report, a document)
