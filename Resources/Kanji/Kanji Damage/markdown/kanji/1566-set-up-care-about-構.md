## Jukugo

結構(けっこう) です。 |  ok / no thanks / very ★★★☆☆ [JERK](../tags/11.html)[1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[結](250-bind-結.html) (bind) + [構](1566-set-up-care-about-構.html) (set up / care about) = 結構 (ok / no thanks / very)  this has 3 meanings, each used just as often as the others .  1)literally, "OK." 2) "No thank you" (just like American women, Japanese people use 'That's ok' to mean 'No.'!)  3) "Very much" : You often hear people saying, "Kekkou atsui ne!" ("It's very hot today, eh?")

[構](1566-set-up-care-about-構.html) (set up / care about) + [造](808-produce-造.html) (produce) = 構造 ( structure)  structure of a physical thing: cell structure, house structure,structure of a society.
