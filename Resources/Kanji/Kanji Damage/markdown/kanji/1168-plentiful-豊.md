## Kunyomi

ゆた*か ( な )  |  abundant - like a rainforest abundant in life or a country's abundant natural resources. You can also use it for a person - he has a lot of education or a wealth of wisdom. (but not used for actual dollars - in that case you'd use 裕福 (yuufuku)。）

## Jukugo

豊満(ほうまん) な |  voluptuous ★☆☆☆☆ [ABU](../tags/1.html)   
[豊](1168-plentiful-豊.html) (plentiful) + [満](1041-full-満.html) (full) = 豊満 (voluptuous)  not what it sounds like! Houman means zatfig, voluptuous. This has two meanings： a) a borderline sexual-harassment word used generally about the big titty women. b) a a relatively polite way to describe a chubby woman. Anyway, ABU!
