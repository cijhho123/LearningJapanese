[BETA](../tags/2.html)

This kanji is mostly used as a radical, and more bad news: for some reason, all the kanji using the vassal radical tend to be super complex and similar-looking. Doh. 

## Onyomi

SHIN, JIN |  I command the vassal to bring us a SHINY new bottle of GIN!
