## Jukugo

仮面(かめん) |  mask ★★★☆☆   
[仮](743-provisional-仮.html) (provisional) + [面](813-front-surface-face-面.html) (front surface / face) = 仮面 (mask)  mask. (literally, "temporary face")  
---|---  
仮名(カナ) |  kana ★☆☆☆☆   
[仮](743-provisional-仮.html) (provisional) + [名](119-name-famous-名.html) (name, famous) = 仮名 (kana)  all the Japanese letterforms which are not Kanji - hiragana, katakana, okurigana, furigana, oshirigana, nurenuregana, bakana, and whatever they think up next!
