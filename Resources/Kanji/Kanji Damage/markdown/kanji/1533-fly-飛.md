## Jukugo

飛行機(ひこうき) |  airplane ★★★★☆   
[飛](1533-fly-飛.html) (fly) + [行](1229-go-行.html) (go) + [機](675-machine-機.html) (machine) = 飛行機 (airplane)   
---|---  
が or へ 飛び出す(とびだす) |  jump up from out of nowhere ★★☆☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[飛](1533-fly-飛.html) (fly) + [出](1024-pull-out-hand-over-出.html) (pull out / hand over) = 飛び出す (jump up from out of nowhere)  to jump out in front of you suddenly. Normally you'll see this on road signs, "Drive slow! Children might dash out!" Unlike the related words 跳躍 and 踊る, 飛び出す emphasizes jumping FROM a place. Jumping FROM the side of the road, or FROM the shadows.  
飛躍(ひやく) |  a leap ★☆☆☆☆   
[飛](1533-fly-飛.html) (fly) + [躍](642-jump-躍.html) (jump) = 飛躍 (a leap)  a leap. Usually figurative. Make a leap forward in technology. Not used as a verb.
