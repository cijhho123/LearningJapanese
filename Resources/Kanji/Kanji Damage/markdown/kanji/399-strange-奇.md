## Jukugo

奇妙(きみょう) な |  weird ★★☆☆☆   
[奇](399-strange-奇.html) (strange) + [妙](195-odd-妙.html) (odd) = 奇妙 (weird)  weird - but more broad than the related term 妙. 奇妙 means unusual _and absurd_ \- a street-person wearing a duck as a hat is 奇妙.  
---|---  
好奇心(こうきしん) |  curiosity ★☆☆☆☆   
[好](7-like-好.html) (like) + [奇](399-strange-奇.html) (strange) + [心](98-heart-心.html) (heart) = 好奇心 (curiosity)  curiosity  BOOBOO: if you want to say you're curious about a specific thing, you'd use 興味 （きょうみ） instead. 好奇心 usually means "curiosity-in-general": you have an inquiring personality, or a gaze of curiosity on your face. You'd never say, "Oh, you play in a band? 好奇心ある！”
