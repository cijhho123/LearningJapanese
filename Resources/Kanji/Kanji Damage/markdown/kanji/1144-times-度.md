## Jukugo

今度(こんど) |  next time ★★★★★   
[今](943-now-今.html) (now) + [度](1144-times-度.html) (times) = 今度 (next time )   
---|---  
態度(たいど) |  attitude/ stance ★★★★☆   
[態](145-looks-like-態.html) (looks like) + [度](1144-times-度.html) (times) = 態度 (attitude/ stance)  an attitude/manner (defiant, peaceful, etc.) or stance (anti-trade, pro-shinto, anti-corporate). When it comes to the first meaning, 態度 is something you can tell just by looking at the person - they might not be doing it intentionally.  
丁度(ちょうど) |  just right ★★★☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[丁](51-nail-丁.html) (nail) + [度](1144-times-度.html) (times) = 丁度 (just right)  exactly, precisely. Just right.  
程度(ていど) |  a degree or extent ★★★☆☆   
[程](451-extent-程.html) (extent) + [度](1144-times-度.html) (times) = 程度 (a degree or extent)
