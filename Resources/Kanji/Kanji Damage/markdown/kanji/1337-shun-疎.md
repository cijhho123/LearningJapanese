#  疎 shun

[マ](1327-mama-マ.html) (Mama) \+ [止](271-stop-止.html) (stop) \+ [束](1336-bundle-of-sticks-束.html) (bundle of sticks) 

★☆☆☆☆

12 strokes

## Onyomi

SO |  Everyone shuns Some One.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  I wish your mama would stop shunning bundles of sticks. She's a homophobe.   
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

( に ) うと*い |  emotionally distant from, or: to not know a lot about   
★☆☆☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

疎外感(そがいかん) |  alienation (usually it means alienation from society or the world at large - more of a withdrawl or angsty thing) ☆☆☆☆☆   
[疎](1337-shun-疎.html) (shun) + [外](122-outside-外.html) (outside) + [感](755-feeling-感.html) (feeling) = 疎外感 (alienation (usually it means alienation from society or the world at large - more of a withdrawl or angsty thing))   
---|---  
疎遠(そえん) になる |  become estranged from ☆☆☆☆☆   
[疎](1337-shun-疎.html) (shun) + [遠](762-far-遠.html) (far) = 疎遠 (become estranged from)  to become estranged from, alienated from (usually used about a specific person or people: ex-husband, family, group of friends, etc.)
