## Jukugo

公園(こうえん) |  park ★★★★★   
[公](137-public-公.html) (public) + [園](763-public-park-園.html) (public park) = 公園 (park)  a public park  
---|---  
公開(こうかい) する |  open to the public ★★★☆☆   
[公](137-public-公.html) (public) + [開](1185-open-開.html) (open) = 公開 (open to the public)   
公共(こうきょう) |  public ★★☆☆☆   
[公](137-public-公.html) (public) + [共](1548-with-共.html) (with) = 公共 (public)  public, as in "public toilet" or "public telephone"
