## Jukugo

貯蓄(ちょちく) |  savings ★☆☆☆☆   
[貯](538-save-up-貯.html) (save up) + [蓄](208-store-or-put-aside-蓄.html) (store or put aside) = 貯蓄 (savings)  All savings, especially investments such as stocks, gold and gems.Or anything else you're hoarding.  
---|---  
蓄積(ちくせき) する |  accumulate. ☆☆☆☆☆   
[蓄](208-store-or-put-aside-蓄.html) (store or put aside) + [積](553-pile-up-積.html) (pile up) = 蓄積 (accumulate.)  accumulate. Used about bad things mostly - bills or stress piles up. Similar to 止まって来た (とまってきた） or 重なる (かさなる）。 But 蓄積 can also can be _good_ , like the compound interest or accumulated knowledge.
