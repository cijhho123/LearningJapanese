## Jukugo

某(ぼう) XXX |  a certain ★☆☆☆☆ PREFIX: you can put it before any noun, with no particles in between, for example: 某国： ぼうごく:a certain country, 某地：ぼうち： an undisclosed location, 某猿： ぼうさる :a certain monkey. etc.
