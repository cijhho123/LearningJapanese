## Kunyomi

は*る |  to physically make something longer. stretch a rubber-band、 stretch a canvas on a paint frame, pitch a tent. Also to extend one's influence.

---|---  
  
## Jukugo

頑張る(がんばる) |  good luck! ★★★★★ [CCW](../tags/4.html)[1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[頑](844-stubborn-頑.html) (stubborn) + [張](906-stretch-張.html) (stretch) = 頑張る (good luck!)  No exact English equivalent. がんばる combines two English expressions: 1) do your best! 2) good luck! You tell your friends 頑張れ！ before a big test or job interview.  
---|---  
緊張(きんちょう) する |  stress ★★★★☆   
[緊](687-tense-緊.html) (tense) + [張](906-stretch-張.html) (stretch) = 緊張 (stress)  stress, stretched to the max ーcan be used both literally and figuratively.
