## Jukugo

描写(びょうしゃ) する |  depiction ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[描](296-depict-描.html) (depict) + [写](652-copy-写.html) (copy) = 描写 (depiction )  a depiction or representation of something (literally, as in 'this film contains graphic depictions of violence' or figuratively, as in 'Describe his personality to me.')
