## Jukugo

調子(ちょうし) |  condition ★★★★☆   
[調](1636-check-out-調.html) (check out) + [子](5-child-子.html) (child) = 調子 (condition)  the condition something's in. Can be used with a living thing or object. Always used only with いい or 悪い. Usually emphaizes the LONG TERM condition. Although hungover people have been heard to remark, 調子が悪い！  
---|---  
調査(ちょうさ) する |  investigate ★★★☆☆   
[調](1636-check-out-調.html) (check out) + [査](1488-inspect-査.html) (inspect) = 調査 (investigate)  an investigation. Emphasizes that there are no correct answers, and implies that the investigators are talking to someone, asking questions directly. (usually used about polls, surveys, and detectives questioning suspects and/or witnesses)  
強調(きょうちょう) する |  emphasize ★★★☆☆   
[強](897-burly-強.html) (burly) + [調](1636-check-out-調.html) (check out) = 強調 (emphasize)
