## Jukugo

家族(かぞく) |  family ★★★★★   
[家](1223-home-家.html) (home) + [族](1048-family-族.html) (family) = 家族 (family)  your immediate family - parents, grandparents, siblings.  
---|---  
政治家(せいじか) |  politician ★★★☆☆   
[政](708-politics-政.html)[治](140-cure-治.html) (politics) + [家](1223-home-家.html) (home) = 政治家 (politician)   
作家：(さっか) |  author ★★★☆☆   
[作](325-make-作.html) (make) + [家](1223-home-家.html) (home) = 作家： (author)   
家庭(かてい) |  the family you are living with. ★★☆☆☆   
[家](1223-home-家.html) (home) + [庭](1155-garden-庭.html) (garden) = 家庭 (the family you are living with.)   
専門家(せんもんか) |  specialist ★☆☆☆☆ [SARC](../tags/20.html)[SUF](../tags/37.html)   
[専](1016-specialty-専.html)[門](160-gate-門.html) (a specialty) + [家](1223-home-家.html) (home) = 専門家 (specialist)  a specialist (often shortened to just ｘｘｘ専) （this is often used sarcastically - for instance a デブ専 is a "fat person specialist", meaning a "chubby chaser.")
