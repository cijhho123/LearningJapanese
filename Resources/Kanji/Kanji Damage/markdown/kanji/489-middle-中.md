## Jukugo

世の中(よのなか) |  the world ★★★★☆   
[世](1029-society-世.html) (society) + [中](489-middle-中.html) (middle) = 世の中 (the world)  the social, everyday world - more poetic than 世界。 Used in dramatic declarations like, "I'm sick of this fuckin' world!" "I don't care about this life!" "Poor Kurt Cobain, he was just too sensitive for this cruel world!" Also, usually implies JUST Japanese society.  
---|---  
真ん中(まんなか) |  the very center ★★★☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[真](1304-really-真.html) (really) + [中](489-middle-中.html) (middle) = 真ん中 (the very center)   
中学校(ちゅうがっこう) |  middle school ★★★☆☆   
[中](489-middle-中.html) (middle) + [学](1624-knowledge-学.html)[校](1011-school-校.html) (school) = 中学校 (middle school)   
中央(ちゅうおう) |  central ★★★☆☆   
[中](489-middle-中.html) (middle) + [央](405-central-央.html) (central) = 中央 (central)  central. Tokyo has a famous train line: 中央線、(the central line).  
中級(ちゅうきゅう) の |  intermediate-level ★★★☆☆   
[中](489-middle-中.html) (middle) + [級](627-level-級.html) (level) = 中級 (intermediate-level)   
中止(ちゅうし) された |  stop/cancel ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[中](489-middle-中.html) (middle) + [止](271-stop-止.html) (stop) = 中止 (stop/cancel)  (temporarily) stopped or (permanently) canceled. This is a word you'd hear in an announcement. You wouldn't say to your friend, "I'm going to 中止 watching TV for a week."  
ｘｘｘ 中(ちゅう) |  in the middle of xxx ★★☆☆☆ [SUF](../tags/37.html) in the middle of doing XXX. XXX is always a noun: 電話中 （電話中）means 'I'm on the phone right now!'
