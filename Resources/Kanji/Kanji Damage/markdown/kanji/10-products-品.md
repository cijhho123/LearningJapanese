[下](12-below-下.html) (below) + [品](10-products-品.html) (products) = 下品 (crude)  crude. Like chicks sitting with their legs open or guys picking their nose.

上品(じょうひん) な |  sophisticated ★☆☆☆☆   
[上](123-above-上.html) (above) + [品](10-products-品.html) (products) = 上品 (sophisticated)  can be used of people or things. It means graceful or sophisticated in a quiet or understated way. When it comes to people, 上品 is usually used about women, I mean 'ladies.'
