## Jukugo

服装(ふくそう) |  dressed like that. ★★★☆☆   
[服](1481-clothes-服.html) (clothes) + [装](800-dress-up-装.html) (dress up) = 服装 (dressed like that.)  a manner of dress- not just a uniform. If you tell your daughter she's not leaving the house 'dressed like that', that's 服装. Or if someone shows up to your party dressed way too formally, that's also a 服装 problem.  
---|---  
変装(へんそう) |  disguise ★★☆☆☆   
[変](904-change-変.html) (change) + [装](800-dress-up-装.html) (dress up) = 変装 (disguise)
