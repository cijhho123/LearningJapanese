原爆(げんばく) |  A-bomb ★☆☆☆☆ [NEO](../tags/15.html)   
[原](737-original-high-plain-原.html) (original/ high plain) + [爆](1559-explode-爆.html) (explode) = 原爆 (A-bomb )  the A-bomb (a contraction of 原子爆弾 (げんしばくだん))  
原住民(げんじゅうみん) |  native ☆☆☆☆☆   
[原](737-original-high-plain-原.html) (original/ high plain) + [住](460-dwell-住.html)[民](1165-folk-民.html) (residents) = 原住民 (native)  a person or animal native to the area. Indigenous.
