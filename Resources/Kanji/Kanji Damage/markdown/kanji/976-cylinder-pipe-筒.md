## Kunyomi

つつ |  usually defined as pipe but more accurately, it means cylinder.  Chimney, exhaust pipe, drainage pipe, gun barrel, canister, are all 筒. （BOOBOO: unlike the related kanji 管 (tube), which can bend, 筒 is always straight).
