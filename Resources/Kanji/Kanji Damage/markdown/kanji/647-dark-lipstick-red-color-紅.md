#  紅 dark lipstick red color

[糸](209-string-糸.html) (string) \+ [工](645-craft-or-industry-工.html) (craft or industry) [Same-ON](../tags/63.html)

★★☆☆☆

9 strokes

along with 朱 （vermilion), this is one of 2 color words for colors that don't exist in English. 

Unfortunately, you need this for 2 fairly common words: lipstick and tea. 

## Onyomi

KOU |  Doesn't my COLON look better with lipstick on it?
