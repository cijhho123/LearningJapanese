## Jukugo

印刷(いんさつ) する |  print ★★☆☆☆   
[印](1465-stamp-印.html) (stamp) + [刷](504-print-刷.html) (print) = 印刷 (print)  the physical process of printing, as in printing press - not to be confused with 出版 (しゅっぱん＝publishing), which is an entire industry.
