## Jukugo

公害(こうがい) |  pollution. ★★☆☆☆   
[公](137-public-公.html) (public) + [害](462-damage-害.html) (damage) = 公害 (pollution.)  pollution. Has a more broad meaning than the related word 汚染（おせん）。 Not just dirty smoggy air, but also noise pollution, the annoyance of someone talking on a cellphone on a train, and 情報公害 （じょうほうこうがい）: "ad pollution" - the inescapable advertisements one sees everywhere. BOOBOO:If you want to say "environmental pollution," you have to use 汚染、 not 公害。  
---|---  
被害(ひがい) |  casualties ★★☆☆☆   
[被](1511-get-injured-被.html) (get injured) + [害](462-damage-害.html) (damage) = 被害 (casualties)  damage (to property) or casualties (human): used about earthquakes, wars, and crimes.  
被害者(ひがいしゃ) |  victim ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[被](1511-get-injured-被.html)[害](462-damage-害.html) (casualties) + [者](255-professional-者.html) (professional) = 被害者 (victim)   
妨害(ぼうがい) |  interference ★★☆☆☆   
[妨](87-prevent-妨.html) (prevent) + [害](462-damage-害.html) (damage) = 妨害 (interference)  interference - a person interferes with you. (妨害 is used with people, while the related term 阻害（そがい） is used about objects）
