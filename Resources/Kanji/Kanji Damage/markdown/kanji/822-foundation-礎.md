## Jukugo

基礎(きそ) |  foundation ★★☆☆☆   
[基](1705-basis-基.html) (basis) + [礎](822-foundation-礎.html) (foundation) = 基礎 (foundation)  literally this kanji means 'foundation stone,' but unless you're a student of architectural history, you'll be using the metaphorical meaning instead: 基礎 means 'the fundamentals' (of a language,of a philosophy, or a martial art, etc.)
