## Jukugo

給料(きゅうりょう) |  wages ★★★☆☆   
[給](937-provide-給.html) (provide) + [料](1585-ingredients-fees-料.html) (ingredients / fees) = 給料 (wages)  wages - a bit more useful than the related word 給与. For instance, 'payday' is 給料日 (きゅうりょうび)、 not 給与日.  
---|---  
供給(きょうきゅう) |  supply ★★☆☆☆   
[供](1551-follower-供.html) (follower) + [給](937-provide-給.html) (provide) = 供給 (supply)  to supply (supply and demand / supply the troops with guns / supply your kid with a school lunches / the feed chute supplies grain to the conveyor belt at the cereal factory)  
給与(きゅうよ) |  salary, wages ★☆☆☆☆   
[給](937-provide-給.html) (provide) + [与](651-bestow-与.html) (bestow) = 給与 (salary, wages )
