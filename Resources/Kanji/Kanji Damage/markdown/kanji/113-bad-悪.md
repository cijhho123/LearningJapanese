## Jukugo

気持ち悪い(きもちわるい) |  pathetic / gross ★★★★☆   
[気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) + [持](1003-hold-持.html) (hold) + [悪](113-bad-悪.html) (bad) = 気持ち悪い (pathetic / gross)  has two meanings: If you say it about yourself, it means you feel terrible. If you say it about someone/thing else, it means,"That's gross and pathetic."  
---|---  
最悪(さいあく) のXXX |  the worst XXX EVAR. ★★★☆☆   
[最](311-most-最.html) (most) + [悪](113-bad-悪.html) (bad) = 最悪 (the worst XXX EVAR.)   
悪人(あくにん) |  (say hi to) the Bad Guy ![](../visualaids/1akujin.jpg) ★★★☆☆   
[悪](113-bad-悪.html) (bad) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 悪人 ((say hi to) the Bad Guy ![](../visualaids/1akujin.jpg))

[悪](113-bad-悪.html) (bad) + [口](9-mouth-small-box-radical-口.html) (mouth) = 悪口 (talking shit about someone.)
