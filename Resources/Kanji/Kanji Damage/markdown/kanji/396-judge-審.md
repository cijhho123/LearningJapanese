## Jukugo

審査(しんさ) する |  investigation, judging ★☆☆☆☆   
[審](396-judge-審.html) (judge) + [査](1488-inspect-査.html) (inspect) = 審査 (investigation, judging)  to judge or examine something - anything from Charles Manson's trial to American Idol. Anything with a panel of judges. Also when the fire marshal or the IRS comes to do an Official Inspection.
