## Jukugo

可愛い(かわいい) |  cute! ★★★★★ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[可](55-possible-可.html) (possible) + [愛](610-love-愛.html) (love) = 可愛い (cute!)   
---|---  
愛国心(あいこくしん) |  patriotism ★★☆☆☆   
[愛](610-love-愛.html) (love) + [国](438-country-国.html) (country) + [心](98-heart-心.html) (heart) = 愛国心 (patriotism)   
恋愛(れんあい) |  love in general ★★☆☆☆   
[恋](903-passion-恋.html) (passion) + [愛](610-love-愛.html) (love) = 恋愛 (love in general)  love or relationships in general - that is to say, if there's an ad for fortune-telling that says, "Test your luck in money! Love! and health!" it'll be using 恋愛. If there's an article in a womens' magazine, "How to get love in 10 steps!" it'll be using 恋愛. But you wouldn't tell a hot guy " I 恋愛 you very much!" See?  
愛情(あいじょう) |  affection : more than friendship and less than love. ★☆☆☆☆   
[愛](610-love-愛.html) (love) + [情](471-emotion-情.html) (emotion) = 愛情 (affection : more than friendship and less than love.)
