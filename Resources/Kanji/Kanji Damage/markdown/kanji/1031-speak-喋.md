## Mnemonic

|  OK, this mnemonic is a bit of a stretch: The right-side part of 喋 looks like 葉, which is a kanji in the word, 'word'=言葉.   
so, if you buy THAT argument,then the mnemonic becomes really easy, like so:   
When you speak, your mouth says words (言葉).
