## Jukugo

番号(ばんごう) |  number ★★★★★   
[番](395-number-番.html) (number) + [号](656-id-number-号.html) (ID number) = 番号 (number)  number which has been assigned to a thing, as in phone number or apartment number, or ZIP code number.  
---|---  
一番(いちばん) |  number one! ★★★★☆ [NEO](../tags/15.html)   
[一](1-one-line-radical-一.html) (one) + [番](395-number-番.html) (number) = 一番 (number one!)  the best! Number one!  
交番(こうばん) |  police box ★★★☆☆   
[交](1009-combine-交.html) (combine) + [番](395-number-番.html) (number) = 交番 (police box)  police box - a small shop in your neighborhood where police hang out and wait to help you or jail you.  
XXX 番目(ばんめ) のYYY |  thing in a row ★★★☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)[COUNTER](../tags/38.html)   
[番](395-number-番.html) (number) + [目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) = 番目 (thing in a row)  this is a term used in counting things in a row. For instance, in English we'd say "Second book from the right" or "Third shelf from the top" in Japanese, they'd say: 2番目のbook OR 4番目のshelf.
