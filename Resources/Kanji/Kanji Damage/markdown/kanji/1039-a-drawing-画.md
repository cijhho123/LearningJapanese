## Jukugo

映画(えいが) |  movie ★★★★★   
[映](406-project-reflect-映.html) (project, reflect) + [画](1039-a-drawing-画.html) (a drawing) = 映画 (movie)   
---|---  
漫画(まんが) |  manga ★★★★☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[漫](111-manga-漫.html) (manga) + [画](1039-a-drawing-画.html) (a drawing) = 漫画 (manga)  what's that???  
計画(けいかく) |  plan ★★★☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[計](18-measure-計.html) (measure) + [画](1039-a-drawing-画.html) (a drawing) = 計画 (plan)  Pretty close to 予定 （よてい） in meaning, but perhaps more elaborate - you'd use 計画 to say "I have a plan to get rich!" or to say "I planned out our whole vacation to 3 countries." . . but you wouldn't use 予定。  
画面(がめん) |  screen ★★★☆☆   
[画](1039-a-drawing-画.html) (a drawing) + [面](813-front-surface-face-面.html) (front surface / face) = 画面 (screen)  screen - both movie screen and computer/tv screen
