#  ￥ yen

★★★★☆

4 strokes

[PK](../tags/18.html)

This is annoying because it looks like sheep but means the same thing as 円. (even Japanese people use ￥ and 円 interchangeably.)   
HOW TO TELL THEM APART:   
Yen （￥）only gots 2 horizontal lines, but sheep (羊） has 3 horizontal lines. . . because _a sheep costs 3 yen._
