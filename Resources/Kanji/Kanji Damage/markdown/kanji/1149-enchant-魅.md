## Jukugo

魅力(みりょく) がある・ない |  alluring ★★☆☆☆   
[魅](1149-enchant-魅.html) (enchant) + [力](175-strong-力.html) (strong) = 魅力 (alluring)  a captivating, charming, glamorous person (not used about movies or artworks)  
---|---  
魅惑(みわく) な or する |  lure or fascination ★☆☆☆☆   
[魅](1149-enchant-魅.html) (enchant) + [惑](668-be-misguided-or-tempted-惑.html) (be misguided or tempted) = 魅惑 (lure or fascination)  a lure or fascination - in the old sense of fascination (i.e. sirens luring sailors to their doom.)
