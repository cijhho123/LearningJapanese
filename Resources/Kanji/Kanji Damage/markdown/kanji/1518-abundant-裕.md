## Jukugo

余裕(よゆう) |  time/room to spare ★★★★☆   
[余](928-excess-余.html) (excess) + [裕](1518-abundant-裕.html) (abundant) = 余裕 (time/room to spare)  room/time/money to spare. Like, don't sweat it.  
---|---  
裕福(ゆうふく) な |  financially wealthy with cash. ★☆☆☆☆   
[裕](1518-abundant-裕.html) (abundant) + [福](1500-good-luck-福.html) (good luck) = 裕福 (financially wealthy with cash.)
