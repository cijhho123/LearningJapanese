## Jukugo

離婚(りこん) する |  divorce ★★★☆☆   
[離](1042-divorce-physical-distance-between-things-離.html) (divorce / physical distance between things) + [婚](1163-marriage-婚.html) (marriage) = 離婚 (divorce)   
---|---  
距離(きょり) |  distance ★★☆☆☆   
[距](684-distance-距.html) (distance) + [離](1042-divorce-physical-distance-between-things-離.html) (divorce / physical distance between things) = 距離 (distance)  physical distance between two things: towns on a map, for instance, or the distance between an archer and a target - a much bigger distance than the related word 間隔.  
分離(ぶんり) |  segregation / separation ☆☆☆☆☆   
[分](169-understand-divide-minute-分.html) (understand / divide / minute) + [離](1042-divorce-physical-distance-between-things-離.html) (divorce / physical distance between things) = 分離 (segregation / separation)  The dictionary says "separation, " as in separation of church and state, or "the river separates the two countries." Politically it means to segregate people, like in the American South, or what Japan does to Burakumin.
