## Kunyomi

し＊ってる |  to know something or someone. (BOOBOO: as opposed to 分かります, which means 'Oh shit! now I get it!' or 'I just realized / comprehended something!')  Typical example: 'Do you know the band Rotting Christ?' (ロッチング・キリストというバンドを知ってる？）

承知(しょうち) する |  to agree in theory ★★☆☆☆ [CCW](../tags/4.html)[F](../tags/26.html)   
[承](1723-to-be-told-consent-承.html) (to be told / consent) + [知](1050-know-知.html) (know) = 承知 (to agree in theory)  to agree in theory (business - speak). Stronger than 承る（うけたまわる）. 承知 is what you say when you have heard and understood a business proposal, and you agree with it. It doesn't necessarily mean that you will sign the contract or do whatever the person says; merely that you want to work together with them to accomplish these goals, in theory, after 100 more meetings.
