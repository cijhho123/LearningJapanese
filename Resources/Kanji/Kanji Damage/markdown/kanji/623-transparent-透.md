## Jukugo

透明(とうめい) な |  transparent ★★☆☆☆   
[透](623-transparent-透.html) (transparent) + [明](43-bright-明.html) (bright) = 透明 (transparent)  transparent (like water or glass)(BOOBOO: not to be confused with 'invisible', which is 目で見えない （めでみえない））  
---|---  
浸透(しんとう) する |  permeate ☆☆☆☆☆   
[浸](631-immerse-浸.html) (immerse) + [透](623-transparent-透.html) (transparent) = 浸透 (permeate)  to permeate (like mud permeating a towel); to stealthily take over (communism) ; osmosis
