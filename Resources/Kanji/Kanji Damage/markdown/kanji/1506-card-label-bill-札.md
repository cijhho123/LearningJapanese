#  札 card / label / bill

[木](335-tree-木.html) (tree) \+ [![](../assets/radsmall/hook-7642bba2d410f3aa50ba5e4e59ec22eae6e5e0330593a10f687f957f8ed5c5d2.jpg)](1766-hook.html) (hook) 

★★☆☆☆

5 strokes

[JERK](../tags/11.html)[COUNTER](../tags/38.html)

This usually means a small card or label, but as a suffix, it means "bill", as in "ten-dollar bill." 

## Onyomi

SATSU |  I SAW TWO dollar bills laying in the sidewalk yesterday. Weird.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  Hang the label from a hook driven into the tree-trunk.  
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

ふだ |  a label, nametag, small sign, etc.   
★★☆☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

千円札(せんえんさつ) |  1,000 yen bill ★★★☆☆ [COUNTER](../tags/38.html)   
[千](229-thousand-千.html) (thousand) + [円](72-yencircle-円.html) (yen/circle) + [札](1506-card-label-bill-札.html) (card / label / bill) = 千円札 (1,000 yen bill)  any money in paper form is a 'satsu' - a ten dollar bill is a $10 satsu, etc.
