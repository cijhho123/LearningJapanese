## Jukugo

意味(いみ) |  meaning ★★★★★   
[意](157-meaning-意.html) (meaning) + [味](383-flavor-味.html) (flavor) = 意味 (meaning)   
---|---  
意見(いけん) |  opinion ★★★★★   
[意](157-meaning-意.html) (meaning) + [見](520-look-見.html) (look) = 意見 (opinion)   
に 注意(ちゅうい) する |  be careful! ★★★★☆   
[注](458-pour-be-careful-注.html) (pour / be careful) + [意](157-meaning-意.html) (meaning) = 注意 (be careful!)  be careful! caution! (when used as a 'suru' verb, it means, 'to warn')  
意識(いしき) のある |  awareness ★★☆☆☆   
[意](157-meaning-意.html) (meaning) + [識](679-be-conscious-識.html) (be conscious) = 意識 (awareness)  to have an awareness or be conscious of something- women are conscious of potential rapists in an everyday, peripheral way that men are not. Hippies are environmentally conscious. That's ishiki.
