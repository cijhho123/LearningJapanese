## Jukugo

失礼(しつれい) な |  rude ★★★★☆   
[失](1051-miss-out-on-失.html) (miss out on) + [礼](1504-polite-礼.html) (polite) = 失礼 (rude)  rude, without manners.  
---|---  
お礼(おれい) に |  in thanks for ★★★☆☆ in thanks for. As in, "In thanks for the sweater, I bought him a moray eel."  
礼儀(れいぎ) |  Formal manners ★★☆☆☆   
[礼](1504-polite-礼.html) (polite) + [儀](878-politeness-儀.html) (politeness) = 礼儀 (Formal manners)  Manners used in formal situations: correct bowing proceedures, sonkeigo, respecting heirarchy.  
礼金(れいきん) |  key money ★★☆☆☆   
[礼](1504-polite-礼.html) (polite) + [金](909-gold-金.html) (gold) = 礼金 (key money)  literally 'politeness money.' - sometimes the phrase "key money" is also used - as if that explains anything. This is the legalized bribe you pay for the privilege of paying rent on a new apartment.
