## Jukugo

矛盾(むじゅん) |  contradiction ★★★☆☆   
[矛](1333-halberd-矛.html) (halberd) + [盾](1303-shield-盾.html) (shield) = 矛盾 (contradiction )  contradiction / hypocrisy (interesting: the kanji literally mean 'spear plus shield'! Picture a guy claiming his shield is for self-defense, even as he holds a bloody spear in his other hand) . . ..  Japanese, tellingly, does not have a word for hypocrisy, so if you want to say hypocrite, you have to use this word (mujun) and try and make it fit.  
---|---  
矛先(ほこさき) |  bruuuunt ☆☆☆☆☆   
[矛](1333-halberd-矛.html) (halberd) + [先](810-earlier-the-tip-先.html) (earlier/ the tip) = 矛先 (bruuuunt)  literally 'spearhead'; figuratively, the brunt.
