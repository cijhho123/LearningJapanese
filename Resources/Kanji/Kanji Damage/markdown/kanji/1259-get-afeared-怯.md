## Kunyomi

おび＊える |  To be intimidated. Sometimes it is used the same way as more popular cousin 怖がる （こわがる）: to be scared. But often it connotes a very specific form of fear : to be cowed, scared into submission - usually used with things like political intimidation or domestic violence. To be scared into complying or obeying.
