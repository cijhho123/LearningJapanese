## Jukugo

純粋(じゅんすい) な |  essence ★★★☆☆   
[純](1718-epitome-純.html) (epitome) + [粋](391-essence-粋.html) (essence) = 純粋 (essence)  the essence of something. Pure pleasure! Pure blood! Pure honor!  
---|---  
単純(たんじゅん) な |  simple version ★☆☆☆☆   
[単](596-merely-単.html) (merely) + [純](1718-epitome-純.html) (epitome) = 単純 (simple version)  the simple kind of X as opposed to the complicated kind (not rude). Also, simple-minded, mouth-breathing person (pretty rude).
