#  現 reality

[王](444-king-王.html) (king) \+ [見](520-look-見.html) (look) 

★★★★☆

11 strokes

[DUPE](../tags/6.html)

Is this one a JERK? The kun-yomi meanings don't quite match the jukugo meanings. But . . . if something appears or is displayed for you, that means you're aware that it's real now. So maybe not a JERK after all!

---|---  
現実(げんじつ) |  reality ★★★☆☆   
[現](524-reality-現.html) (reality) + [実](964-truth-実.html) (truth) = 現実 (reality)   
現場(げんば) |  the current place ★★★☆☆ [KUN ON](../tags/36.html)   
[現](524-reality-現.html) (reality) + [場](1208-place-場.html) (place) = 現場 (the current place)  the current place. (this one is used on the 'YOU ARE HERE' part of maps)

[現](524-reality-現.html) (reality) + [在](1103-be-real-在.html) (be real) = 現在 (the present time)  the present time - but also can mean "the present situation" or "the staus quo."
