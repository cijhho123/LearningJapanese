yeah, this is the taku from "otaku." (BOOBOO: In fact, that "otaku" is 99% of the time written as オタク, so you don't get to use the kanji.) 

## Onyomi

TAKU |  as in, "I TALKED TO the homeless guy about what kind of residence he would like. "  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  They say that “A man's residence is his castle." So that explains why I put on a crown and kick up my heels when I get home from work.

## Jukugo

帰宅(きたく) する |  go home. (a little formal) ★☆☆☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[帰](638-go-back-home-帰.html) (go back home) + [宅](127-residence-宅.html) (residence) = 帰宅 (go home. (a little formal))   
---|---  
自宅(じたく) |  one's own home ★☆☆☆☆   
[自](78-my-own-自.html) (my own) + [宅](127-residence-宅.html) (residence) = 自宅 (one's own home )  a slightly formal word for one's own home (BOOBOO: most people just say うち！）  
宅急便(たっきゅうびん) |  home-delivery ☆☆☆☆☆   
[宅](127-residence-宅.html) (residence) + [急](633-urgent-急.html) (urgent) + [便](1594-convenient-poop-便.html) (convenient / poop) = 宅急便 (home-delivery)  home-delivery service (pizza, sex)
