配達(はいたつ) する |  deliver ★★★☆☆   
[配](724-distribute-配.html) (distribute) + [達](332-pluraldelivery-達.html) (plural/delivery) = 配達 (deliver)  to deliver. Used about business - used when money changes hands. Deliver by truck or UPS or FEDEX.

[配](724-distribute-配.html) (distribute) + [慮](1686-consideration-慮.html) (consideration) = 配慮 (to give consideration to, to be kind to xxxx. To give special treatment to (the handicapped, i.e. by providing ramps))
