## Jukugo

襲撃(しゅうげき) する |  assault ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[襲](1730-onslaught-襲.html) (onslaught) + [撃](1300-charge-撃.html) (charge) = 襲撃 (assault)  a raid. Like an assault done by a team of people, not just one mean guy. Usually used about the SWAT team raiding a house. But a gang can raid another gang.
