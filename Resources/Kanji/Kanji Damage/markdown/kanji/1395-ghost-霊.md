## Jukugo

幽霊(ゆうれい) |  ghost ★★★☆☆   
[幽](830-occult-幽.html) (occult) + [霊](1395-ghost-霊.html) (ghost) = 幽霊 (ghost)  a vengeful ghost - unlike 悪霊（あくりょう） and 妖怪 （ようかい）, 幽霊 were once human and driven by revenge. 幽霊 are more popular in horror movies (like The Ring), whereas 妖怪 are more traditional.  
---|---  
悪霊(あくりょう) |  evil spirit ★☆☆☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[悪](113-bad-悪.html) (bad) + [霊](1395-ghost-霊.html) (ghost) = 悪霊 (evil spirit)  an evil spirit; a spirit who puts curses on people, or brings misfortune to entire towns, or possesses innocent fools. Unlike 幽霊, they don't attack you directly, and unlike 妖怪 don't have a physical form  
霊感(れいかん) |  inspiration ★☆☆☆☆   
[霊](1395-ghost-霊.html) (ghost) + [感](755-feeling-感.html) (feeling) = 霊感 (inspiration)  inspiration. To get inspired: 霊感を受ける（うける）  To inspire someone: 霊感を与える（あたえる）
