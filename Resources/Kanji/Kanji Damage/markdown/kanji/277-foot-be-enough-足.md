## Jukugo

不足(ふそく) |  shortage of ★★★★☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[不](13-un-不.html) (un) + [足](277-foot-be-enough-足.html) (foot/ be enough) = 不足 (shortage of)  To not have enough of a physical thing (money, time, nurses, teachers.) Also implies that there is a specific amount that is required, For example -犬不足 (a dog shortage). There's no particle or verb needed.
