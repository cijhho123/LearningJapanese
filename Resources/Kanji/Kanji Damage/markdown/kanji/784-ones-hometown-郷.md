## Jukugo

帰郷(ききょう) する |  return to one's hometown ☆☆☆☆☆   
[帰](638-go-back-home-帰.html) (go back home) + [郷](784-ones-hometown-郷.html) (one's hometown) = 帰郷 (return to one's hometown)   
---|---  
郷愁(きょうしゅう) |  nostalgia ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[郷](784-ones-hometown-郷.html) (one's hometown) + [愁](379-sorrow-愁.html) (sorrow) = 郷愁 (nostalgia)  literary word for nostalgia (BOOBOO: most people just say 懐かしい （なつかしい！）)  
故郷(こきょう) |  hometown ☆☆☆☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[故](707-breakdown-accident-故.html) (breakdown, accident) + [郷](784-ones-hometown-郷.html) (one's hometown) = 故郷 (hometown )  A somewhat literary word for one's hometown （BOOBOO: most people just say 実家 （じっか）, meaning "parents' house" instead).
