共有(きょうゆう) |  jointly owned ★★☆☆☆   
[共](1548-with-共.html) (with) + [有](1100-exist-有.html) (exist) = 共有 (jointly owned)  shared / communal / jointly owned things.  
有毒(ゆうどく) な |  toxic, poisonous ★☆☆☆☆   
[有](1100-exist-有.html) (exist) + [毒](483-poison-addict-毒.html) (poison/ addict) = 有毒 (toxic, poisonous)   
所有者(しょゆうしゃ) |  owner ★☆☆☆☆   
[所](506-area-attribute-所.html) (area / attribute) + [有](1100-exist-有.html) (exist) + [者](255-professional-者.html) (professional) = 所有者 (owner)  the owner of something. (しょゆうする； to own)  
有益(ゆうえき) な |  beneficial ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[有](1100-exist-有.html) (exist) + [益](1444-profit-益.html) (profit) = 有益 (beneficial)  useful or beneficial, (a policy beneficial to the State /it was a useful experience (a learning experience))
