## Jukugo

自由(じゆう) な |  freedom ★★★★★   
[自](78-my-own-自.html) (my own) + [由](1374-freedom-reason-由.html) (freedom / reason) = 自由 (freedom)   
---|---  
自分(じぶん) の |  one's own ★★★★★   
[自](78-my-own-自.html) (my own) + [分](169-understand-divide-minute-分.html) (understand / divide / minute) = 自分 (one's own)  one's own (dog, blunt, fungus, etc.) - BOOBOO: sometimes it also is used to mean "you" or "she", as in "She did it herself, as opposed to having others do it for her."  
自信(じしん) |  self-confidence ★★★★☆   
[自](78-my-own-自.html) (my own) + [信](63-believe-信.html) (believe) = 自信 (self-confidence)   
自衛隊(じえいたい) |  Japan's army ★★☆☆☆   
[自](78-my-own-自.html)[衛](1579-defense-衛.html) (self-defense) + [隊](1228-troop-隊.html) (troop) = 自衛隊 (Japan's army)  Japan's army, in English called the SDF (self-defense forces)  
自業自得(じごうじとく) |  "I brought this on myself!" ★☆☆☆☆   
[自](78-my-own-自.html) (my own) + [業](1397-business-業.html) (business) + [得](1237-bargain-obtain-得.html) (bargain / obtain) = 自業自得 ("I brought this on myself!")
