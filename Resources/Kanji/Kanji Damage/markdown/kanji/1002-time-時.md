[四](694-the-numeral-4-四.html)[時](1002-time-時.html) (4 o'clock) + [間](162-a-period-of-time-間.html) (a period of time) = 四時間 (4 hours' time)  4 hours' time. As in, this bus ride takes 4 hours.
