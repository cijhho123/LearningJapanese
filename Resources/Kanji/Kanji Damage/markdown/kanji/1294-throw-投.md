## Jukugo

投票(とうひょう) する |  vote ★★★☆☆   
[投](1294-throw-投.html) (throw) + [票](575-ballot-票.html) (ballot) = 投票 (vote)  to vote (also, it can mean election, as in "Did he win the vote?")  
---|---  
投書(とうしょ) する |  contribute ★☆☆☆☆   
[投](1294-throw-投.html) (throw) + [書](1659-write-書.html) (write) = 投書 (contribute)  contribute to a magazine - letter to the editor, purikura, etc.
