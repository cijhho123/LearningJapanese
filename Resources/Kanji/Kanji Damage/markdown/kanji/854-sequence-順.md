## Jukugo

順番(じゅんばん) |  order ★★★★☆   
[順](854-sequence-順.html) (sequence) + [番](395-number-番.html) (number) = 順番 (order)  order of people in a line (take a number!) - order of bands on a live show.  
---|---  
順調(じゅんちょう) な or である |  no problem ★☆☆☆☆   
[順](854-sequence-順.html) (sequence) + [調](1636-check-out-調.html) (check out) = 順調 (no problem)  fine, fair, no problems, smoothly. Usually used like this : "Hi! How's business these days?" "Oh, 順調、 I can't complain!"  
順序(じゅんじょ) |  sequence of steps ☆☆☆☆☆   
[順](854-sequence-順.html) (sequence) + [序](1330-first-part-preface-序.html) (first part / preface) = 順序 (sequence of steps)  the sequence of steps in a proceedure - the order of books arranged by a certain system, (i.e. Dewey Decimal).
