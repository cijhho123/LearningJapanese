## Jukugo

探偵(たんてい) |  private detective ★★★☆☆   
[探](568-look-for-探.html) (look for) + [偵](543-detective-偵.html) (detective) = 探偵 (private detective)  BOOBOO: Most books say this means 'detective' but really it means private detective, not police. (police detective is 刑事 －けいじ）. There's a huge P.I. industry here - they have advertisements in all the magazines and even posters on walls on the street!
