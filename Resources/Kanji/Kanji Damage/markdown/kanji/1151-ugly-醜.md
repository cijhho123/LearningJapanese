## Jukugo

醜悪(しゅうあく) |  hideous, scandalous ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[醜](1151-ugly-醜.html) (ugly) + [悪](113-bad-悪.html) (bad) = 醜悪 (hideous, scandalous)  hideous - more literary and more extreme than the related word 醜い (みにくい). Also - unlike みにくい, 醜悪 is more often used figuratively to mean 'scandalous' : an ugly divorce, a scandalous rift between political factions.
