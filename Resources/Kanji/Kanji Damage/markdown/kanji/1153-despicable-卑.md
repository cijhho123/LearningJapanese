## Kunyomi

## Jukugo

卑怯(ひきょう) な |  treacherous coward ★☆☆☆☆   
[卑](1153-despicable-卑.html) (despicable) + [怯](1259-get-afeared-怯.html) (get afeared) = 卑怯 (treacherous coward)  not just a coward ,but someone who does treacherous, trifling bullshit because they're a coward - like if they fight you they know they can't win fairly so they'll knee your groin, they're a 卑怯なmotherfucker.  
---|---  
卑下(ひげ) する |  put yourself down ☆☆☆☆☆   
[卑](1153-despicable-卑.html) (despicable) + [下](12-below-下.html) (below) = 卑下 (put yourself down)  to put yourself down (BOOBOO: only used when talking to others - mumbling "I suck!" to yourself doesn't count for some reason)
