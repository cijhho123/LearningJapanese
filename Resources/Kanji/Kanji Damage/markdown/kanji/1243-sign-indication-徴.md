## Jukugo

特徴(とくちょう) |  characteristic ★★★☆☆   
[特](1196-special-特.html) (special) + [徴](1243-sign-indication-徴.html) (sign / indication) = 特徴 (characteristic )  a characteristic of something ("Loving big stuff for the sake of big stuff is characteristic of Americans.")  
---|---  
象徴(しょうちょう) |  symbol ★★☆☆☆   
[象](1220-elephant-phenomenon-象.html) (elephant / phenomenon) + [徴](1243-sign-indication-徴.html) (sign / indication) = 象徴 (symbol)  literary symbol ; "In the novel, the trees symbolize life." 'The color red in the painting symbolizes communism.' (しょうちょうする=to symbolize something)
