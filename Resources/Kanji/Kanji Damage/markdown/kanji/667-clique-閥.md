## Jukugo

派閥(はばつ) |  faction ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[派](759-factionbling-派.html) (faction/bling) + [閥](667-clique-閥.html) (clique) = 派閥 (faction)  A faction within a political party or corporation. Like every country has factions, but 派閥 has the nuance of 'We all claim to be loyal to the organization but behind closed doors we are plotting to hijack it with our own agenda.' Nobody would say publicly, "I'm in this 派閥！”

FDR specifically told MacArthur to break them up, but Mac didn't.Companies like Mitsui bank and Mitsubishi, Nissan, etc. have profited handsomely off of slave labor and Hirohito's war, and they continue to be the bedrock of society.
