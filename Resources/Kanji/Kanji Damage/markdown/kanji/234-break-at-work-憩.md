#  憩 break at work

[舌](230-tongue-舌.html) (tongue) \+ [息](101-son-breath-息.html) (son / breath) 

★★☆☆☆

16 strokes

## Onyomi

KEI |  it's OK to take a break if you've been working for over 3 minutes.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  A break at work is when you catch your breath and talk to co-workers with your tongue.   
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

いこい |  place for recreation and relaxation - 99% used in the following one expression: 憩いの場 (ikoi no basho).   
☆☆☆☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

休憩(きゅうけい) |  break-time ★★☆☆☆   
[休](340-rest-休.html) (rest) + [憩](234-break-at-work-憩.html) (break at work) = 休憩 (break-time)  break at work OR break from studying/training. Just long enough to eat some food and stretch - usually 15 minutes to an hour. .
