#  地 area

[土](235-earth-土.html) (earth) \+ [![](../assets/radsmall/scorpion-3c15993d9c684db536adfa517996e2723eaf952bb7974b438627432ceb3196ae.jpg)](1599-scorpion.html) (scorpion) [Same-ON](../tags/63.html)

★★★★☆

6 strokes

[BETA](../tags/2.html)

This is different from other 'place' kanji like 場 and 所, because 地 doesn't emphasize the a particular activity done at the place : 地 instead emphasizes the land, the physical, geographical aspects of the space. 

## Onyomi

CHI, JI |  Damn it smells good in here. Did JESUS cut the CHEESE in this area?   
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  Dangerous scorpions are on the earth in that area.   
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

地球(ちきゅう) |  earth ★★★★☆   
[地](1600-area-地.html) (area) + [球](727-sphere-球.html) (sphere) = 地球 (earth)  the world, the physical planet Earth.  
---|---  
地域(ちいき) |  district, zone ★★★★☆   
[地](1600-area-地.html) (area) + [域](671-region-域.html) (region) = 地域 (district, zone)  a region or district, a zone. (emphasizes a social community moreso than a geographical area) (i.e. you wouldn't use it for the tropics or the Arctic, but you'd use it for Chinatown ,the American South or Kanto/Kansai)  
地図(ちず) |  map ★★★★☆   
[地](1600-area-地.html) (area) + [図](1587-diagram-map-図.html) (diagram/ map) = 地図 (map)   
地震(じしん) |  earthquake ★★★☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[地](1600-area-地.html) (area) + [震](1389-shake-tremble-震.html) (shake, tremble) = 地震 (earthquake)   
地帯(ちたい) |  zone ★★☆☆☆   
[地](1600-area-地.html) (area) + [帯](825-belt-帯.html) (belt) = 地帯 (zone)  a geographical district or zone - emphaizes a physical location: the tropics, the steppes. ちたい is much bigger and broader than 地域.  
地面(じめん) |  ground ★★☆☆☆   
[地](1600-area-地.html) (area) + [面](813-front-surface-face-面.html) (front surface / face) = 地面 (ground)  the ground/floor (applies both inside and outside)  
地理(ちり) |  geography ★☆☆☆☆   
[地](1600-area-地.html) (area) + [理](445-reason-理.html) (reason) = 地理 (geography)
