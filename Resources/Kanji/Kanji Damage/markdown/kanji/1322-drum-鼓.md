## Jukugo

太鼓(たいこ) |  Japanese drums ★☆☆☆☆   
[太](413-fat-太.html) (fat) + [鼓](1322-drum-鼓.html) (drum) = 太鼓 (Japanese drums)  traditional Japanese drums - the kind that can be up to 15 feet wide. (BOOBOO: Rock and roll drums are just called ドラム.)  
---|---  
鼓動(こどう) |  heartbeat ☆☆☆☆☆   
[鼓](1322-drum-鼓.html) (drum) + [動](1078-movement-動.html) (movement) = 鼓動 (heartbeat )  heartbeat (although most people just say ドキドキ, the sound of a beating heart)
