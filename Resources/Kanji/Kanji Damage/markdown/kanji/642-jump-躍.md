## Jukugo

飛躍(ひやく) |  a leap ★☆☆☆☆   
[飛](1533-fly-飛.html) (fly) + [躍](642-jump-躍.html) (jump) = 飛躍 (a leap)  a leap. Usually figurative. Make a leap forward in technology. Not used as a verb.  
---|---  
跳躍(ちょうやく) する |  to jump horizontally ★☆☆☆☆   
[跳](1654-leap-up-跳.html) (leap up) + [躍](642-jump-躍.html) (jump) = 跳躍 (to jump horizontally)  to jump horizontally, as in the Olympic long jump.  
躍進(やくしん) |  rapid progress ☆☆☆☆☆   
[躍](642-jump-躍.html) (jump) + [進](333-progress-進.html) (progress) = 躍進 (rapid progress)
