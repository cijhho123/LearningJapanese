## Jukugo

卑猥(ひわい) な |  obscene ★☆☆☆☆   
[卑](1153-despicable-卑.html) (despicable) + [猥](1455-obscene-猥.html) (obscene) = 卑猥 (obscene)  Mostly used BY older men, and ABOUT a certain kind of person; Nasty, dirty, obscene. Not always used in a bad way.  
---|---  
猥褻(わいせつ) な |  obscene ★☆☆☆☆ [ABU](../tags/1.html) gross, obscene. Always bad. Used about a lewd behavior or an incident (i.e. train groping), not about the person doing it.
