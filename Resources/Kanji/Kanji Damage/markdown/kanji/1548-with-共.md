[共](1548-with-共.html) (with) + [通](1342-pass-通.html) (pass) + [点](126-point-点.html) (point) = 共通点 (common point)  common point (between two things or people)  
---|---  
公共(こうきょう) |  public ★★☆☆☆   
[公](137-public-公.html) (public) + [共](1548-with-共.html) (with) = 公共 (public)  public, as in "public toilet" or "public telephone"  
共産主義(きょうさんしゅぎ) |  communism ★★☆☆☆   
[共](1548-with-共.html) (with) + [産](736-childbirth-production-of-things-産.html) (childbirth, production of things) + [主](454-mastermainly-主.html)[義](877-righteousness-義.html) (- ism) = 共産主義 (communism)   
共有(きょうゆう) |  jointly owned ★★☆☆☆   
[共](1548-with-共.html) (with) + [有](1100-exist-有.html) (exist) = 共有 (jointly owned)  shared / communal / jointly owned things.
