#  大 big

★★★★★

3 strokes

[PN](../tags/19.html)[PK](../tags/18.html)[ILL PAIR](../tags/10.html)

If you're looking up a 大 jukugo in your dictionary and can't find it, try using おお instead of だい or たい. For some reason, this kanji tends to go kunyomi in its jukugo! 

## Onyomi

## Jukugo

大好き(だいすき) |  really like ★★★★★   
[大](397-big-大.html) (big) + [好](7-like-好.html) (like) = 大好き (really like)   
---|---  
大変(たいへん) な |  that's terrible! ★★★★★   
[大](397-big-大.html) (big) + [変](904-change-変.html) (change) = 大変 (that's terrible!)  this has 2 meanings, both used equally often: "Tony is tai hen!" , for instance, could mean:  a) I feel sorry for Tony because his job sucks and his girl is cheating.  b) Tony is a really scary, mean person.   
大事(だいじ) な |  vital ★★★★☆   
[大](397-big-大.html) (big) + [事](1660-action-incident-事.html) (action/ incident) = 大事 (vital)  vital, important. Can also be used as a sort of goodbye - but only in cases where you're sort of worried about the person. Like if they're coming down with a cold, you'd say, "お大事に！” instead of ”またね！”  
大人(おとな) |  adult ★★★☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[大](397-big-大.html) (big) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 大人 (adult)  adult, as opposed to minor, not as opposed to 'with clothes on.'  
大家(おおや) さん |  landlord ★★★☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[大](397-big-大.html) (big) + [家](1223-home-家.html) (home) = 大家 (landlord)   
大型(おおがた) の |  large-scale ★★☆☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[大](397-big-大.html) (big) + [型](1191-type-proper-way-型.html) (type / proper way) = 大型 (large-scale )
