## Kunyomi

さかい |  boundary - can be used with countries OR property lines of 2 pieces of land. Usually used when talking about crossing a boundary, or being within the boundary.   
★☆☆☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

環境(かんきょう) |  environment ★★★☆☆   
[環](764-environment-環.html) (environment) + [境](523-border-境.html) (border) = 環境 (environment)  environment (like English, this is not limited to nature. A bad kid can be a "product of his environment.")  
---|---  
国境(こっきょう) |  national border ★★☆☆☆   
[国](438-country-国.html) (country) + [境](523-border-境.html) (border) = 国境 (national border)   
境界(きょうかい) |  boundary ★★☆☆☆   
[境](523-border-境.html) (border) + [界](935-the-world-界.html) (the world) = 境界 (boundary)  means border or boundary of property (80% the same usage as さかい （境） , but a bit more formal). BOTH 境 and 境界 are often used in the figurative sense - like "This film pushes the envelope of good taste" or "His art blurs the boundary between painting and sculpture".  Also used in science.
