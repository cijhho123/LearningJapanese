## Jukugo

比較的(ひかくてき) |  comparatively ★★★☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[比](21-compare-比.html)[較](1065-evaluate-較.html) (compare) + [的](1430-motivation-al-的.html) (motivation / '-al') = 比較的 (comparatively)  comparatively (BOOBOO: you DON'T use a 'na' at the end, for some reason)  
---|---  
比較(ひかく) する |  compare ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[比](21-compare-比.html) (compare) + [較](1065-evaluate-較.html) (evaluate) = 比較 (compare)  a more formal word for 'compare': You'd 比べる AC/DC albums to see which one rocked the most, but you'd 比較 the GDP of developing nations.

[比](21-compare-比.html) (compare) + [率](1648-ratio-率.html) (ratio) = 比率 (ratio)
