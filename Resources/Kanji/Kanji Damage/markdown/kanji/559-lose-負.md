## Jukugo

勝負(しょうぶ) |  win or lose situation ★★★★☆   
[勝](891-win-勝.html) (win) + [負](559-lose-負.html) (lose) = 勝負 (win or lose situation)  a win or lose situation. Fighting over a girl/boy. Not neccesairly the final, decisive battle, the Superbowl. . The game/battle is won or lost on account of ..XXX. .  
---|---  
負け犬(まけいぬ) |  loser ★★☆☆☆   
[負](559-lose-負.html) (lose) + [犬](408-dog-犬.html) (dog) = 負け犬 (loser)  Since it literally means 'losing dog,' you'd think that was a transliteration of the English 'underdog,' But no, it simply means loser or schlemiel. Japan doesn't really have much respect for plucky underdogs who dare to try and rise above their status.  
負担(ふたん) |  financial responsibility ☆☆☆☆☆   
[負](559-lose-負.html) (lose) + [担](304-carry-on-your-back-担.html) (carry on your back) = 負担 (financial responsibility)  a responsibility, usually financial, which is declared by a system. "If a guest falls off the hotel roof, it's the hotel's responsibility." "The parent is responsible for paying for the child's lunch at boarding school."
