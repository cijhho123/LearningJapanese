## Jukugo

召し上がる(めしあがる) |  let's eat ★★☆☆☆ [F](../tags/26.html)   
[召](166-summon-召.html) (summon) + [上](123-above-上.html) (above) = 召し上がる (let's eat)  Very polite way to say, "let's eat" to a superior person. This sounds like you don't need it, but when you go to a restaurant, YOU, believe it or not, are a superior person, and so you'll need to know this word to understand what the waiter is saying to you.
