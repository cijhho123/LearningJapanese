## Jukugo

請求(せいきゅう) する or に |  claim, demand ★★☆☆☆   
[請](467-request-請.html) (request) + [求](725-demandask-求.html) (demand/ask) = 請求 (claim, demand)  Usually used in business. On-demand service. A bill for services. When used with に、 it means, "According to the customer's request," or "According to her wishes."
