## Jukugo

愚痴(ぐち) をこばす |  grumble ★★☆☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[愚](1382-dum-diddy-dum-dum-愚.html) (dum diddy dum dum) + [痴](1351-molester-痴.html) (molester) = 愚痴 (grumble)  grumble or mutter a complaint over and over - to bitch, whine, grouse, sing the blues. (as opposed to 文句 (もんく) which means make a loud and articulate complaint)  
---|---  
愚問(ぐもん) |  stupid question ☆☆☆☆☆   
[愚](1382-dum-diddy-dum-dum-愚.html) (dum diddy dum dum) + [問](163-question-problem-問.html) (question, problem) = 愚問 (stupid question)
