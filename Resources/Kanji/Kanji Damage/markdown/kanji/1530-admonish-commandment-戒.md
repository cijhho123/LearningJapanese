警戒(けいかい) する |  to be vigilant ☆☆☆☆☆   
[警](1420-the-fuzz-警.html) (the fuzz) + [戒](1530-admonish-commandment-戒.html) (admonish / commandment) = 警戒 (to be vigilant)  to be vigilant, to take precautions. To be on high alert - police or security. Describes a tense but temporary situation.
