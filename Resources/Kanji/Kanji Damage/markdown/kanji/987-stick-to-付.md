## Jukugo

気を付けて(きをつけて) |  be careful! ★★★★★   
[気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 気を付けて (be careful!)   
---|---  
見付ける(みつける) |  find ★★★★☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[見](520-look-見.html) (look) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 見付ける (find)  find (your car keys that you thought you'd lost)ー BOOBOO: you don't use 見つける about things you're discovering for the first time. That's 発見 (はっけん）!  
に 追い付く(おいつく) |  catch up to ★★★★☆   
[追](1616-follow-追.html) (follow) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 追い付く (catch up to)   
と 付き合う(つきあう) |  date, be in a relationship ★★★★☆   
[付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) + [合](936-to-suit-合.html) (to suit) = 付き合う (date, be in a relationship)  Usually used like 今、誰かつきあってる？ ('Are you seeing anyone now?') Ｓｔａｌｉｎと付き合ってる. ('I'm dating Stalin.')  
受付(うけつけ) |  reception ★★★☆☆ [NOKURI](../tags/43.html)   
[受](604-receive-受.html) (receive) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 受付 (reception)  the reception desk in an office.  
気付いた(きづいた) |  realize something ★★★☆☆   
[気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 気付いた (realize something)  to belatedly realize something - d'oh! (also pronounced 気がついた）  
片付ける(かたづける) |  clean up, tidy ★★★☆☆   
[片](1106-fragment-片.html) (fragment) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 片付ける (clean up, tidy)  clean up the room or desk. Not just sweep the floor but like straighten up all the papers, put the pens in a row, etc.  
勇気付ける(ゆうきづける) |  encourage ★★☆☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[勇](1328-courage-勇.html) (courage) + [気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 勇気付ける (encourage)  to encourage someone  
に or が 近付く(ちかづく) |  get close to destination ★★☆☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[近](328-near-近.html) (near) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 近付く (get close to destination)  to draw close to a physical destination or a goal.  
添付(てんぷ) する |  attach ★☆☆☆☆   
[添](881-append-添.html) (append) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 添付 (attach)  attatch (a file to your email) (read the attached instructions)  
錆付いた(さびついた) |  rusted / rusty ★☆☆☆☆ [KANA](../tags/12.html)   
[錆](911-rust-錆.html) (rust) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 錆付いた (rusted / rusty)

"Stick to" is the only kanji where the dot is on the inside, so you could think of it as a dot of GLUE making the left and right right radicals stick to each other.
