## Jukugo

強烈(きょうれつ) な |  intense ★★☆☆☆   
[強](897-burly-強.html) (burly) + [烈](1317-violently-intense-烈.html) (violently intense) = 強烈 (intense)  intense, furious : close to 激しい （はげしい）： a punch, a violent speech. Emphasizes the extent of emotion invested in it.  
---|---  
猛烈(もうれつ) な |  fierce ★★☆☆☆   
[猛](1454-fierce-猛.html) (fierce) + [烈](1317-violently-intense-烈.html) (violently intense) = 猛烈 (fierce )  severe, violent, terrible, fierce -  Unlike the related word 強烈（きょうれつ）、 もうれつ is usually used about natural phenomena: a fierce storm; a violent wild-fire. The toboggan moving at a scary intense velocity downhill. Also can be used with emotions: A violent argument after someone got caught cheating.  
熱烈(ねつれつ) な |  passionate enthusiasm ★☆☆☆☆   
[熱](1279-hot-thing-fever-熱.html) (hot thing / fever) + [烈](1317-violently-intense-烈.html) (violently intense) = 熱烈 (passionate enthusiasm)  fervent, passionate enthusiasm.
