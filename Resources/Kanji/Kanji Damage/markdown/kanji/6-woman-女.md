## Jukugo

彼女(かのじょ) |  her / girlfriend ★★★★★   
[彼](1326-him-彼.html) (him) + [女](6-woman-女.html) (woman) = 彼女 (her / girlfriend)  her. Might also mean 'girlfriend' depending on context.  
---|---  
女の人(おんなのひと) |  woman ★★★★★   
[女](6-woman-女.html) (woman) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 女の人 (woman)  the polite- but not formal - way to say woman  
女の子(おんなのこ) |  girl ★★★★★   
[女](6-woman-女.html) (woman) + [子](5-child-子.html) (child) = 女の子 (girl)  girl . Newspapers use it for a female up through junior high, but guys talking about 'chicks,dude' might use it to refer to a 20 year old.  
少女(しょうじょ) |  young girl ★★★★☆   
[少](193-a-little-amount-少.html) (a little (amount)) + [女](6-woman-女.html) (woman) = 少女 (young girl)  young girl - usually 5- 15 years old.  
女性(じょせい) |  female ★★★☆☆   
[女](6-woman-女.html) (woman) + [性](475-sex-essential-nature-性.html) (sex / essential nature) = 女性 (female)  female - emphasizes biology, (sex, rather than gender).  
女子(じょし) |  female xxx / xxx for girls ★★★☆☆ [PRE](../tags/35.html)   
[女](6-woman-女.html) (woman) + [子](5-child-子.html) (child) = 女子 (female xxx / xxx for girls)  PREFIX used about women from first grade through college. Used to refer to female students as a group: "女子 line up on the left side of the class, 男子 (danshi) line up on the right side." BOOBOO: You wouldn't say "She's a joshi."  
女子高生(じょしこうせい) |  high school girl. ★★★☆☆   
[女](6-woman-女.html)[子](5-child-子.html) (female xxx / xxx for girls) + [高](1639-tall-高.html) (tall) + [生](473-life-birth-生.html) (life / birth) = 女子高生 (high school girl.)
