## Kunyomi

から*い |  spicy hot, not fresh-from-the-oven hot (BOOBOO)   
★★★☆☆  
---|---  
つら＊い |  something is tremendously difficult or painful to do - (the life of a coal miner is hard!).  Not only does つらい have nothing to do with からい, but they both LOOK the same: 辛い、辛い. - so there's no way to tell which is which! (although usually つらい is written in hiragana, and からい is written with kanji ) [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
★★★☆☆  
  
## Jukugo
