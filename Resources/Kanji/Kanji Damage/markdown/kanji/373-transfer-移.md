## Kunyomi

( を or に ) うつ＊す |  broad meaning! A transfer or shift - as in, climate change, moving to a new house, transmitting a cold, yawns that are 'catching', fire spreading to the next building, or moving a conversation on to the next subject. These are all 移す！

移民(いみん) |  immigrant ★☆☆☆☆   
[移](373-transfer-移.html) (transfer) + [民](1165-folk-民.html) (folk) = 移民 (immigrant )  immigrant (only applies to people who became Japanese citizens and generally their families live here for generations)(i.e. not you)  Countries like America, UK, or Brazil are 移民国 （いみんこく- immigrant countries), built by immigrants. Japan, like most of the world, still thinks of citizenship as blood and DNA. So if you're coming from the USA, remember your open-minded point-of-view is a small minority!
