## Jukugo

医者(いしゃ) |  doctor ★★★☆☆   
[医](1047-doctor-医.html) (doctor) + [者](255-professional-者.html) (professional) = 医者 (doctor)  doctor  
---|---  
歯医者(はいしゃ) |  dentist ★★☆☆☆   
[歯](1032-tooth-歯.html) (tooth) + [医](1047-doctor-医.html)[者](255-professional-者.html) (doctor) = 歯医者 (dentist)   
医師(いし) |  doctor ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[医](1047-doctor-医.html) (doctor) + [師](1619-master-teacher-師.html) (master, teacher) = 医師 (doctor)  a more formal word for doctor -(you wouldn't say "I need to see the 医師!" - you'd say, "I need to see the 医者（いしゃ）！”）  
XXX 医(い) |  -ology ★☆☆☆☆ [SUF](../tags/37.html) a SUFFIX meaning, 'XXX branch of medicine -' in other words, 'XXXology'.  
医療(いりょう) |  medical treatment ★☆☆☆☆   
[医](1047-doctor-医.html) (doctor) + [療](1364-medical-therapy-療.html) (medical therapy) = 医療 (medical treatment)  medical treatment- the kind one gets in a hospital (x-rays, MRI, surgery, etc.)
