## Onyomi

OTSU |  You OUGHT TO be mad that Nihongo puts girls in second place.  Honestly, unless you're specializing in contract law, you won't need this ONyomi, but it was so obvious it practically wrote itself.

## Jukugo

乙女(おとめ) |  pure, virginal girl ★★☆☆☆ [FR](../tags/9.html)   
[乙](480-second-rank-girl-乙.html) (second rank / girl) + [女](6-woman-女.html) (woman) = 乙女 (pure, virginal girl)  a girl. Unlike the related term 少女, 乙女 emphasizes the "pure, virginal" aspect of girlhood - used about girls around 12-19 years old.
