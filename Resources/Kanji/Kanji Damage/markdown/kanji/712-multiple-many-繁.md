## Jukugo

頻繁(ひんぱん) に |  frequently ★☆☆☆☆ often, frequently  
---|---  
繁殖(はんしょく) |  breeding ☆☆☆☆☆   
[繁](712-multiple-many-繁.html) (multiple, many) + [殖](1313-agricultural-breeding-reproduction-殖.html) (agricultural breeding, reproduction) = 繁殖 (breeding)  breeding. Unlike the related term 生殖 （せいしょく）、繁殖 refers to the entire process of finding a mate, not just the physical act of insemination: Breeding grounds. Mating season. Farmers breeding livestock.
