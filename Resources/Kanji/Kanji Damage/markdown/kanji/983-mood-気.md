## Mnemonic

---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

き |  basically, 'mood', but there are over 3,000 expressions that use it. KI can be put in, become exhausted ,become lost, change, be pulled, be big, small, changing. KI gets followed, met, used, lightened, lengthened, shortened, rotated, cut. KI can be strong, weak, floating, heavy. . .. but you never say, "how's your ki doing ?" or "I've got great ki today!" - You have to use it as part of an expression. [CCW](../tags/4.html)

気違い(きちがい) |  fuckin' crazy ★★★☆☆ [ABU](../tags/1.html)   
[気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) + [違](1577-different-and-therefore-wrong-違.html) (different and therefore wrong) = 気違い (fuckin' crazy)  (literally, 'different feeling') crazy - this is one of the biggest cusses in the whole language-a garunteed fight-starter. This is instructive in several ways. 1) Japanese is such a polite language that 'different feeling' is one of the worst insults.  2) it takes a certain kind of culture to think that having a different feeling is such a bad thing in the first place!  3) Historically it is a discriminatory term against people who suffer from mental illness.  
気付いた(きづいた) |  realize something ★★★☆☆   
[気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) + [付](987-stick-to-付.html) (stick to) = 気付いた (realize something)  to belatedly realize something - d'oh! (also pronounced 気がついた）  
気晴らし(きばらし) に |  Just for fun ☆☆☆☆☆   
[気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) + [晴](470-weather-get-good-now-晴.html) (weather get good now) = 気晴らし (Just for fun)  Just for fun, do you wanna (blah blah blah)?"
