## Jukugo

先祖(せんぞ) |  one's family ancestors ★★☆☆☆   
[先](810-earlier-the-tip-先.html) (earlier/ the tip) + [祖](1492-ancestor-祖.html) (ancestor) = 先祖 (one's family ancestors)  one's own personal ancestors. Ancestor worship.  
---|---  
祖先(そせん) |  far-away ancestor ☆☆☆☆☆   
[祖](1492-ancestor-祖.html) (ancestor) + [先](810-earlier-the-tip-先.html) (earlier/ the tip) = 祖先 (far-away ancestor)  includes the meaning of 先祖, but it's more broad. 祖先 can also refer to ancestors dating back to monkeys, fish, germs, etc. "The two words/phyla of fish/languages share a common ancestor."
