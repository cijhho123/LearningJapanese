## Jukugo

本当(ほんとう) に |  for realz. ★★★★★   
[本](338-book-the-real-本.html) (book / the real) + [当](634-hit-the-target-当.html) (hit the target) = 本当 (for realz.)   
---|---  
適当(てきとう) な |  appropriate ★★★★☆ [JERK](../tags/11.html)   
[適](716-suitable-適.html) (suitable) + [当](634-hit-the-target-当.html) (hit the target) = 適当 (appropriate)  appropriate, as in an appropriate gift. However, 適当 also means irresponsible! Doing something without considering the consequences. 適当な人間だ！ “He's irresponsible. He'll say anything that pops into his head.”  
当然(とうぜん) の |  natural, obvious ★★★☆☆   
[当](634-hit-the-target-当.html) (hit the target) + [然](411-nature-然.html) (nature) = 当然 (natural, obvious)  just, natural, deserved - although it's often used like 'That's obvious, isn't it?'  
当日(とうじつ) に |  that day . . . ★★☆☆☆   
[当](634-hit-the-target-当.html) (hit the target) + [日](23-sun-day-日.html) (sun, day) = 当日 (that day . . .)
