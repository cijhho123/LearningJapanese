## Jukugo

に 期待(きたい) する or しよう |  expectations ★★★★☆   
[期](1706-period-of-time-期.html) (period of time) + [待](1236-wait-待.html) (wait) = 期待 (expectations)  Expectations. For example, 今回は負けたけど、次に期待しよう！ （こんかいはまけたけど、つぎにきたいしよう！） We lost this time, but I expect we'll win next time!   
Or, I expect that my students will give at least 33% effort.  
---|---  
待たせる(またせる) |  made you wait! ★★★☆☆ to make someone wait. The person at a cash register will generally say 'Taihenおまたせしました!' if you have to wait over 3 seconds.
