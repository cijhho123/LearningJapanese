## Jukugo

検査(けんさ) する |  checkup ★★★☆☆   
[検](416-investigate-検.html) (investigate) + [査](1488-inspect-査.html) (inspect) = 検査 (checkup)  Medical checkup. Also, investigation, inspection, review: (fiancial audit, smog check, check the condition of goods that roll off the assemby line, etc). Has the nuance of a test where something passes or fails.  
---|---  
調査(ちょうさ) する |  investigate ★★★☆☆   
[調](1636-check-out-調.html) (check out) + [査](1488-inspect-査.html) (inspect) = 調査 (investigate)  an investigation. Emphasizes that there are no correct answers, and implies that the investigators are talking to someone, asking questions directly. (usually used about polls, surveys, and detectives questioning suspects and/or witnesses)  
捜査(そうさ) |  criminal investigation ★★☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[捜](1370-search-捜.html) (search) + [査](1488-inspect-査.html) (inspect) = 捜査 (criminal investigation)   
を 査定(さてい) する |  assess ☆☆☆☆☆   
[査](1488-inspect-査.html) (inspect) + [定](282-plan-定.html) (plan) = 査定 (assess)
