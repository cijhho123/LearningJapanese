## Jukugo

延長(えんちょう) する |  prolonging ★★☆☆☆   
[延](1157-prolong-延.html) (prolong) + [長](905-long-boss-長.html) (long / boss) = 延長 (prolonging)  extension or prolonging, in time. Also means,to extend a linear thing by building it longer. A bit more official than the related term 伸ばす (のばす).  
---|---  
延期(えんき) する |  postpone ★★☆☆☆   
[延](1157-prolong-延.html) (prolong) + [期](1706-period-of-time-期.html) (period of time) = 延期 (postpone)   
生き延びる(いきのびる) |  outlive ★☆☆☆☆   
[生](473-life-birth-生.html) (life / birth) + [延](1157-prolong-延.html) (prolong) = 生き延びる (outlive)  to outlive others, to survive a fucked event
