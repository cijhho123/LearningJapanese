## Jukugo

ｘｘｘ 区(く) |  ward ★★★★☆ [SUF](../tags/37.html) SUFFIX meaning 'ward'- for example, 新宿区 (しんじゅくく) Shinjuku ward. (ward is a district of a city)  
---|---  
区別(くべつ) する |  distinguish ★★★☆☆   
[区](680-ward-区.html) (ward) + [別](1314-separate-別.html) (separate) = 区別 (distinguish)  to make a distinction - to tell one thing from another - 2 synonyms, for instance. or 2 similar-looking kanji  
区役所(くやくしょ) |  ward office ★★☆☆☆   
[区](680-ward-区.html) (ward) + [役](1293-role-役.html) (role) + [所](506-area-attribute-所.html) (area / attribute) = 区役所 (ward office)  the government office for your ward- where you get your immigration paperwork and junk.
