ただ*しい |  correct, in BOTH senses : literally , as in a correct answer. (Implies that there is ONLY ONE correct one). And figuratively: something that is morally correct or just.

## Jukugo

正直(しょうじき) な |  honest / loyal ★★★☆☆   
[正](280-correct-正.html) (correct) + [直](1308-correct-a-problem-direct-contact-直.html) (correct a problem / direct contact) = 正直 (honest / loyal)  very honest person, very loyal. Person with integrity. Also means, "To tell you the truth. . . xxxx"  
---|---  
正解(せいかい) |  correct answer ★★★☆☆   
[正](280-correct-正.html) (correct) + [解](1199-solve-untie-解.html) (solve / untie) = 正解 (correct answer)   
正確(せいかく) な |  precise ☆☆☆☆☆   
[正](280-correct-正.html) (correct) + [確](1057-make-certain-確.html) (make certain) = 正確 (precise)  accurate, precise (used about machines, not people)
