## Jukugo

担当(たんとう) する |  be responsible for ★★★☆☆   
[担](304-carry-on-your-back-担.html) (carry on your back) + [当](634-hit-the-target-当.html) (hit the target) = 担当 (be responsible for)  to be in charge of; to be responsible for  
---|---  
負担(ふたん) |  financial responsibility ☆☆☆☆☆   
[負](559-lose-負.html) (lose) + [担](304-carry-on-your-back-担.html) (carry on your back) = 負担 (financial responsibility)  a responsibility, usually financial, which is declared by a system. "If a guest falls off the hotel roof, it's the hotel's responsibility." "The parent is responsible for paying for the child's lunch at boarding school."
