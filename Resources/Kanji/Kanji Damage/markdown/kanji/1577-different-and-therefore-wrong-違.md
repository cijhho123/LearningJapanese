## Jukugo

間違い(まちがい) |  a mistake ★★★★★ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[間](162-a-period-of-time-間.html) (a period of time) + [違](1577-different-and-therefore-wrong-違.html) (different and therefore wrong) = 間違い (a mistake)  Verb form; 間違える！  
---|---  
勘違い(かんちがい) する |  misunderstanding ★★★☆☆   
[勘](1709-perception-勘.html) (perception) + [違](1577-different-and-therefore-wrong-違.html) (different and therefore wrong) = 勘違い (misunderstanding)  a misunderstanding - like the related term 誤解 , but 勘違い is a little smaller. If I'm reading and confuse one kanji with a similar looking kanji, that's 勘違い. But if I see my fiancee with her cousin from out of town, and assume she's having an affair and punch the guy, that's 誤解  
気違い(きちがい) |  fuckin' crazy ★★★☆☆ [ABU](../tags/1.html)   
[気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) + [違](1577-different-and-therefore-wrong-違.html) (different and therefore wrong) = 気違い (fuckin' crazy)  (literally, 'different feeling') crazy - this is one of the biggest cusses in the whole language-a garunteed fight-starter. This is instructive in several ways. 1) Japanese is such a polite language that 'different feeling' is one of the worst insults.  2) it takes a certain kind of culture to think that having a different feeling is such a bad thing in the first place!  3) Historically it is a discriminatory term against people who suffer from mental illness.  
相違(そうい) |  a somewhat formal word for difference ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[相](350-partner-相.html) (partner) + [違](1577-different-and-therefore-wrong-違.html) (different and therefore wrong) = 相違 (a somewhat formal word for difference)
