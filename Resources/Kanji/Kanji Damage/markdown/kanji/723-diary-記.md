## Jukugo

記録(きろく) |  record ★★★☆☆   
[記](723-diary-記.html) (diary) + [録](912-record-録.html) (record) = 記録 (record)  a record (like the minutes of a meeting or a log-file), but also a record like Guiness' Book. To break a record, like your Moms did at the Special Olympics, is 記録を破る （きろくをやぶる）.  
---|---  
日記(にっき) |  diary ★★★☆☆   
[日](23-sun-day-日.html) (sun, day) + [記](723-diary-記.html) (diary) = 日記 (diary)   
記者(きしゃ) |  reporter ★★☆☆☆   
[記](723-diary-記.html) (diary) + [者](255-professional-者.html) (professional) = 記者 (reporter)  reporter  
記念(きねん) |  memorial ★☆☆☆☆   
[記](723-diary-記.html) (diary) + [念](945-concern-念.html) (concern) = 記念 (memorial)  a memorial, an anniversary of something good , like marriage or a band's anniversary gig. Or something you give your friend to remember you by.
