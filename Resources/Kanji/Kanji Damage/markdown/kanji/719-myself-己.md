#  己 myself

★★☆☆☆

3 strokes

[PK](../tags/18.html)[STRONG](../tags/24.html)

Although the onyomi for this is KO, it is STRONG FOR 'KI', - which is to say, most of the kanji using this as a radical have the onyomi of KI for some reason! 

## Onyomi

KO |   
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  The sound of the word KO makes you think of kodomo(子), and 己 looks like a snake, so you could say:  I myself, am a baby snake.  
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

おのれ |  myself- a very LITERARY way of saying 私 [F](../tags/26.html)   
★★☆☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

自己紹介(じこしょうかい) |  introduce yourself to a group. ★★☆☆☆   
[自](78-my-own-自.html)[己](719-myself-己.html) (one's own) + [紹](210-acquaint-紹.html)[介](934-introduce-intervene-介.html) (introduce ) = 自己紹介 (introduce yourself to a group.)   
---|---  
自己(じこ) |  one's own ★★☆☆☆   
[自](78-my-own-自.html) (my own) + [己](719-myself-己.html) (myself) = 自己 (one's own)  One's own (as in, self-control, self-hate, self-help books, one's own discretion)
