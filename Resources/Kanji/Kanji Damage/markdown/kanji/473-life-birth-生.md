## Jukugo

先生(せんせい) |  teacher ★★★★★   
[先](810-earlier-the-tip-先.html) (earlier/ the tip) + [生](473-life-birth-生.html) (life / birth) = 先生 (teacher)   
---|---  
生活(せいかつ) |  lifestyle ★★★★★   
[生](473-life-birth-生.html) (life / birth) + [活](232-vivid-lively-活.html) (vivid, lively) = 生活 (lifestyle)   
学生(がくせい) |  student ★★★★★   
[学](1624-knowledge-学.html) (knowledge) + [生](473-life-birth-生.html) (life / birth) = 学生 (student)   
生(なま) XXX |  live and direct. ★★★☆☆ [PRE](../tags/35.html) this is hard. It got tons of meanings: nama tv is live, not prerecorded. Nama beer is poured straight from the bottle, not like a fancy mixed drink. Nama information is first hand. Nama meat is raw.  
人生(じんせい) |  life (in general) ★★★☆☆   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [生](473-life-birth-生.html) (life / birth) = 人生 (life (in general))  Life, as in someone's entire life, or life in general:. "He lived a quiet life." "Life is a bitch." "Life span."  
生き物(いきもの) |  living thing ★★☆☆☆   
[生](473-life-birth-生.html) (life / birth) + [物](1201-animal-thing-物.html) (animal / thing) = 生き物 (living thing)  living, moving thing . . . (BOOBOO：it doesn't apply to plants! Only little dudes that can move around and forage for food are いきもの)
