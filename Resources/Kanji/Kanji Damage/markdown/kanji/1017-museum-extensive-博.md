## Jukugo

博物館(はくぶつかん) |  museum ★★★☆☆   
[博](1017-museum-extensive-博.html) (museum /extensive) + [物](1201-animal-thing-物.html) (animal / thing) + [館](1614-big-hall-館.html) (big hall) = 博物館 (museum)  a natural history museum or aerospace museum. (fine-art museums are 美術館 （びじゅつかん））  
---|---  
博覧会(はくらんかい) |  exposition ★☆☆☆☆   
[博](1017-museum-extensive-博.html) (museum /extensive) + [覧](689-view-覧.html) (view) + [会](1269-big-meeting-会.html) (big meeting) = 博覧会 (exposition)  a GIANT exposition. Not like, "Cezanne's greatest fuckin' paintings all up in here festival" but huger - like a World's Fair. To break it down: 博覧会 (biggest kind of expo ) 展示会（てんじかい＝trade show) 展覧会 （てんらんかい＝art exhibition)
