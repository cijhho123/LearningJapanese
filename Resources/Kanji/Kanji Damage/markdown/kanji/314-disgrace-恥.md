#  恥 disgrace

[耳](309-ear-耳.html) (ear) \+ [心](98-heart-心.html) (heart) 

★★★★☆

10 strokes

## Onyomi

CHI |  I got embarrassed when I cut the CHEESE in front of Lemmy.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  When the ear hears the disgraceful story, the heart feels embarrassment.  
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

は*ずかしい |  embarrassed! (or, this being Japan, pretending to be embarrassed!)   
★★★★☆  
---|---  
はじ |  a disgrace! (usually used like 恥をかける） [NUBI](../tags/17.html)   
★☆☆☆☆  
  
## Jukugo

恥ずかしがり屋(はずかしがりや) |  someone who gets embarrassed easily. ★★★☆☆   
[恥](314-disgrace-恥.html) (disgrace) + [屋](1260-store-屋.html) (store) = 恥ずかしがり屋 (someone who gets embarrassed easily.)   
---|---  
恥をかく(はじをかく) |  embarrass one's self ★☆☆☆☆ to embarrass one's self. (You can't say this about yourself, in the manner of, "OH! I fucked up!" But you can say はじをかいた! to someone else, like a mother scolding a child who doesn't know it's impolite to yell "POOOPY POOOPY!")  
恥知らず(はじしらず) |  shameless ★☆☆☆☆   
[恥](314-disgrace-恥.html) (disgrace) + [知](1050-know-知.html) (know) = 恥知らず (shameless)  shameless (literally, "Not knowing the meaning of shame")
