#  越 go beyond

[走](286-run-走.html) (run) \+ [成](745-become-成.html) (become) 

★★★☆☆

12 strokes

[DUPE](../tags/6.html)[ILL PAIR](../tags/10.html)

Unlike the OTHER こす （超す） , which means go OVER (a number or limit), THIS 越す means go BEYOND - physically.
