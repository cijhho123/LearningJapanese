## Jukugo

軍隊(ぐんたい) |  army ★★★☆☆   
[軍](1068-army-軍.html) (army) + [隊](1228-troop-隊.html) (troop) = 軍隊 (army)  an army - but not necessarily the whole army. It could just be some troops.  
---|---  
自衛隊(じえいたい) |  Japan's army ★★☆☆☆   
[自](78-my-own-自.html)[衛](1579-defense-衛.html) (self-defense) + [隊](1228-troop-隊.html) (troop) = 自衛隊 (Japan's army)  Japan's army, in English called the SDF (self-defense forces)  
ｘｘｘ 隊(たい) |  -squad ★★☆☆☆ [SUF](../tags/37.html) SUFFIX； putting 隊 at the end of any word is like saying 'squad,': 'vice squad', 'turtle squad,' whatever.
