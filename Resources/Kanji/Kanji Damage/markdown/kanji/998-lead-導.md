## Jukugo

誘導(ゆうどう) する |  lead ★★☆☆☆   
[誘](622-invite-entice-誘.html) (invite / entice) + [導](998-lead-導.html) (lead) = 誘導 (lead)  to lead or conduct : A teacher leads the class on the field trip. The policewoman conducts traffic. The nuance is, 誘導 means literally going together with the people to the place.  
---|---  
導入(どうにゅう) |  introduce new concept ★☆☆☆☆   
[導](998-lead-導.html) (lead) + [入](60-putgo-in-入.html) (put/go in) = 導入 (introduce new concept)  to introduce - not a person but a new concept or thing. "Billy Fuckpants introduced tea to Europe from China."
