## Kunyomi

( を ) あ＊げる |  To metaphorically bring up something (an objection, a point) to give an example of , to cite or mention something. But also - irritatingly - to literally raise one's hand (Oo! Oo! I know ! I know!) However, if you're raising anything else BUT your hand, you have to use 上げる instead of 挙げる. Yeah, I know, I know. What can you do?? [NP](../tags/16.html)   
★★☆☆☆  
---|---  
( が ) あ＊がる |  something gets cited or mentioned in a report or article.   
★★☆☆☆
