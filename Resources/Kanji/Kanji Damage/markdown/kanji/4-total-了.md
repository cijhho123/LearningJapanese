## Jukugo

完了(かんりょう) する |  finish ★★★☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[完](572-complete-完.html) (complete) + [了](4-total-了.html) (total) = 完了 (finish)  to finish work on a project - an annual report, a re-formatting of the database, etc.  
---|---  
了解(りょうかい) |  OK, got it. ★★★☆☆   
[了](4-total-了.html) (total) + [解](1199-solve-untie-解.html) (solve / untie) = 了解 (OK, got it.)  roger that! ('I got it!') (originally used by military, but now often used when you text, because of its brevity)!  
終了(しゅうりょう) |  expire ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[終](955-end-終.html) (end) + [了](4-total-了.html) (total) = 終了 (expire)  to expire, to come to an end (used about contracts or subscriptions expiring)
