#  悔 regret

[![](../assets/radsmall/heartleft-042206e623366c5af4ceb06fc7c22311edc86e29e45f230326971c638d995d15.jpg)](98-heart-心.html) (leftheart) \+ [毎](484-every-毎.html) (every) 

★★★★☆

9 strokes

This isn't a moral regret- (that's more like 省みる (kaerimiru) or 後悔（koukai)). 悔しい is more like, you just regret that you didn't succeed in your evil schemes. 

## Onyomi

KAI |  Charlie Brown regrets the time his KITE got eaten!  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  I regret that I broke every heart I encountered  
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

くや＊しい |  this means, "Fuck! I almost had it and then it all went to hell GOD DAMN IT I WAS SO CLOSE!" [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
★★★★★  
---|---  
く＊い |  to succeed but still feel like you could have done better (used exclusiely in the expression: くい が のこる)   
☆☆☆☆☆  
  
## Jukugo

後悔(こうかい) する |  regret ★★★☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[後](1234-afterwards-behind-後.html) (afterwards / behind) + [悔](487-regret-悔.html) (regret) = 後悔 (regret)  the most general, broad, useful word for regret
