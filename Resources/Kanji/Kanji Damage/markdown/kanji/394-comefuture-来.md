This is used as a PREFIX, meaning next : (next year, month, week). 

HOWEVER, BOOBOO!!! It's NEVER used with tomorrow, or next hour, or next minute. Nonono. Sorry.

来月(らいげつ) |  next month ★★★★★   
[来](394-comefuture-来.html) (come/future) + [月](41-moon-organ-月.html) (moon) = 来月 (next month)   
は or が 出来る(できる) |  possible ★★★★★ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[出](1024-pull-out-hand-over-出.html) (pull out / hand over) + [来](394-comefuture-来.html) (come/future) = 出来る (possible)  it's possible to do.(I can run, I can shit, but I can't run and shit at the same time.)  
以来(いらい) |  since then ★★★☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[以](1721-compared-to-以.html) (compared to) + [来](394-comefuture-来.html) (come/future) = 以来 (since then)   
将来(しょうらい) |  one's future ★★★☆☆ a specific person's future (i.e. the dumb interview question, "What do you see yourself doing 10 years from now?") Also sometimes used with Japan's future.
