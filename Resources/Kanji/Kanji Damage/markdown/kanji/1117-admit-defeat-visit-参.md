## Kunyomi

( が or に ) まい＊る |  When you say まいた！！ it means, to say uncle, or !No mas! (Confusingly, it can _also_ mean 'I'm fed up!' 'Fuck this, I'm outta here.') However, there's another meaning: to visit an important place. （luckily, this JERK meaning is very formal and almost never used anymore)  What is really fucked up is, usually when a word has 2 meanings, you can tell which meaning it is by the particle. But with MAIRU, both meanings use NI. [NP](../tags/16.html)[1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)

## Jukugo

参加(さんか) する |  join ★★★☆☆   
[参](1117-admit-defeat-visit-参.html) (admit defeat / visit) + [加](176-add-加.html) (add) = 参加 (join)  to join a group, or participate in something  
---|---  
が 降参(こうさん) する |  surrender ★★☆☆☆   
[降](1571-descend-rainfall-get-out-of-vehicle-降.html) (descend /rainfall / get out of vehicle ) + [参](1117-admit-defeat-visit-参.html) (admit defeat / visit) = 降参 (surrender)  a casual word for surrender: to give up mid-game. A politician concedes defeat. To say uncle.  
に 参拝(さんぱい) する |  go to a temple to pray ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[参](1117-admit-defeat-visit-参.html) (admit defeat / visit) + [拝](1746-worship-拝.html) (worship) = 参拝 (go to a temple to pray)
