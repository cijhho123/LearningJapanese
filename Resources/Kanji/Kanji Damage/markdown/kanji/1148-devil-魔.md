## Jukugo

邪魔(じゃま) します |  interrupt ★★★★☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[邪](1712-heresy-邪.html) (heresy) + [魔](1148-devil-魔.html) (devil) = 邪魔 (interrupt)  to interrupt. Often heard in the phrase: おじゃまします!('May I come in?') Used with co-workers or friends who are busy. Not used when you go to a store or someone's house.  
---|---  
悪魔(あくま) |  devil ★★★☆☆   
[悪](113-bad-悪.html) (bad) + [魔](1148-devil-魔.html) (devil) = 悪魔 (devil)  [I am The Devil, do you understand?!?](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLTrGt2Yf6I&feature=related) (used only for the Christian devil, not native Japanese demons,which are 鬼。)  
魔法(まほう) |  witchcraft ★★★☆☆   
[魔](1148-devil-魔.html) (devil) + [法](1258-law-法.html) (law) = 魔法 (witchcraft )  witchcraft - Applies to anything - flying, shooting lightning, turning dudes into trout.  
魔女(まじょ) |  witch ★★★☆☆   
[魔](1148-devil-魔.html) (devil) + [女](6-woman-女.html) (woman) = 魔女 (witch)
