## Jukugo

証拠(しょうこ) |  physical evidence ★★☆☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)[NP](../tags/16.html)   
[証](284-proof-証.html) (proof) + [拠](1288-evidence-which-is-the-basis-for-a-judgment-拠.html) (evidence which is the basis for a judgment) = 証拠 (physical evidence)  physical evidence, such as a footprint, or "This green tap-water is evidence of pollution." Also this is the word one uses about evidence at trials, not 証.  
---|---  
根拠(こんきょ) |  grounds ★☆☆☆☆   
[根](781-root-根.html) (root) + [拠](1288-evidence-which-is-the-basis-for-a-judgment-拠.html) (evidence which is the basis for a judgment) = 根拠 (grounds)  a grounds or basis for doing something. The authority to do something.
