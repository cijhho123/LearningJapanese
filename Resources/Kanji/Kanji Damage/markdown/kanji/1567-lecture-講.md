## Jukugo

講演(こうえん) |  a non-political speech ★★☆☆☆   
[講](1567-lecture-講.html) (lecture) + [演](1020-performance-演.html) (performance) = 講演 (a non-political speech)  any public speech or talk given to a general audience. Authors doing a reading, dudes addressing a club, the graduation speaker at your graduation, are all doing 講演.
