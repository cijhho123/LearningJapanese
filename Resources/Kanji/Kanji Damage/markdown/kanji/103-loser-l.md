#  L loser

(left radical) 

[PK](../tags/18.html)[SYMBOLIC](../tags/25.html)

I'm calling it "loser" rather than other "L" words (llama, labia, Luftwaffe, etc.) for a reason! Most of the kanji which use "L" have something to do with loss and death.
