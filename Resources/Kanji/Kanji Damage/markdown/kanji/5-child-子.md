## Jukugo

子供(こども) |  child ★★★★★ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[子](5-child-子.html) (child) + [供](1551-follower-供.html) (follower) = 子供 (child)   
---|---  
親子(おやこ) |  meat and eggs ★★★★☆ [SARC](../tags/20.html)[KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[親](525-parentskindness-親.html) (parents/kindness) + [子](5-child-子.html) (child) = 親子 (meat and eggs)  parents and child together - also the rather cruel name for a traditional dish: boiled chicken served with scrambled eggs.  
調子(ちょうし) |  condition ★★★★☆   
[調](1636-check-out-調.html) (check out) + [子](5-child-子.html) (child) = 調子 (condition)  the condition something's in. Can be used with a living thing or object. Always used only with いい or 悪い. Usually emphaizes the LONG TERM condition. Although hungover people have been heard to remark, 調子が悪い！  
椅子(いす) |  chair ★★★☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)[1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)  
車椅子(くるまいす) |  wheelchair ★★☆☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)[NEO](../tags/15.html)   
[車](1058-car-車.html) (car) + [子](5-child-子.html) (chair) = 車椅子 (wheelchair)
