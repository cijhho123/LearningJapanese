## Kunyomi

え＊る |  get or obtain :Unlike the OTHER える(得る ), 獲る is used for hunting - the predator obtains prey. Also sometimes used about sports victories.   
★☆☆☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

獲得(かくとく) する |  acquire ★★☆☆☆   
[獲](1461-prey-on-get-獲.html) (prey on / get) + [得](1237-bargain-obtain-得.html) (bargain / obtain) = 獲得 (acquire)  to obtain. Very similar to 獲る （える） but 獲得 has the connotation of "obtain with great effort", or "FINALLY obtain." 獲る is more like, obtain something easy or small.  
---|---  
獲物(えもの) |  prey ★☆☆☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[獲](1461-prey-on-get-獲.html) (prey on / get) + [物](1201-animal-thing-物.html) (animal / thing) = 獲物 (prey)
