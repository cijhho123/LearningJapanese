## Jukugo

倉庫(そうこ) |  storage ★☆☆☆☆   
[倉](932-storage-倉.html) (storage) + [庫](1131-warehouse-庫.html) (warehouse) = 倉庫 (storage)  means "warehouse," but also "storage" (as in the English phrase, "It's in storage.") 倉庫 is a stand-alone building - a warehouse or shack in back.
