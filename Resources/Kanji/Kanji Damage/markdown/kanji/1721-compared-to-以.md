---|---  
以上(いじょう) |  more than / that's all ★★★★☆ [JERK](../tags/11.html)   
[以](1721-compared-to-以.html) (compared to) + [上](123-above-上.html) (above) = 以上 (more than / that's all)  ２ meanings. usually said when you finish ordering something (food, copies, condoms) ； 'That'll be all.' It also means, "on top of all that" or "more than" (used like xxx以上、or sometimes also 以上 のｘｘｘ）  
以前(いぜん) |  in the past ★★★☆☆   
[以](1721-compared-to-以.html) (compared to) + [前](865-before-前.html) (before) = 以前 (in the past)  previously, earlier, in the past
