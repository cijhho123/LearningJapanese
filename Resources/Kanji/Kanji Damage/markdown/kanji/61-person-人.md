人気(にんき) |  famous ★★★★☆   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [気](983-mood-気.html) (mood) = 人気 (famous)  famous - usually used like this: 人気 が ある！  
人間(にんげん) |  human ★★★★☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [間](162-a-period-of-time-間.html) (a period of time) = 人間 (human)   
人生(じんせい) |  life (in general) ★★★☆☆   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [生](473-life-birth-生.html) (life / birth) = 人生 (life (in general))  Life, as in someone's entire life, or life in general:. "He lived a quiet life." "Life is a bitch." "Life span."  
名人(めいじん) |  an expert ★★★☆☆   
[名](119-name-famous-名.html) (name, famous) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 名人 (an expert)   
大人(おとな) |  adult ★★★☆☆ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[大](397-big-大.html) (big) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 大人 (adult)  adult, as opposed to minor, not as opposed to 'with clothes on.'  
人口(じんこう) |  population ★★☆☆☆   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [口](9-mouth-small-box-radical-口.html) (mouth) = 人口 (population)   
目上の人(めうえ) のひと |  higher person ★☆☆☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) + [上](123-above-上.html) (above) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 目上の人 (higher person)  someone who out-ranks you - at work, school, or in society.  
人食い(ひとくい) xxx |  a man-eating XXX. ★☆☆☆☆   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [食](921-eat-食.html) (eat) = 人食い (a man-eating XXX.)
