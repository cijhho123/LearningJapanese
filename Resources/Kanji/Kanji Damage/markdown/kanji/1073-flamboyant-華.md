## Kunyomi

はな |  in theory, it means flower, but in practice no one ever uses it that way. If you use it twice in a row: 華々しい、(はなばなしい), it means gorgeous or flamboyant. . . so you can still see the roots of the flower (heh).. [NUBI](../tags/17.html)
