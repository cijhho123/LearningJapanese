bitter - Well, not just REGULAR bitter (which is 苦い （にがい）), but a really specific KIND of bitterness that only Japanese people can taste! 

In fact, my Japanese friends could only think of 3 cases where you'd use it: unripe fruit, too-strong green tea, or certain raw persimmons.

Shibui can also refer to a sort of sophisticated, cooly sexy older man, such as George Clooney or Keifer Sutherland.
