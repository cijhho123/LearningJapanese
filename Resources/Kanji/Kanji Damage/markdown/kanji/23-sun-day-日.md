#  日 sun, day

[口](9-mouth-small-box-radical-口.html) (mouth/small box radical) 

★★★★★

4 strokes

[PK](../tags/18.html)[SYMBOLIC](../tags/25.html)[COUNTER](../tags/38.html)[COCK](../tags/5.html)

As a radical, it usually means "bright". 

COCK: This kanji has not one but two ON-yomis, and as if that wasn't enough, most of the jukugo don't use either of them! This is by far the worst kanji when it comes to not following the rules of pronunciation. Luckily, most of the FP words you'll use every day, right from your first day in Noob Nihongo, so you'll learn 'em without even trying. 

Also, it means "Japan" - a sort of contraction of 日本.

今日(きょう) |  today ★★★★★ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[今](943-now-今.html) (now) + [日](23-sun-day-日.html) (sun, day) = 今日 (today)   
明日(あした) |  tomorrow ★★★★★ [FP](../tags/8.html)   
[明](43-bright-明.html) (bright) + [日](23-sun-day-日.html) (sun, day) = 明日 (tomorrow)   
ｘｘｘ 曜日(ようび) |  day of week ★★★★★ [KUN ON](../tags/36.html)[COUNTER](../tags/38.html)[SUF](../tags/37.html)   
[曜](644-day-of-the-week-曜.html) (day of the week) + [日](23-sun-day-日.html) (sun, day) = 曜日 (day of week)  Day of the week (BOOBOO； in practice, most Japanese omit the final '日'.So, 木曜日(もくようび） becomes simply 木曜 (もくよう）)  
日米(にちべい) |  east-and-west ★☆☆☆☆   
[日](23-sun-day-日.html) (sun, day) + [米](384-rice-america-米.html) (rice / America) = 日米 (east-and-west)  add 日, (which is short for 日本), to 米, (which somehow means "America"), and you have a word which means "East-and-West" or "East meets West." Usually seen on cultural exchange-related things.
