#  逆 opposite

[![](../assets/radsmall/moving-0e80c2bf34c8fb0abb4d80bddd87b84d2e0840852ee5f185818858a6f305b652.jpg)](327-motion.html) (motion) \+ [屯](1717-bent-dagger-屯.html) (bent dagger)

## Onyomi

GYAKU |  If you tell me I GOTTA COOL down, I'll do the opposite.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  You'll note that the bent dagger radical is bent to the left, rather than the right. Because this kanji means opposite!  So now we can say . . .  Whichever way you move,I'll move the opposite !  
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

( に ) さか*らう |  to counter-react, to do the opposite of what you're supposed   
★★★☆☆  
---|---  
  
## Jukugo

逆(ぎゃく) xxx |  opposite of xxx, or reacting against xxx ★★★★☆ [PRE](../tags/35.html) this is a prefix, meaning "opposite of"- you can put it in front of any word, the way you'd use "Bizarrrro"  
---|---  
逆さま(さかさま) のXXX |  upside-down ★★☆☆☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html) upside-down or inside-out xxx  
逆効果(ぎゃくこうか) |  the opposite effect ☆☆☆☆☆   
[逆](1720-opposite-逆.html) (opposite) + [効](1013-effective-効.html)[果](354-fruit-result-果.html) (an effect) = 逆効果 (the opposite effect )  the opposite effect from what you'd intended.
