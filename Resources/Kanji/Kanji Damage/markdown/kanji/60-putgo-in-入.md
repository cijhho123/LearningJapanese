## Jukugo

入り口(いりぐち) |  entrance ★★★★☆ [NOKURI](../tags/43.html)   
[入](60-putgo-in-入.html) (put/go in) + [口](9-mouth-small-box-radical-口.html) (mouth) = 入り口 (entrance)  entrance (often shortened to 入口）  
---|---  
に 入院(にゅういん) する |  check in hospital ★★★☆☆   
[入](60-putgo-in-入.html) (put/go in) + [院](788-institution-院.html) (institution) = 入院 (check in hospital)  check into the hospital - not for a simple exam, but like you have to stay there at least one night, you're too sick to leave.  
に 入学(にゅうがく) |  to start school at a new school ★★☆☆☆   
[入](60-putgo-in-入.html) (put/go in) + [学](1624-knowledge-学.html) (knowledge) = 入学 (to start school at a new school)   
入館料(にゅうかんりょう) |  admission fee ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[入](60-putgo-in-入.html) (put/go in) + [館](1614-big-hall-館.html) (big hall) + [料](1585-ingredients-fees-料.html) (ingredients / fees) = 入館料 (admission fee)
