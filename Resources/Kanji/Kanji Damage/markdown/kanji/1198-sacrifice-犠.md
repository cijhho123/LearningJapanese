[SIDEKICK](../tags/21.html)[ILL PAIR](../tags/10.html)

You like how this kanji and the previous kanji mean the same thing? And use the same left radical? And are only ever used in the same one word? 

What a waste! 

If I was you, I'd just study the word 犠牲 and forget about studying the individual kanji. 

Sort of reminds you of 喧嘩, doesn't it? Two kanji with the same left- radical that are only used in conjunction with one another.

## Jukugo

犠牲(ぎせい) |  a sacrifice ★☆☆☆☆   
[犠](1198-sacrifice-犠.html) (sacrifice) + [牲](1197-sacrificial-victim-牲.html) (sacrificial victim) = 犠牲 (a sacrifice)  a sacrifice - not only religion. You can sacrifice your happiness for someone else. If a bunch of people die in a train wreck, you can use 犠牲。  
---|---

[犠](1198-sacrifice-犠.html)[牲](1197-sacrificial-victim-牲.html) (a sacrifice) + [者](255-professional-者.html) (professional) = 犠牲者 ( martyr)  a victim, a martyr
