## Jukugo

人権(じんけん) |  human rights ★★★★☆   
[人](61-person-人.html) (person) + [権](1056-rights-権.html) (rights) = 人権 (human rights )   
---|---  
権利(けんり) |  the right to do ★★★☆☆   
[権](1056-rights-権.html) (rights) + [利](374-handy-利.html) (handy) = 権利 (the right to do)  the authority or right to do something  
権威(けんい) |  authority ★★☆☆☆   
[権](1056-rights-権.html) (rights) + [威](752-terrible-majesty-威.html) (terrible majesty) = 権威 (authority)  an authority one has based on power alone (parents, cops, etc.) rather than on rights (authority based on rights is 権利（けんり））  
権力(けんりょく) |  power ★☆☆☆☆   
[権](1056-rights-権.html) (rights) + [力](175-strong-力.html) (strong) = 権力 (power)  power (over other people)(as in boss, parent, governor, pope, etc.)
