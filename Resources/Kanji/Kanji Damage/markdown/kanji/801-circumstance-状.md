## Jukugo

状況(じょうきょう) |  context, condition ★★★★★   
[状](801-circumstance-状.html) (circumstance) + [況](528-condition-況.html) (condition) = 状況 (context, condition)  the state of affairs, the condition, the context: どういう状況でその言葉を使う？ (どういう じょうきょう で その ことぼあ を つかう？ = In what situation do you use that word?)  
---|---  
状態(じょうたい) |  state ★★★☆☆   
[状](801-circumstance-状.html) (circumstance) + [態](145-looks-like-態.html) (looks like) = 状態 (state)  the state or status of something: water is the liquid state of h20, but ice is the solid state. State of shock. Pristine state. Also can mean the state of affairs : good, bad, deadlocked, deteroriorating, improving.
