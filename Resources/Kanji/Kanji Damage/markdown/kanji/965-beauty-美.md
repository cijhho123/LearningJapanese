---|---  
美少年(びしょうねん) |  cute underage boy ★★★☆☆   
[美](965-beauty-美.html) (beauty) + [少](193-a-little-amount-少.html)[年](1572-year-年.html) (young boy) = 美少年 (cute underage boy)   
美容院(びよういん) |  beauty salon. ★★☆☆☆   
[美](965-beauty-美.html) (beauty) + [容](1521-appearance-容.html) (appearance) + [院](788-institution-院.html) (institution) = 美容院 (beauty salon.)  beauty salon. Yes, hospital （**びょう** いん） and beauty salon (び**よう** いん） DO sound totally alike.This is one of the basic mindfucks that teachers like to spring on newbies, so act like you know.
