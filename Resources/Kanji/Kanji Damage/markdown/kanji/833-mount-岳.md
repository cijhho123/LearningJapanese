#  岳 mount

[丘](317-hill-丘.html) (hill) \+ [山](823-mountain-山.html) (mountain) 

★★☆☆☆

8 strokes

This means the same as 山 but it is more used in Hokkaido, whereas 山 is more used in southern Japan. 

## Onyomi

GAKU |  It GOT COOL on top of Mt. Gaku this morning.
