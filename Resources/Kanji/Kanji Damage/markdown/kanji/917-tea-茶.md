## Jukugo

お茶(おちゃ) |  tea ★★★★★  
---|---  
茶色(ちゃいろ) |  brown ★★★★☆ [KUN ON](../tags/36.html)   
[茶](917-tea-茶.html) (tea) + [色](1210-color-色.html) (color) = 茶色 (brown)   
喫茶店(きっさてん) |  coffe-shop ★★★☆☆   
[喫](456-enjoy-a-drink-and-a-smoke-喫.html) (enjoy a drink and a smoke) + [茶](917-tea-茶.html) (tea) + [店](1132-shop-店.html) (shop) = 喫茶店 (coffe-shop)  a coffee-shop. These are really popular because Japanese don't invite people over to their homes very often - they meet at the coffee-shop. This is fairly polite example to point to when a Japanese asks you, "Do foreigners really think we're a cold people?" BOOBOO: most Japanese don't say きっさてん。 they just say 喫茶、(きっさ)。  
緑茶(りょくちゃ) |  green tea ★☆☆☆☆   
[緑](632-green-緑.html) (green) + [茶](917-tea-茶.html) (tea) = 緑茶 (green tea)  green tea (AKA the only time you'll ever use the on-yomi for 'green')  
紅茶(こうちゃ) |  black tea ☆☆☆☆☆   
[紅](647-dark-lipstick-red-color-紅.html) (dark lipstick red color) + [茶](917-tea-茶.html) (tea) = 紅茶 (black tea )
