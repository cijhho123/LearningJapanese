## Onyomi

BIN |  He's BEEN poor so long, he'll borrow some Kool-aid from you and then come back to borrow water.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  If you consider that one of the meanings of 分 is 'part', then you can say:   
They're so poor, they only got part of a dollar.
