## Kunyomi

あやま*る |  to make a mistake- Basically this is a more formal version of 間違える! Used by older folks instead of 間違える, but you can use it if you're talking about a miscalculation or if you took the wrong course of action/ a failed policy. [F](../tags/26.html)

## Jukugo

誤解(ごかい) する |  misunderstanding ★★★★☆   
[誤](1735-mistake-誤.html) (mistake) + [解](1199-solve-untie-解.html) (solve / untie) = 誤解 (misunderstanding)  a misunderstanding- often used about situations (a la TV sitcoms) or when you misread someone's feelings.  
---|---  
誤字(ごじ) |  tpo ★☆☆☆☆   
[誤](1735-mistake-誤.html) (mistake) + [字](129-letter-字.html) (letter) = 誤字 (tpo)  a typo
