## Jukugo

一泊(いっぱく) |  one night's stay ★☆☆☆☆ [COUNTER](../tags/38.html)   
[一](1-one-line-radical-一.html) (one) + [泊](38-stay-for-the-night-泊.html) (stay for the night) = 一泊 (one night's stay)  a COUNTER for the number of nights you stay. You use this when making a reservation at a hotel. Ippaku is , of course, one night.  
---|---  
二泊(にはく) |  to stay somewhere for two nights. ★☆☆☆☆   
[二](2-two-二.html) (two) + [泊](38-stay-for-the-night-泊.html) (stay for the night) = 二泊 (to stay somewhere for two nights.)   
に 宿泊(しゅくはく) する |  stay the night ☆☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[宿](817-lodge-at-宿.html) (lodge at) + [泊](38-stay-for-the-night-泊.html) (stay for the night) = 宿泊 (stay the night)  the act of staying the night -(kind of formal; you might read it in a hotel guidebook, but you wouldn't say "Let's 宿泊 over there, guys!"
