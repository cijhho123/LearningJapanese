## Jukugo

簡単(かんたん) な |  simple ★★★★☆   
[簡](323-simplicity-簡.html) (simplicity) + [単](596-merely-単.html) (merely) = 簡単 (simple)  simple or easy, but in a good way  
---|---  
単語(たんご) |  simple word ★★★★☆   
[単](596-merely-単.html) (merely) + [語](1574-language-語.html) (language) = 単語 (simple word)  Tango describes a SIMPLE Japanese word (note the 単 kanji) , whether the word is hiragana OR a single kanji . But compound words or long-ass words are NOT 'tango'.  
単位(たんい) |  unit ★☆☆☆☆   
[単](596-merely-単.html) (merely) + [位](153-rank-位.html) (rank) = 単位 (unit)  denomination or unit (college credit, but also centimeter or pound)  
単純(たんじゅん) な |  simple version ★☆☆☆☆   
[単](596-merely-単.html) (merely) + [純](1718-epitome-純.html) (epitome) = 単純 (simple version)  the simple kind of X as opposed to the complicated kind (not rude). Also, simple-minded, mouth-breathing person (pretty rude).
