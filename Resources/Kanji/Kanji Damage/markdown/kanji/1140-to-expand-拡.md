## Jukugo

拡張(かくちょう) |  enlarge ★☆☆☆☆   
[拡](1140-to-expand-拡.html) (to expand) + [張](906-stretch-張.html) (stretch) = 拡張 (enlarge)  to extend or physically enlarge a thing (an extended family, enlarge the factory).  
---|---  
拡大(かくだい) |  zoom in / escalate ★☆☆☆☆   
[拡](1140-to-expand-拡.html) (to expand) + [大](397-big-大.html) (big) = 拡大 (zoom in / escalate)  Literally it means to zoom in on or magnify. Figuratively it means to escalate (a conflict) or scale-up (a buildup of some sort)
