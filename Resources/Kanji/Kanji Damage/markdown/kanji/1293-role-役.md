## Jukugo

役に立つ(やくにたつ) |  to work like it's supposed to ★★★☆☆   
[役](1293-role-役.html) (role) + [立](148-stand-up-立.html) (stand up) = 役に立つ (to work like it's supposed to)  to be useful, to work like it's supposed to (used about things, not humans)  
---|---  
役割(やくわり) |  role ★★★☆☆ [KUNKUN](../tags/13.html)   
[役](1293-role-役.html) (role) + [割](463-divide-割.html) (divide) = 役割 (role)  role, but more general and figurative than just 役（やく）。 Gay dudes who are top or bottom - that's 役割。 If your role is 'the black sheep of the family," that's 役割。 The guy in the boy-band who is "the edgy one." The person on the reality -tv show who is "the crazy one." The verb is 果たす（はたす）, as in XXX の役割を果たす（ to play the role of XXX)  
役人(やくにん) |  bureaucrat ★★★☆☆ [ABU](../tags/1.html)   
[役](1293-role-役.html) (role) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 役人 (bureaucrat)  a petty bureaucrat ass-grower like at the DMV. These are almost as unpopular in Japan as they are in the West, so maybe don't ask someone "Are you a 役人?" to their face.
