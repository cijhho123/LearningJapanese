## Jukugo

国民(こくみん) |  people of a country ★★★☆☆   
[国](438-country-国.html) (country) + [民](1165-folk-民.html) (folk) = 国民 (people of a country)  the people, as in, "The Republicans are the People's party!"  
---|---  
民族(みんぞく) |  ethnic ★★★☆☆   
[民](1165-folk-民.html) (folk) + [族](1048-family-族.html) (family) = 民族 (ethnic)  two meanings: 1) ethnic group, 2) folk (as in folk customs, folk music, etc)  
市民(しみん) |  citizens of a city ★★☆☆☆   
[市](73-small-city-dagger-radical-市.html) (small city) + [民](1165-folk-民.html) (folk) = 市民 (citizens of a city)   
民主主義(みんしゅしゅぎ) |  democracy ★★☆☆☆   
[民](1165-folk-民.html) (folk) + [主](454-mastermainly-主.html) (master/mainly) + [主](454-mastermainly-主.html)[義](877-righteousness-義.html) (- ism) = 民主主義 (democracy )
