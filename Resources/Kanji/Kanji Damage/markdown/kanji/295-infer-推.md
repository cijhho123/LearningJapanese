## Jukugo

推測(すいそく) する |  speculate. ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[推](295-infer-推.html) (infer) + [測](548-scientific-measurement-測.html) (scientific measurement) = 推測 (speculate.)  speculate. Kind of formal: "It's too early to speculate about the economy." "Scientists speculate that..."  
---|---  
推定(すいてい) |  estimate ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[推](295-infer-推.html) (infer) + [定](282-plan-定.html) (plan) = 推定 (estimate)  a guess, an estimate (i.e. estimate the price of a repair)  
推理(すいり) |  deductive reasoning, inference. ☆☆☆☆☆   
[推](295-infer-推.html) (infer) + [理](445-reason-理.html) (reason) = 推理 (deductive reasoning, inference.)
