## Jukugo

露出狂(ろしゅつきょう) |  exhibitionist ★☆☆☆☆   
[露](1393-outdoors-public-露.html) (outdoors, public) + [出](1024-pull-out-hand-over-出.html) (pull out / hand over) + [狂](1458-go-nuts-狂.html) (go nuts) = 露出狂 (exhibitionist)   
---|---  
が 発狂(はっきょう) する |  mental breakdown ★☆☆☆☆   
[発](1186-launch-発.html) (launch) + [狂](1458-go-nuts-狂.html) (go nuts) = 発狂 (mental breakdown)  As opposed to 狂ってる (born crazy), 発狂 is someone who is normally sane but had a breakdown from stress, or a sudden flip-out.
