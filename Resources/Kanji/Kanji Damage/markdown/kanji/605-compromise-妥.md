## Jukugo

---|---  
妥当(だとう) な |  adequate ☆☆☆☆☆   
[妥](605-compromise-妥.html) (compromise) + [当](634-hit-the-target-当.html) (hit the target) = 妥当 (adequate)  means 'appropriate' - but more relaxed than related words like 適当 (てきとう) and 正しい (ただしい). . .妥当 is more like "adequate."
