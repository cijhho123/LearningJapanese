## Kunyomi

ち＊らす |  to scatter, but in a GOOD way - seeds, information, sprinkles on a cupcake (also sometimes means, to distract someone's attention, which seems a little arbitrary).   
★☆☆☆☆  
---|---  
ち＊らかす |  to scatter, in a negative sense: Yo Mama's thong is scattered all over the room! (and half of the adjoining room as well)   
★★★☆☆
