## Jukugo

情熱(じょうねつ) |  enthusiastic ★★☆☆☆   
[情](471-emotion-情.html) (emotion) + [熱](1279-hot-thing-fever-熱.html) (hot thing / fever) = 情熱 (enthusiastic)  passionate! Although pretty far from how we'd use that word in english: the Japanese version means "dedicated , hardworking, diligent." 情熱な学生 (a passionate student) . . perhaps a better translation is simply 'enthusiastic.'  
---|---  
熱心(ねっしん) な or に |  passionate ★☆☆☆☆   
[熱](1279-hot-thing-fever-熱.html) (hot thing / fever) + [心](98-heart-心.html) (heart) = 熱心 (passionate)  passion - usually used about sports or performances: They praised the actor's passionate performance. She's passionate about her hobby.  
熱帯(ねったい) |  tropics ★☆☆☆☆   
[熱](1279-hot-thing-fever-熱.html) (hot thing / fever) + [帯](825-belt-帯.html) (belt) = 熱帯 (tropics)  the tropics, tropical zone
