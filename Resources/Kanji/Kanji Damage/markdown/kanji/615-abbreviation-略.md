## Jukugo

省略(しょうりゃく) する |  abbreviate or leave out ★★★☆☆   
[省](196-ministry-cut-down-on-省.html) (ministry / cut down on) + [略](615-abbreviation-略.html) (abbreviation) = 省略 (abbreviate or leave out)  ｔｏ ａｂｂｒｅｖｉａｔｅ, omit, but also contraction. ( contractions like 'ain't' are 省略）  
---|---  
略語(りゃくご) |  abbreviation ★☆☆☆☆   
[略](615-abbreviation-略.html) (abbreviation) + [語](1574-language-語.html) (language) = 略語 (abbreviation)  abbreviation or acronym, like CIA or SHIELD, NWA, or KRS ONE, BDP, CREAM, etc.
