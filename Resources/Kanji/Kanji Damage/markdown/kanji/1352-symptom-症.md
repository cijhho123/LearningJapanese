## Jukugo

XXX症(XXXしょう) |  XXX - disease ★★★☆☆ [SUF](../tags/37.html) SUFFIX meaning XXX-disease. Only used in the proper names of specific diseases, in contrast to 病気（びょうき）, which means sick in general.  
---|---  
花粉症(かふんしょう) |  hay fever ★★☆☆☆   
[花](65-flower-花.html) (flower) + [粉](393-powder-粉.html) (powder) + [症](1352-symptom-症.html) (symptom) = 花粉症 (hay fever)   
XXX 恐怖症(きょうふしょう) |  xxx- phobia ★☆☆☆☆ [SUF](../tags/37.html)   
[恐](1291-dread-恐.html)[怖](1096-scary-怖.html) (super duper terror) + [症](1352-symptom-症.html) (symptom) = 恐怖症 (xxx- phobia)   
症状(しょうじょう) |  symptom ☆☆☆☆☆   
[症](1352-symptom-症.html) (symptom) + [状](801-circumstance-状.html) (circumstance) = 症状 (symptom)  symptom of a disease
