## Jukugo

お 返事(へんじ) する |  reply ★★★★☆   
[返](740-return-or-respond-返.html) (return or respond) + [事](1660-action-incident-事.html) (action/ incident) = 返事 (reply)  reply. In Japan, even if you respond to your friend's email 5 minutes after you got it, you have to start all mails with "Sorry my reply is so slow." (お返事が遅くてゴメン！） （おへんじ が おそくて ゴメン）  
---|---
