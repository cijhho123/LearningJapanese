## Kunyomi

たの*む |  to ask someone to do something for you. You can use this when ordering food at a restaraunt, but also when asking a favor from a friend. たのむ has the nuance that the person being asked can refuse. [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
★★★★☆  
---|---  
たの＊み |  a favor, a request.   
★★☆☆☆  
( に or を ) たよ＊る |  to trust or rely on someone: Say, you're robbing a bank and you get your friend to be the getaway driver. You have to count on him to actually be waiting when you get out of the bank.   
★★☆☆☆  
  
## Jukugo

依頼(いらい) する |  commission ★★☆☆☆   
[依](768-depend-on-依.html) (depend on) + [頼](1338-ask-a-favor-頼.html) (ask a favor) = 依頼 (commission)  a request or commission. Not like 'Please pass the salt,' but like 'I'd like to hire you to make a custom suit' or to hire a lawyer or PR firm. Any relationship where you'd use the phrase "The client wants. . ... "is 依頼。  
---|---  
依頼人(いらいにん) |  client ★☆☆☆☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[依](768-depend-on-依.html)[頼](1338-ask-a-favor-頼.html) (commission) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 依頼人 (client)   
信頼(しんらい) |  trust ☆☆☆☆☆   
[信](63-believe-信.html) (believe) + [頼](1338-ask-a-favor-頼.html) (ask a favor) = 信頼 (trust)
