---|---  
私達(わたしたち) |  us ★★★★☆ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[私](371-me-private-私.html) (me / private) + [達](332-pluraldelivery-達.html) (plural/delivery) = 私達 (us)   
配達(はいたつ) する |  deliver ★★★☆☆   
[配](724-distribute-配.html) (distribute) + [達](332-pluraldelivery-達.html) (plural/delivery) = 配達 (deliver)  to deliver. Used about business - used when money changes hands. Deliver by truck or UPS or FEDEX.  
XXX 達(たち) |  more than one of XXX. ★★★☆☆ [SUF](../tags/37.html) SUFFIX, meaning "more than one of XXX."But unlike English, you can't pluralize just any word.  In fact 90% of the time you'll see 達, it's in one of these 3 situations: 1) 仲間達：なかまたち： the group of friends. 2) 人達：ひとたち： people, and 3) 子供達：こどもたち： the children.  
xxxの 達人(たつじん) |  master of xxx ★★☆☆☆   
[達](332-pluraldelivery-達.html) (plural/delivery) + [人](61-person-人.html) (person) = 達人 (master of xxx)
