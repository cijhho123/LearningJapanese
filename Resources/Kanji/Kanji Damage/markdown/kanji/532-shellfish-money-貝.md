#  貝 shellfish/money

(bottom radical) [目](76-eye-目.html) (eye) \+ [![](../assets/radsmall/legsanimal-f47296d2b96e0a2e1a35f2aab69890c9d8933e6119690f625c0997710fd80265.jpg)](531-animal-legs.html) (animal legs) 

★★★★★

7 strokes

[SOLO](../tags/23.html)[JERK RADICAL](../tags/29.html)[SYMBOLIC](../tags/25.html)

JERK KANJI ALERT - by itself it means shellfish, but when combined with another kanji to form a compound word, it means "money". They say that shellfish used to be kind of like "wampum" before gold was discovered.
