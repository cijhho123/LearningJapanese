---|---  
は or が 出来る(できる) |  possible ★★★★★ [1/2 KANA](../tags/31.html)   
[出](1024-pull-out-hand-over-出.html) (pull out / hand over) + [来](394-comefuture-来.html) (come/future) = 出来る (possible)  it's possible to do.(I can run, I can shit, but I can't run and shit at the same time.)  
へ 出発(しゅっぱつ) |  departure ★★★★☆ [NP](../tags/16.html)   
[出](1024-pull-out-hand-over-出.html) (pull out / hand over) + [発](1186-launch-発.html) (launch) = 出発 (departure)  departure of train, bus, plane.  
xxxの 出身(しゅっしん) |  a thing from XXX ★★★★☆ [SUF](../tags/37.html)   
[出](1024-pull-out-hand-over-出.html) (pull out / hand over) + [身](1595-ones-own-flesh-身.html) ((one's own) flesh) = 出身 (a thing from XXX)  a thing made in XXX, a person from XXX。  
引き出し(ひきだし) |  drawer ★★☆☆☆
