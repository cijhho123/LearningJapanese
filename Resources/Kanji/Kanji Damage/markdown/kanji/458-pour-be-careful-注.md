## Jukugo

に 注意(ちゅうい) する |  be careful! ★★★★☆   
[注](458-pour-be-careful-注.html) (pour / be careful) + [意](157-meaning-意.html) (meaning) = 注意 (be careful!)  be careful! caution! (when used as a 'suru' verb, it means, 'to warn')  
---|---  
注文(ちゅうもん) する |  order ★★★☆☆   
[注](458-pour-be-careful-注.html) (pour / be careful) + [文](92-culture-sentence-文.html) (culture/ sentence) = 注文 (order)  to order an item - both in a store and by mail. Also, if you ask your friend to do an annoying and detailed favor, like, "Could you do the laundry again with this other kind of soap!", that's chuumon.
