#  僧 buddhist monk

[![](../assets/radsmall/man-d0fa8d3e87b0dcd06a7777a6693f057bfe7d041f88edfa20c6663c61cf324435.jpg)](61-person-人.html) (personleft) \+ [曽](1604-gain-曽.html) (gain) [Same-ON](../tags/63.html)

This is a somewhat formal kanji. Most people just say お坊さん （おぼうさん） for monk in casual conversation. 

## Onyomi

SOU |  The monk is SO wise.  
---|---  
  
## Mnemonic

|  A monk is a person who gains enlightenment  
---|---  
  
## Kunyomi

そう |  a somewhat formal, official way to refer to a monk [LAZY](../tags/14.html)[F](../tags/26.html)   
☆☆☆☆☆  
---|---
