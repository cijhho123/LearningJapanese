#  ![](../assets/radsmall/fingerprint-f2fc77d4b69bc59b5a9ea5e1de1994b89b9e238a273bd392bbcdd987687bbf34.jpg) fingerprint

(right radical) 

[PK](../tags/18.html)[SYMBOLIC](../tags/25.html)

It looks like the whorl of one's fingerprint, and generally means "proof of identity." 

In the west we use fingerprints, in the East they use little "chop" stamps with the family name on it. 

This radical is SYMBOLIC for stamp or seal or hand.
