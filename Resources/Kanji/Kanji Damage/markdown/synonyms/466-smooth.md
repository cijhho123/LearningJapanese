##  smooth

[巧](../kanji/650-adroit-巧.html)みな(たくみな) |  Smooth, slick, and usually bad or cunning in some way:a clever ad slogan, slick flattery, adroit political manipulation.   
---|---  
[巧](../kanji/650-adroit-巧.html)み(たくみに) |  Physical OR mental finesse:a PR man finesses a hard question with a bullshit answer so slick that the reporters don’t realize they got hoodwinked. A boxer with fancy footwork. Also, to do something deftly (a movie deftly interweaves 3 stories:a teenager deftly juggles church life and gang membership, etc.)   
[器](../kanji/403-instrument-器.html)[用](../kanji/224-utilize-用.html)な(きような) |  Adroit, dexterous. This is more positive than 巧み. 器用な means someone like a kung-fu fighter that can jump around and not get kicked, or a pianist who plays hella difficult pieces without any fucked notes. There is also an implication that the person can improvise! [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
[巧](../kanji/650-adroit-巧.html)[妙](../kanji/195-odd-妙.html)な(こうみょうな) |  Used about speaking – the way a hustler or a grifter talks. Slick talking.   
[素](../kanji/464-element-素.html)[早](../kanji/29-early-早.html)い(すばやい) |  Physical speed or agility. Without the emphasis on technique or skill found in 巧み or 器用. 素早い is a fast bird or lizard. Doing a simple task very fast at the office. [$$$](../tags/75.html)

俊[敏](../kanji/705-agile-sensitive-敏.html)な(しゅんびんな) |  Agile, quick-witted. Very similar to 機敏、but with the added implication of adaptable. Something that can adapt to changes in the environment.
