##  small town or village

[里](../kanji/236-village-里.html)(さと) |  Small villages in general, as in “Country life is more peaceful than city life.” Also has the nuance of being the village where you are from. Be careful, though:If you say your wife or girlfriend went back to the 里、people think you mean she broke up with you!
