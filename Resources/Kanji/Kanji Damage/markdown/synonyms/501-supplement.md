##  supplement

[補](../kanji/1563-supplement-補.html)う(おぎなう) |  To supplement – like 補充, but the nuance is that you are filling a vacancy. For instance, nutritional supplement. Substitute teacher. Assist in the completion of something (I helped write the speech). To make up for (i.e. Mitsubishi bank loaned money to Toyota to make up for their shortfall =みつびし銀行はトヨタの赤字補いました。) [$$$](../tags/75.html)[TTT](../tags/79.html)
