##  machine

[機](../kanji/675-machine-機.html)[械](../kanji/1531-contraption-械.html)(きかい) |  A machine, particularly something with a motor in it which moves when activated. A computer is not a 機会, but a robot arm is. Emphasis is that the 機械 is one single big thing.   
---|---  
[器](../kanji/403-instrument-器.html)[械](../kanji/1531-contraption-械.html)(きかい) |  More like the English word ‘equipment.’ Somewhat simpler or smaller than the other 機械 – and also more likely to come in a set than 機械. Examples of 器械 are:exercise equipment, scientific measuring devices, or those weird metal boxes you see on tops of mountains that measure earthquakes or weather. ZZZ   
そうち |  Devices which are not particularly hi-tech and do not have moving parts: fire extinguishers, air-bags, defilibrators.   
[器](../kanji/403-instrument-器.html)[具](../kanji/570-tool-具.html)(きぐ) |  An appliance:anything from chain-saws to gas stoves to birth control devices like IUDs can be called 器具. Honestly, it’s really arbitrary!   
[道](../kanji/861-street-道.html)[具](../kanji/570-tool-具.html)(どうぐ) |  A tool which has no moving parts:fork, shovel, hammer, scissors.
