##  social status

[身](../kanji/1595-ones-own-flesh-身.html)[分](../kanji/169-understand-divide-minute-分.html)(みぶん) |  Social status – from kings to peasants and slaves. One’s status never changes in Japanese society – it is with you no matter what you do with your life.   
---|---  
[分](../kanji/169-understand-divide-minute-分.html)[際](../kanji/1181-edge-際.html)(ぶんざい) |  Kind of a prejudice hate-speech word. Means “your inferior status.” As in, “Don’t forget your place!” Usually said like this:xxxの分際でそんなこと何で言うんだよ！ （”How dare you talk to your betters like that?”)- but you don’t have to be a stuffy headmaster or wizened dowager to use 分際. You could be a neighborhood bully or something. 分際 doesn’t mean just social class – it could refer to someone’s place in any pecking order.   
[階](../kanji/786-story-of-a-building-階.html)[級](../kanji/627-level-級.html)(かいきゅう) |  Means the same as 身分 but is generally understood to refer to foreign societies. Unlike 身分、 you can change your 階級 with luck or hard work.
