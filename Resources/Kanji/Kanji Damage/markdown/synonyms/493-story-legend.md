##  story, legend

[逸](../kanji/1212-more-or-less-than-normal-逸.html)[話](../kanji/231-conversation-話.html)(いつわ) |  Anecdote   
---|---  
[昔](../kanji/1543-long-ago-昔.html)[話](../kanji/231-conversation-話.html)(むかしばなし) |  Old (over 1000 years!) story – but not about gods, (that’s 神話） Think of Beowulf or the Icelandic epics.   
[物](../kanji/1201-animal-thing-物.html)[語](../kanji/1574-language-語.html)(ものがたり) |  A tale- often used in the title of a book or movie. As in ‘The Tale Of Genji’ or ‘The Tale Of Two Cities.’   
[童](../kanji/239-kid-童.html)[話](../kanji/231-conversation-話.html)(どうわ) |  Children’s story   
[伝](../kanji/1267-transmit-伝.html)[説](../kanji/863-explain-説.html)(でんせつ) |  A legend – King Arthur, Robin Hood, etc.   
[神](../kanji/1498-god-kanji-神.html)[話](../kanji/231-conversation-話.html)(しんわ) |  Myth (literally ‘god story’)   
[小](../kanji/192-small-size-小.html)[説](../kanji/863-explain-説.html)(しょうせつ) |  A novel   
[語](../kanji/1574-language-語.html)り(かたり) |  Narration   
メルヘン |  A German word, referring to Grimm’s Fairy tales and the like.
