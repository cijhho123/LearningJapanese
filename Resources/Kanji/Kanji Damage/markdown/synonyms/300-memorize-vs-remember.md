##  memorize vs. remember

[覚](../kanji/1625-bear-in-mind-覚.html)える(おぼえる) |  To put something new into your memory. Like “Learn 10 kanji a day.”   
---|---  
[思](../kanji/102-think-思.html)[出](../kanji/1024-pull-out-hand-over-出.html)す(おもいだす) |  To retrieve something which you learned before. “I remember the last time I saw him – in 1985 on a beach.”
