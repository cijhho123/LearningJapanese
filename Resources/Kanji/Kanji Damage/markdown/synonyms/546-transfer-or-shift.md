##  transfer or shift

[移](../kanji/373-transfer-移.html)す(うつす) |  Broad meaning! A shift – as in, climate change, moving to a new house, transmitting a cold, yawns that are ‘catching’, fire spreading to the next building, or move a conversation on to the next subject. These are all 移す！
