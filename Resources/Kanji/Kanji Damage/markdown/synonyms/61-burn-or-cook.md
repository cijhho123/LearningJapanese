##  burn or cook

[焼](../kanji/522-roast-grill-焼.html)く(やく) |  To roast food, but sometimes used with other stuff, like cross-burning or flag-burning, or even CD-burning.   
---|---  
[焦](../kanji/263-char-scorch-焦.html)がす(こがす) |  To burn to a crisp – to scorch – whether intentionally (flame-thrower!) or unintentionally (bad cook). Also, often used with hot, passionate emotions:burning with the fiiiire of looooooveeeee!!!!!!!!!!!!   
[燃](../kanji/412-burn-燃.html)える(もえる) |  Incinerate, destroy with fire. You’ll often see signs on garbage cans for 燃えるごみ!
