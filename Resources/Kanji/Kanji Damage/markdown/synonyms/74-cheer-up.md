##  cheer up

[勇](../kanji/1328-courage-勇.html)[気](../kanji/983-mood-気.html)[付](../kanji/987-stick-to-付.html)ける(ゆうきづける) |  To encourage someone. Basically the same as in English – although 勇気付ける does NOT have the nuance of “pressure” that “encourage” has. Like, if a father “encourages” his son to become a lawyer instead of a jockey, you wouldn’t use 勇気付ける。

[慰](../kanji/996-console-慰.html)める(なぐさめる) |  To console someone – not used so much about people who are depressed or hate life in general, more about someone who lost a game or a contest.
