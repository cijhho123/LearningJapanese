##  disaster

[災](../kanji/1733-disaster-災.html)[害](../kanji/462-damage-害.html)(さいがい) |  A TV news-worthy disaster:tornado, tsunami, earthquake.   
---|---  
[災](../kanji/1733-disaster-災.html)い(わざわい) |  A personal misfortune:house burned down, father had a stroke.   
[災](../kanji/1733-disaster-災.html)[難](../kanji/422-difficult-難.html)(さいなん) |  This means disaster in the sense of ‘How was your trip to Tahiti?’ ‘Oh my god, it was a total disaster!!! First we missed the plane, then I got an infection, then on top of all that our hotel was run by zombies!’ 災難 carries the sense of a bunch of random unconnected misfortunes. It never rains, but it pours, kind of thing.
