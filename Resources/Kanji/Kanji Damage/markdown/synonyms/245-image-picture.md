##  image, picture

[映](../kanji/406-project-reflect-映.html)[像](../kanji/1221-statue-image-像.html)(えいぞう) |  Any reflected or moving image, including TV or a computer screen, a shadow-puppet, etc.   
---|---  
[画](../kanji/1039-a-drawing-画.html)[像](../kanji/1221-statue-image-像.html)(がぞう) |  Used to mean “portrait” but now means an (unmoving) image – on a computer screen. Basically it means .jpgs!   
[絵](../kanji/1270-picture-絵.html)(え) |  An unmoving image on paper – a painting or drawing.   
[像](../kanji/1221-statue-image-像.html)(ぞう) |  A sculpture. (Although in practice, it’s much more common to use the French オブジェ)!
