##  joint

[節](../kanji/1473-season-joint-節.html)(ふし) |  Can be used about the knuckle, but mostly it refers to INANIMATE joints. That might seem like a contradiction, but consider the “knuckles” on a bamboo plant, or knots on a tree.

[関](../kanji/887-connected-to-関.html)[節](../kanji/1473-season-joint-節.html)(かんせつ) |  Regular human or machine joints i.e. moving joints.
