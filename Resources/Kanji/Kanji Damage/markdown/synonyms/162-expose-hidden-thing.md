##  expose hidden thing

[暴](../kanji/1558-rampage-暴.html)く(あばく) |  Expose some bad thing in public – like a journalist or watchdog group would.   
---|---  
ばれる |  To give away one’s own lie unintentionally. Like, saying “I’d never cheat on you, baby.” but you have lipstick on your collar. ばれた!!!   
[摘](../kanji/718-pluck-摘.html)[発](../kanji/1186-launch-発.html)(てきはつ) |  A legal/police term for exposing wrongdoing. But not necessarily making it public, like 暴く. ‘The investigation exposed corruption in the parking department.”   
[指](../kanji/299-finger-point-at-指.html)[摘](../kanji/718-pluck-摘.html)(してき) |  To point out a shortcoming – You can use it about your friends. Or “The critic pointed out the shortcomings of the play.”
