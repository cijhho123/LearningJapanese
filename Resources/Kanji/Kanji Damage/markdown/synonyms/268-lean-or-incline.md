##  lean or incline

[傾](../kanji/851-lean-傾.html)く(かたむく) |  To lean, as in “Leaning Tower of Pizza,” but also to lean ON, as in, “The drunk leaned on the wall for support.” Can be used literally OR figuratively.   
---|---  
[偏](../kanji/970-be-inclined-偏.html)る(かたよる) |  Figuratively, to be biased. Literally, to have an asymmetrical distribution of objects. For example, “There are crows in every prefecture, but there is a much higher distribution of them in Aichi prefecture.” Or, aesthetically speaking, “I put all the chairs on the left side of the room to make it かたよる.”   
[傾](../kanji/851-lean-傾.html)[向](../kanji/1640-turn-to-face-向.html)(けいこう) |  A general tendency or trend (not one specific person, and not with any intention) (i.e. people who watch 8 hours of TV a day tend to drool) 傾向 is NOT a fashion trend – that’s 流行. [$$$](../tags/75.html)
