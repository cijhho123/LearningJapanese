---|---  
[見](../kanji/520-look-見.html)[方](../kanji/84-direction-method-person-方.html)(みかた) |  Literally, “a way of looking at things.” Pretty much the same as 立場、 but 見方 seems to mostly be used in metaphors or explanations, whereas 立場 is more used in arguments, with a sort of right/wrong angle to it.
