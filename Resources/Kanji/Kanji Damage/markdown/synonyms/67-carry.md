##  carry

[運](../kanji/1071-carry-luck-運.html)ぶ(はこぶ) |  To carry – with an emphasis on the fact that you’re transporting something. If you pick up your suitcase and take it to the airport, that’s 運ぶ。   
---|---  
[担](../kanji/304-carry-on-your-back-担.html)う(になう) |  To carry a heavy load – unlike 運ぶ、担う emphasizes the heaviness. Can also be used figuratively – as in the English phrase “She bears the burden of caring for her retarded sister.” [NP](../tags/16.html)  
[担](../kanji/304-carry-on-your-back-担.html)ぐ(かつぐ) |  ”carry specifically on your shoulders” – like a backpack or a rifle.
