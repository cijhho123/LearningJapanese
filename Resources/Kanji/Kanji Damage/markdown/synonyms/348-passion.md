##  passion

[情](../kanji/471-emotion-情.html)[熱](../kanji/1279-hot-thing-fever-熱.html)(じょうねつ) |  Passionate! Although pretty far from how we’d use that word in English:the Japanese version means “dedicated, hardworking, diligent.” Example: 彼女は熱心な学生 = She’s a passionate student.   
---|---  
[熱](../kanji/1279-hot-thing-fever-熱.html)[心](../kanji/98-heart-心.html)(ねっしん) |  Passion – usually used about sports or performances:They praised the actor’s passionate performance. She’s passionate about her hobby.   
[恋](../kanji/903-passion-恋.html)(こい) |  Romantic passion.
