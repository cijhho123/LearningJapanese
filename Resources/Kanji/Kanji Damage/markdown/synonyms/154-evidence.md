##  evidence

[証](../kanji/284-proof-証.html)[拠](../kanji/1288-evidence-which-is-the-basis-for-a-judgment-拠.html)(しょうこ) |  Physical evidence, such as a footprint, or, “This green tap-water is evidence of pollution.” Also, this is the word one uses about evidence at trials, not 証.   
---|---  
[証](../kanji/284-proof-証.html)[明](../kanji/43-bright-明.html)(しょうめい) |  Proof, mathematical or logical.   
[証](../kanji/284-proof-証.html)(あかし) |  Somewhere in between 証明 and 証拠. You’d typically use 証 when you’re talking about evidence of God, such as miracles. Or you’d challenge someone:“You say you’re honest and give to charity, but do you have any evidence of this?” Unlike 証拠、 証 does not have to be physical, it can be verbal. [NP](../tags/16.html)  
[証](../kanji/284-proof-証.html)[言](../kanji/11-say-言.html)(しょうげん) |  Verbal testimony given in court
