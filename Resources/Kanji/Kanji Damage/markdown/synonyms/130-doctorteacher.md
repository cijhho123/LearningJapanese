##  doctor/teacher

[医](../kanji/1047-doctor-医.html)[者](../kanji/255-professional-者.html)(いしゃ) |  Medical doctor – normal version   
---|---  
[医](../kanji/1047-doctor-医.html)[師](../kanji/1619-master-teacher-師.html)(いし) |  Medical doctor – formal version. Used in newspapers and documents. You wouldn’t say “I just got hit by a scooter! Call the 医師!”   
[先](../kanji/810-earlier-the-tip-先.html)[生](../kanji/473-life-birth-生.html)(せんせい) |  Teacher, but also any upper-class smart professional:doctor, lawyer, philosopher, etc.   
[教](../kanji/710-teach-教.html)[師](../kanji/1619-master-teacher-師.html)(きょうし) |  A more formal word for teacher (and, unlike 先生, this ONLY refers to teachers).
