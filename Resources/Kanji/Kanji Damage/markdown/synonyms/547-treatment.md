##  treatment

[扱](../kanji/628-treatment-扱.html)う(あつかう) |  Treatment – usually applied to people, but originally used for objects, so it ‘s a little rude. Usually used like あつかいがいい or あつかいがわるい. Can mean ‘treatment of others’ but, confusingly, can ALSO mean ‘easy or hard for ME to DEAL WITH HIM/HER.’ For example, 彼は扱いが難しい does NOT mean ‘he treats me difficultly’, it means ‘He’s difficult to deal with.’
