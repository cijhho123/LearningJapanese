##  husband

[夫](../kanji/588-husband-夫.html)(おっと) |  Husband   
---|---  
[旦](../kanji/30-danna-husband-旦.html)那(だんな) |  Husband   
  
Both words mean “husband” and both are used on formal as well as informal occasions. But 夫 is used in documents, while 旦那 is more used in conversation. The female equivalent of 夫 is 妻 (つま). The female equivalent of 旦那さん is 奥さん (おくさん).
