##  self aware, self conscious

[意](../kanji/157-meaning-意.html)[識](../kanji/679-be-conscious-識.html)(いしき) |  Consciousness, as in “I lost consciousness,” but also as in, “She’s environmentally conscious.” or “I hate those socially-conscious rock stars like Bono. Bono should perish.” If you want to talk about a magic lamp or magic shoe-horn that is alive and has intelligence, use 意識.   
---|---  
てれる |  Self-conscious in the awkward teenage sense.   
[自](../kanji/78-my-own-自.html)[覚](../kanji/1625-bear-in-mind-覚.html)(じかくする) |  A more philosophical word than 意識. 自覚 is more like self-aware or enlightened. Or if you say 自覚的 it means subjective (as opposed to objective). [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
  
It seems like you can use both 意識 and 自覚 in the negative, to mean “did xxx without thinking”.
