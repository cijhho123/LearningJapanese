##  on the other hand

[反](../kanji/739-anti-反.html)[面](../kanji/813-front-surface-face-面.html)、xxx(そのはんめん、xxx) |  On the other hand, xxx.   
---|---  
[一](../kanji/1-one-line-radical-一.html)[方](../kanji/84-direction-method-person-方.html)(いっぽう) |  On the one hand.   
[他](../kanji/1602-other-他.html)[方](../kanji/84-direction-method-person-方.html)で yyy(たほうで) |  On the other hand.   
  
These two expression are typically used as a set – the same way the English expressions “On the one hand / on the other hand” are used:On the one hand, I’d like to assassinate Phil Collins, but on the other hand, I don’t want to get blood on me.
