##  accompany

[伴](../kanji/190-accompany-伴.html)う(ともなう) |  Accompany, both literal and figurative: Bill accompanies Doug. But also, rain accompanies high winds at this time of year. (Notice how it doesn’t care about cause and effect.)   
---|---  
[同](../kanji/974-same-同.html)[伴](../kanji/190-accompany-伴.html)する(どうはん する) |  A person accompanies another person.   
[添](../kanji/881-append-添.html)える(そえる) |  To come with, to be included with:(not used with people – in that case you’d say 伴）添える is more like, the CDR comes with the textbook. Or the applesauce comes with the pork chops.
