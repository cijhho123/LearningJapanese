##  pipe, tube

[筒](../kanji/976-cylinder-pipe-筒.html)(つつ) |  Usually defined as pipe but more accurately, it means cylinder.- chimney, exhaust pipe, drainage pipe, gun barrel, canister.   
---|---  
[管](../kanji/1618-tube-管.html)(くだ) |  Tube – rubber tube, plumbing pipe. To put it another way, 管 can bend, but 筒 is always straight.
