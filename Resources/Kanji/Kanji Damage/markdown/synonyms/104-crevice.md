##  crevice

隙[間](../kanji/162-a-period-of-time-間.html)(すきま) |  A crack between two different objects. Between the cabinet and the wall. Between two planks in a log cabin.   
---|---  
[裂](../kanji/1316-tear-up-裂.html)[目](../kanji/76-eye-目.html)(さけめ) |  A crack inside of an object:a cracked brick or a rift in a glacier. Your butt. Also, typically すきま is used of 2-dimensional objects, while さけめ is used about 3-dimensional ones.
