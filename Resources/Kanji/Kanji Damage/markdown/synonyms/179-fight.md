##  fight

[喧](../kanji/131-fight-or-quarrel-喧.html)嘩(けんか) |  Any kind of fight – from a verbal quarrel to a back-alley stabbing. [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
---|---  
[戦](../kanji/672-fight-戦.html)う(たたかう) |  Even more broad than 喧嘩, 戦う can be used about fist-fights, but it’s also commonly used in sports, similar to the English “Go! Fight! Win!”. In that case, it’s not that different from 頑張れ！ [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
[闘](../kanji/999-struggle-闘.html)う(たたかう) |  Fight – (only used figuratively) – struggle against temptation, war on poverty etc., the war on drugs, struggle against prejudice, etc.   
もみあい |  Literally, “fondling each other.” Unlike a 戦う (battle), which can go on a long time, a もみあい breaks out suddently – usually in a place where people are drunk. Close to 喧嘩 but more narrow:it’s a shoving match or fist-fight.   
[殴](../kanji/1298-punch-殴.html)[合](../kanji/936-to-suit-合.html)い(なぐりあい) |  Literally, “hitting each other.” 殴り合い is not totally limited to muggings and gang beatings – if you and your friend are drunk and start slapping each other on a dare, that’s also 殴り合い.   
[戦](../kanji/672-fight-戦.html)[闘](../kanji/999-struggle-闘.html)(せんとう) |  A war battle. [NP](../tags/16.html)  
[合](../kanji/936-to-suit-合.html)[戦](../kanji/672-fight-戦.html)(かっせん) |  Like 戦闘、 but only used in proper nouns:the Battle of the Bulge, The Battle of 1812. The Battle of Pearl Harbor, etc.
