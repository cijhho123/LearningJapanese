##  differ

[異](../kanji/1556-to-differ-異.html)なる(ことなる) |  The only verb of the bunch. To differ. と異なる: Use と when one thing is very specifically NOT like the other thing. に異なる: Use に when you want to say that something is different according to the circumstances （like xxxによって違う）   
---|---  
[差](../kanji/874-discriminate-差.html)(さ) |  Mathematical difference. Usually used in math, or the phrase 年の差が大きい！（the age difference between them is so big!)   
[食](../kanji/921-eat-食.html)[違](../kanji/1577-different-and-therefore-wrong-違.html)い(くいちがい) |  This is a BAD difference – a discrepancy or gap (i.e. a credibility gap). [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
[相](../kanji/350-partner-相.html)[違](../kanji/1577-different-and-therefore-wrong-違.html)(そうい) |  A more formal/newspaperish word for a difference between two or more things.   
[間](../kanji/162-a-period-of-time-間.html)[隔](../kanji/1743-segregate-隔.html)(かんかく) |  A distance or gap ;a literal space between two things:between two close-together buildings, say, or between the train and the platform.   
[区](../kanji/680-ward-区.html)[別](../kanji/1314-separate-別.html)(くべつ) |  To MAKE a difference or make a distinction between two things. (Opposite: 混同:こんどう: to confuse one thing with a similar thing)   
[距](../kanji/684-distance-距.html)[離](../kanji/1042-divorce-physical-distance-between-things-離.html)(きょり) |  Physical distance between two things:towns on a map, for instance, or the distance between an archer and a target – a much bigger distance than the related word 間隔.
