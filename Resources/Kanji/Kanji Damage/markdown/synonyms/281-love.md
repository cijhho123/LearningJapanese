##  love

[愛](../kanji/610-love-愛.html)(あい) |  Pure love – usually two-way.   
---|---  
[恋](../kanji/903-passion-恋.html)[愛](../kanji/610-love-愛.html)(れんあい) |  Love or relationships in general – that is to say, if there’s an ad for fortune-telling that says, “Test your luck in money! Love! and health!” it’ll be using 恋愛. If there’s an article in a womens’ magazine, “How to get love in 10 steps!” it’ll be using 恋愛. But you wouldn’t tell a hot guy ” I 恋愛 you very much!” See?   
[恋](../kanji/903-passion-恋.html)(こい) |  Sexual passion.   
[想](../kanji/352-ideaimagination-想.html)い(おもい) |  Usually heard in song lyrics. Means, “The one I’m thinking of.” – can be used whether the crush is recirprocal or not. “The one I’m thinking of, I wonder what he’s doing right now?”
