##  lodge

[泊](../kanji/38-stay-for-the-night-泊.html)まる(とまる) |  Stay overnight – at a hotel or your friends house. This is the one you use when you want to say, “I have nowhere to stay!”   
---|---  
[宿](../kanji/817-lodge-at-宿.html)[泊](../kanji/38-stay-for-the-night-泊.html)(しゅくはく) |  Same as 泊まる, but more formal. Only used in guidebooks. You don’t 宿泊 at a slumber party.
