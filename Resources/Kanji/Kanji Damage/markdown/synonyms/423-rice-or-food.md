##  rice or food

[米](../kanji/384-rice-america-米.html)(こめ) |  Raw, uncooked rice. The kind you buy in bulk.   
---|---  
[飯](../kanji/924-rice-meal-飯.html)(ごはん) |  Cooked rice, but also food in general; “ご飯食べたい?” = “Do you want to eat?” 晩ご飯＝dinner, etc. [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
[食](../kanji/921-eat-食.html)[物](../kanji/1201-animal-thing-物.html)(たべもの) |  Literally ‘edible things.’ Used in only two situations - I’m hungry! You got something to eat around here? おなかが空いた！なんか食べ物ある？ and, Lions hunt their own food. ライオンが自分で食べ物を狩る。   
[料](../kanji/1585-ingredients-fees-料.html)[理](../kanji/445-reason-理.html)(りょうり) |  Two meanings. 1) cooking, as in, “My hobby is cooking.” Or “I’m cooking right now, I can’t talk.” 2:) cuisine, as in, “My favorite cuisine is Mexican. My favorite cuisine is Indonesian.” You don’t say, though, “I’m going to buy some 料理.” Or “I’m eating 料理 right now, I’ll call you back.”
