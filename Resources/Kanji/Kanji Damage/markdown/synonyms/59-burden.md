##  burden

[負](../kanji/559-lose-負.html)[担](../kanji/304-carry-on-your-back-担.html)(ふたん) |  A responsibility, usually financial, which is declared by a system. “If a guest falls off the hotel roof, it’s the hotel’s responsibility.” “The parent is responsible for paying for the child’s lunch at boarding school.”

[背](../kanji/804-stature-back-of-the-body-背.html)[負](../kanji/559-lose-負.html)う(せおう) |  An emotional burden, which is NOT part of a pre-arranged system:Taking care of your retarded sister, dealing with your son’s alcoholism, the feeling that you let down everyone on your team.
