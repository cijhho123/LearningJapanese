##  surface

[表](../kanji/767-express-表.html)(おもて) |  Surface. The emphasis is, “This is the FRONT surface, as opposed to the hidden side.” (opposite:裏 うら). Usually used about 2-dimensional things (paper, walls) but can also be used with the ol’ 建前:本音.   
---|---  
[面](../kanji/813-front-surface-face-面.html)(めん) |  ENTIRE surface of a 3-dimensional object.   
[面](../kanji/813-front-surface-face-面.html)(つら) |  A yakuza slang for face: I can’t believe you’d dare show your face around here!   
[表](../kanji/767-express-表.html)[面](../kanji/813-front-surface-face-面.html)(ひょうめん) |  A literal surface – unlike 面 (men), 表面 is more technical – usually used by architects or craftspeople. Or, for instance, to describe a “skin” you’d put on a polygon 3d character.
