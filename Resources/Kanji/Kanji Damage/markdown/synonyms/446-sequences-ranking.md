##  sequences, ranking

[第](../kanji/894-rank-or-number-in-series-第.html)(だい) |  Ranking by sequence. For example, 第二巻 means “second book in the series.” Also, 第 二 can refer to the #2 person in an organization.   
---|---  
[位](../kanji/153-rank-位.html)(い) |  Ranking by preference:like in a race, the silver medal – the #2 spot, is ２位. Unlike 第一,第二,位 is a temporary ranking, and implies a judgment of performance.   
[成](../kanji/745-become-成.html)[績](../kanji/552-achievements-績.html)(せいせき) |  Ranking of results（i.e., the results of a test, a competition, or when a hostess bar posts the framed pictures of their #1,2,and 3 earners outside the club on the marquee).   
[序](../kanji/1330-first-part-preface-序.html)[列](../kanji/1315-row-列.html)(じょれつ) |  One’s ranking in the pecking order. More limited than 位. 序列 is limited to, say, organization hierarchy or social stratification.   
[成](../kanji/745-become-成.html)[績](../kanji/552-achievements-績.html)(せいせき) |  One’s marks in school.
