##  word

[単](../kanji/596-merely-単.html)[語](../kanji/1574-language-語.html)(たんご) |  A single-kanji word, such as 犬, or a hiragana word like イヤ. But compound words or long-ass words are not 単語.   
---|---  
[言](../kanji/11-say-言.html)[葉](../kanji/1030-leaf-葉.html)(ことば) |  ANY word – including compound words, or onomatopoeia. Also 言葉 can mean “way of speaking.” There are expressions like “watch what you say!” that use 言葉 this way:言葉使い が丁寧 (her way of speaking is very polite!) or 言葉大切につかいましょう （let’s be careful how we speak!)
