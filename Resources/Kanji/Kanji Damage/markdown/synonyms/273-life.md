##  life

[人](../kanji/61-person-人.html)[生](../kanji/473-life-birth-生.html)(じんせい) |  Life, as in someone’s entire life, or life in general:. “He lived a quiet life.” “Life is a bitch.” “Life span.”   
---|---  
[命](../kanji/949-life-命.html)(いのち) |  Life, as a particular person’s life spirit:life-or-death. Life as in a single thing that can be given, taken, lost, etc:“Your money or your life!” said the robber. The doctor’s emergency surgery saved her life.   
[生](../kanji/473-life-birth-生.html)[命](../kanji/949-life-命.html)(せいめい) |  Life – as in biology. Usually used about the beginning or ending of life. “The beginning of life is a miracle!” “xxx lives were lost in the fire.”
