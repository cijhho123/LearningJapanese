##  girl, woman, female

[女](../kanji/6-woman-女.html)[性](../kanji/475-sex-essential-nature-性.html)(じょせい) |  Female – a biological term   
---|---  
[女](../kanji/6-woman-女.html)[人](../kanji/61-person-人.html)(おんなのひと) |  Woman – a social term, unlike the biological term 女性.   
[嬢](../kanji/1673-young-lady-嬢.html)(じょう) |  Girl. Has the implication of an eligible single daughter of a rich family. お嬢さん。 A flattering word used when picking up girls. Up to 20 years old, but if you’re 70 you can use it to a single 40-year-old woman.   
[乙](../kanji/480-second-rank-girl-乙.html)[女](../kanji/6-woman-女.html)(おとめ) |  A 12-19 years old girl. Emphasis is on purity or virginity.   
[女](../kanji/6-woman-女.html)[子](../kanji/5-child-子.html)(おんなのこ) |  A girl, 11-19ish, doesn’t matter if she is a virgin.   
[女](../kanji/6-woman-女.html)[子](../kanji/5-child-子.html)(じょし) |  PREFIX used about women from first grade through college. Used to refer to female students as a group: “女子 line up on the left side of the class, 男子 (danshi) line up on the right side.” You wouldn’t say, “She’s a 女子.”
