##  receive

[受](../kanji/604-receive-受.html)ける(うける) |  To receive anything – a letter, an email, some bad news, a bad influence from the neighbor’s kid...   
---|---  
[受](../kanji/604-receive-受.html)[取](../kanji/310-take-取.html)る(うけとる) |  To receive a physical thing that is handed to you – paperwork or a summons.   
もらう |  Used with another verb, as in 手伝ってもらう or 買ってもらう. To receive an action (from someone) – used when you ask someone for a favor and they do it. [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
くれる |  This is tricky – used by itself, it means “to give.” But if – like もらうー you use it with another verb, as in 助けてくれた or 食べてくれた, it means ‘to receive an action from someone else – THAT THEY DID WITHOUT YOU ASKING’. Literally, “She gave me xxx.”
