##  misunderstand

[誤](../kanji/1735-mistake-誤.html)[解](../kanji/1199-solve-untie-解.html)(ごかい) |  A misunderstanding- often used about situations (a la TV sitcoms) or when you misread someone’s feelings.   
---|---  
[勘](../kanji/1709-perception-勘.html)[違](../kanji/1577-different-and-therefore-wrong-違.html)い(かんちがい) |  Like the related term 誤解 (misunderstanding), but 勘違い is a little smaller. If I’m reading and confuse one kanji with a similar looking kanji, that’s 勘違い. But if I see my fiancée with her cousin from out of town, and assume she’s having an affair and punch the guy, that’s 誤解.
