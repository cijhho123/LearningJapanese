##  way to do

[方](../kanji/84-direction-method-person-方.html)[法](../kanji/1258-law-法.html)(ほうほう) |  Kind of formal. “Way to get a Japanese Visa”. Used in instruction manuals. “Way to operate this dishwasher.”   
---|---  
[仕](../kanji/246-work-仕.html)[方](../kanji/84-direction-method-person-方.html)(しかた) |  Not often used – except in the negative: “仕方ない.” -“There’s no way.” Usually you’d say the name of the thing you’re doing plus 方. 泳ぎ方（how to swim) 跳び方（how to fly) etc.
