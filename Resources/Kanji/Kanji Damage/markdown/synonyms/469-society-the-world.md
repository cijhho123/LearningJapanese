##  society, the world

[世](../kanji/1029-society-世.html)[中](../kanji/489-middle-中.html)(よのなか) |  The social, everyday world – more poetic than 世界。 Used in dramatic declarations like, “I’m sick of this fuckin’ world!” “I don’t care about this life!” “Poor Kurt Cobain, he was just too sensitive for this cruel world!” Also, usually implies JUST Japanese society.
