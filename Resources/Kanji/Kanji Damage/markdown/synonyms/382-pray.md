##  pray

[拝](../kanji/1746-worship-拝.html)む(おがむ) |  Literally, to pray (at a Shinto temple). Figuratively, to worship the ground someone walks on. 拝む also means “to contemplate”, like a stalker his idol of choice, his face like he’s looking at God himself   
---|---  
[祈](../kanji/1496-pray-祈.html)る(いのる) |  Literally, to pray (Christian style). Figuratively, to wish (for good weather, long life to your friends, to pass this fuckin’ math test). Emphasizes the ACT of prayer, rather than the contents.   
[祈](../kanji/1496-pray-祈.html)[願](../kanji/843-beseech-願.html)(きがん) |  A figurative prayer or wish, made to pass a test or to not go to jail, etc.
