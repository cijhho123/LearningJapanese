##  tell the difference, discriminate

[区](../kanji/680-ward-区.html)[別](../kanji/1314-separate-別.html)(くべつ) |  To tell the difference between two similar things. If you want to tell your Japanese friend, “I can never tell the difference between the synonyms X and Y,” then you’d use 区別出来ない！ [TTT](../tags/79.html)  
---|---  
[差](../kanji/874-discriminate-差.html)[別](../kanji/1314-separate-別.html)(さべつ) |  In theory this means the same as 区別、 but in practice it’s almost always used to mean ‘discriminate’ – as in bigotry. The only exception is the phrase 差別化、 which is corporate-speak for “make our products stand out from the competition.” [PPP](../tags/78.html)
