##  sign or symptom

[兆](../kanji/1652-omen-兆.html)[候](../kanji/1049-climatecandidate-候.html)(ちょうこう) |  A sign that something will start soon:(Those clouds on the horizon are a sign a taiphoon is coming)   
---|---  
[前](../kanji/865-before-前.html)[兆](../kanji/1652-omen-兆.html)(ぜんちょう) |  A sign that something has already started (That slamming door means my wife has come home from work hella mad and I shouldn’t disturb her).   
[縁](../kanji/1222-rim-omen-縁.html)[起](../kanji/721-wake-up-occur-起.html)(えんぎ) |  An omen in a superstitious way (I saw a rainbow after I bet my life savings on a racehorse- that means he’ll win!) Unlike the English word ‘omen’, 縁起 can be both good and bad.   
[直](../kanji/1308-correct-a-problem-direct-contact-直.html)[感](../kanji/755-feeling-感.html)(ちょっかん) |  premonition – unlike 縁起、 直感 is not based on anything. More like a hunch, but with a supernatural tinge to it. Can be bad or good.
