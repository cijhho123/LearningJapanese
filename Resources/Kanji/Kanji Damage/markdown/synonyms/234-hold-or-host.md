##  hold or host

[催](../kanji/827-sponsor-催.html)す(もよおす) |  To host a small event – a party, for instance. Unlike 主催、 催す has the idea that you’re responsible for what happens, rather than just giving money to the event.   
---|---  
[主](../kanji/454-mastermainly-主.html)[催](../kanji/827-sponsor-催.html)(しゅさい) |  To sponsor a large event. “This golf tournament is brought to you by Penzoil.”   
[開](../kanji/1185-open-開.html)[催](../kanji/827-sponsor-催.html)(かいさい) |  To open a huge event. The opening ceremonies. “Is that expo open yet?”
