##  reproduction

[生](../kanji/473-life-birth-生.html)[殖](../kanji/1313-agricultural-breeding-reproduction-殖.html)(せいしょく) |  These are both kind of scientific, non-sexy words for reproduction, but 生殖 means reproduction in a very narrow sense:as in ‘reproductive organs,’ or ‘the reproductive process’   
---|---  
[繁](../kanji/712-multiple-many-繁.html)[殖](../kanji/1313-agricultural-breeding-reproduction-殖.html)(はんしょく) |  Breeding. Unlike the related term 生殖、繁殖 refers to the entire process of finding a mate, not just the physical act of insemination:Breeding grounds. Mating season. Farmers breeding livestock.
