##  rare

[珍](../kanji/1121-very-rare-珍.html)しい(めずらしい) |  Unusual. [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
---|---  
まれ |  Also means unusual, but more formal/newspapery. Also has the connotation of rare-because-it’s-valuable (endangered species, a rare talent, etc.) If you see a dog-turd shaped like Lyle Lovett, that’s 珍しい, not まれ。   
[希](../kanji/1095-request-uncommon-希.html)[少](../kanji/193-a-little-amount-少.html)な(きしょうな) |  Rare animals or plants. academic word. [NP](../tags/16.html)  
[妙](../kanji/195-odd-妙.html)に(みょうに) |  unusual and strange! Usually used about behavior. Something strange is going on, I can feel it! She’s acting strange today. That homeless guy is acting weird.
