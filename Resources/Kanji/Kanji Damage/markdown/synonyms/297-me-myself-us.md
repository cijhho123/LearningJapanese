##  me, myself, us

[身](../kanji/1595-ones-own-flesh-身.html)(み) |  Means ‘me’ but usually it’s only used in certain set idioms, so don’t use this one when you write, unless you know the idiom.

[自](../kanji/78-my-own-自.html)[分](../kanji/169-understand-divide-minute-分.html)(じぶん) |  Means ‘me’ but also means ‘you’, like 彼が自分のゴハンを持ってきた.（He brought his own lunch).   
[我](../kanji/669-we-我.html)(われ) |  Me (or, often, 我々, ‘us’) – kind of a pompous, old-man word.ZZZ
