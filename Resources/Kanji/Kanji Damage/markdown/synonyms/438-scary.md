[恐](../kanji/1291-dread-恐.html)ろしい(おそろしい) |  Horrible, terrifying. Like すさまじい、 恐ろしい emphasizes the extent or force of something, rather than its appearance or fangs or whatever. He drove the car at a terrifying, terribly fast speed. You’ll shudder when you learn the awful truth about the president!   
  
Wait, here’s a better distinction: Use 怖い or おっかない about scary things you saw with your own eyes, but USE 恐ろしい OR すさまじい ABOUT THINGS YOU READ ABOUT / HEARD ABOUT.
