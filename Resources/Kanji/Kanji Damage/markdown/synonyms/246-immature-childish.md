##  immature, childish

[幼](../kanji/205-childhood-幼.html)[稚](../kanji/368-childish-稚.html)(ようち) |  Childish, immature, puerile. “Hey I wrote POOP on a picture of the president! Want to come see?”   
---|---  
[幼](../kanji/205-childhood-幼.html)い(おさない) |  Like 幼稚, but one difference:if you use it about an actual child, it doesn't have a bad meaning. It just means ‘of children’ – like ‘childhood memory’ or ‘At an early age,’ or ‘in infancy.’
