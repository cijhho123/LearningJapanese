##  pollution

[汚](../kanji/653-dirty-汚.html)[染](../kanji/365-dye-染.html)(おせん) |  Pollution OR contamination. If you want to say “environmental pollution,” you have to use 汚染, not 公害. [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
---|---  
[公](../kanji/137-public-公.html)[害](../kanji/462-damage-害.html)(こうがい) |  Pollution. Has a more broad meaning than the related word 汚染. Not just dirty smoggy air, but also noise pollution, the annoyance of someone talking on a cell phone on a train, and 情報公害 （じょうほうこうがい）:“ad pollution” – the inescapable advertisements one sees everywhere.
