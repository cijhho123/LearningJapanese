[意](../kanji/157-meaning-意.html)[地](../kanji/1600-area-地.html)[汚](../kanji/653-dirty-汚.html)い(いじきたない) |  This is an odd one! This is an insult used on someone who is so penny-pinching that they don’t have self-respect. Like if their hot dog falls on the dirt, they’ll pick it up and eat it, rather than buy another. Or if they lick the bowl clean because dammed if they didn’t pay for every molecule of soup. This isn’t stingy in the western sense of short-changing other people, refusing to tip, etc. Usually used like this:意地汚い人間.
