##  training

[練](../kanji/358-practice-練.html)[習](../kanji/1535-learn-習.html)(れんしゅう) |  Practice – usually used with sports or performance (theater, band, etc.). Includes both physical and mental training.   
---|---  
[訓](../kanji/856-kunyomi-訓.html)[練](../kanji/358-practice-練.html)(くんれん) |  Martial arts / basic training in the army. The connotation is that it’s physical, and you do it over and over until you become a master of it.   
[研](../kanji/1189-polish-sharpen-研.html)[修](../kanji/1118-master-a-skill-修.html)(けんしゅう) |  Special advanced study – like at a post-graduate seminar.
