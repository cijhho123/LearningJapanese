##  remodel, improve, reform

[改](../kanji/720-renew-improve-改.html)[造](../kanji/808-produce-造.html)(かいぞう) |  Remodel a house or building.   
---|---  
[改](../kanji/720-renew-improve-改.html)[善](../kanji/1632-morally-good-善.html)(かいぜん) |  Improve a thing – your performance in Math class / the economy / the efficiency of the car   
[改](../kanji/720-renew-improve-改.html)[良](../kanji/775-good-良.html)する(かいりょうする) |  Reform or improve   
[改](../kanji/720-renew-improve-改.html)[善](../kanji/1632-morally-good-善.html)(かいぜん) |  Almost the same as 改良:to improve something – like new technology improves the productivity of the factory, but also like “If you don’t improve your grades, you can’t stay on the varsity football team, young man.”   
[改](../kanji/720-renew-improve-改.html)[良](../kanji/775-good-良.html)(かいりょう) |  Almost the same as 改善 , but usually used only about technology, rather than social reform or personal betterment.   
[変](../kanji/904-change-変.html)[更](../kanji/1591-all-over-again-更.html)(へんこう) |  To change, but NOT to improve:to change the schedule of a meeting, or change a plan – (‘Instead of completing the building on January, we’ll finish it in March’)   
[更](../kanji/1591-all-over-again-更.html)[新](../kanji/345-new-新.html)(こうしん) |  Renew – a license or a library book.   
[改](../kanji/720-renew-improve-改.html)める(あらためる) |  Change PLUS improvement. Usually used with behavior – meaning to turn over a new leaf, to quit self-destructive or bad behavior. Also has a totally unrelated meaning:When a customs official or police asks to see your ID card, that’s also 改める for some reason.   
[改](../kanji/720-renew-improve-改.html)[革](../kanji/514-leatherrevolution-革.html)/(かいかく) |  Radical revolutionary change or total overhaul.   
レフォーム |  Can mean re-modeling a building, but also means corporate re-structuring (i.e. firing people)   
矯める(ためる) |  Fix or improve a system, correct a bad habit.
