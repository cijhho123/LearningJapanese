##  intimidate, threaten

[脅](../kanji/178-threaten-脅.html)す(おどす) |  The normal word for threaten. [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
---|---  
[脅](../kanji/178-threaten-脅.html)かす(おどかす) |  To intimidate. 脅かす doesn’t really sound like a literal “Do this or else I’ll break your legs!” type of verbal threat. If a big enough guy shows up at your house at 3AM, he doesn’t need to put it into words.   
[脅](../kanji/178-threaten-脅.html)[迫](../kanji/330-press-upon-迫.html)(きょうはく) |  More formal. Not just to threaten, but to be intimidating.   
[恐](../kanji/1291-dread-恐.html)喝(きょうかつ) |  The legal term for blackmail or extortion.
