##  downfall

[没](../kanji/1296-downfall-没.html)[落](../kanji/612-falldrop-落.html)(ぼつらく) |  To be ruined, bankrupt, downfallen. But usually ruined, or washed-up. [NP](../tags/16.html)  
---|---  
[滅](../kanji/751-annihilate-滅.html)[亡](../kanji/104-dying-亡.html)(めつぼう) |  The downfall of a civilization or the dying-out of a certain ethnic group.   
[崩](../kanji/828-collapse-崩.html)[壊](../kanji/1681-break-壊.html)(ほうかい) |  To collapse – said of a building, but also (figuratively) like ‘The collapse of communism.’   
[破](../kanji/1324-rend-破.html)[壊](../kanji/1681-break-壊.html)(はかい) |  Destruction. This usually means physical destruction, but if you’re looking for a word with the ‘this will be your downfall!’ blame-a-dude aspect, 破壊 works better than the previous two.   
[絶](../kanji/1214-extinct-絶.html)[滅](../kanji/751-annihilate-滅.html)(ぜつめつ) |  (for animals) To go extinct.   
  
Neither 滅亡 nor 崩壊 have the moral ‘It’s your own fault’ judgment of the English word ‘downfall.’
