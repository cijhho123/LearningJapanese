##  art or literary criticism.

[批](../kanji/298-call-bullshit-on-批.html)[評](../kanji/189-art-or-literary-criticism-評.html)する(ひひょうする) |  Review of one specific performance (showbiz event, exhibition, play, etc.) More commonly used as a verb.   
---|---  
[評](../kanji/189-art-or-literary-criticism-評.html)[論](../kanji/972-make-a-case-for-論.html)(ひょうろん) |  Critique of an entire genre. More often literary (novels, plays) Usually used as a noun. [NP](../tags/16.html)  
  
Both words can mean harsh judgment but not necessarily so.
