##  correspond to

[一](../kanji/1-one-line-radical-一.html)[致](../kanji/1255-polite-do-致.html)(いっち) |  To have the same idea. To be in keeping with. Used about ideas or attitudes. “His attitude towards immigration corresponds to my own.” Can be used about numbers – but only in the sense of agreement:“We both conducted the same experiment and our data agree.”   
---|---  
[該](../kanji/1698-correspond-to-該.html)[当](../kanji/634-hit-the-target-当.html)(がいとう) |  To correspond to. This is the one to use if you want to say, “Which Japanese word corresponds to the English word ‘dingleberry?’” Or if you’re explaining a graph, and you want to say, “The vertical axis represents time, and the horizontal axis represents profit.” You’d use 該当. [$$$](../tags/75.html)  
[当](../kanji/634-hit-the-target-当.html)たる(あたる) |  Basically the same as 該当、but 当たる also has a LOT of other meanings, whereas 該当 is more specific.   
[適](../kanji/716-suitable-適.html)[応](../kanji/1128-react-応.html)(てきおう) |  Usually means ‘adapt to’, but sometimes it means ‘apply to’, as in, ‘This restriction applies to children, but not to adults.’   
[意](../kanji/157-meaning-意.html)[味](../kanji/383-flavor-味.html)する(いみする) |  To stand for or symbolize something. “The ‘E’ in ‘E = mc2’ stands for ‘energy.’
