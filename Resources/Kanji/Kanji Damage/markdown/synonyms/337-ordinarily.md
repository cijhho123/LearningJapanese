##  ordinarily

[大](../kanji/397-big-大.html)[分](../kanji/169-understand-divide-minute-分.html)(だいぶ) |  Has to do with extent. It’s closer to かなり or ほど than 普通。 EXAMPLE: It was pretty far: だいぶ遠かった。   
---|---  
[常](../kanji/1630-usual-常.html)に(つねに) |  Often defined as “ordinarily”, but it’s stronger! More like “always”. “A professional spy always carries a gun!” “I never leave the house without my camera.” Etc.   
[常](../kanji/1630-usual-常.html)[事](../kanji/1660-action-incident-事.html)(じょうじ) |  Regular (as opposed to intermittent) or consistent (as opposed to you have to special-order it). You’ll find this on signs for, say, regular trains that run every hour on the hour, or security guards that are on-call or on-patrol 24-7.
