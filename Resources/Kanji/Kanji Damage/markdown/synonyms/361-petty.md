##  petty

些[細](../kanji/213-slender-細.html)(ささい) |  Annoying petty details. (An application form, procedure, etc.)   
---|---  
[細](../kanji/213-slender-細.html)かい(こまかい) |  Similar to 些細. You can understand the difference like this: the procedure is 些細 because it’s full of 細かい details. However, unlike 些細、細かい can also have a good meaning (fine or detailed textures on a work of art), so be careful.   
[執](../kanji/185-to-hella-do-執.html)[念](../kanji/945-concern-念.html)[深](../kanji/569-deep-深.html)い(しゅうねんぶかい) |  If you want to use ‘petty’ in the sense of a person who is vindictive or spiteful, use this. But it doesn’t exactly fit the English version:執念深い applies equally to grudges that are legitimate as well as grudges like “You ate my doughnut so I’ll get you back next year as you’re about to get promoted.” [PPP](../tags/78.html)
