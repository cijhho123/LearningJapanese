##  sink

[沈](../kanji/1692-sink-沈.html)む(しずむ) |  To submerge (a rubber ducky in the tub, a submarine submerges). Doesn’t always have the bad meaning of 沈没, but it can: Venice is sinking into the sea 1 cm a year!!! Also, unlike 沈没、沈む can be used figuratively:one can become ‘submerged’ in grief, self-pity, depression. [$$$](../tags/75.html)[TTT](../tags/79.html)
