# NINJAL "Loanword Paraphrase Proposals" - offline mirror

Source: <https://www2.ninjal.ac.jp/gairaigo/> ("「外来語」言い換え提案", National Institute for
Japanese Language and Linguistics / 国立国語研究所).

A mirror of the loanword (外来語) paraphrase-proposal section of NINJAL's site. Between 2002 and 2006
a NINJAL committee reviewed katakana loanwords used in public-facing Japanese (government notices,
news, official forms) that surveys showed a large share of native readers - especially readers 60
and over - did not understand, and published plain-Japanese paraphrase suggestions for them in four
rounds.

## Contents

252 files, 8.8 MB total:

- `iikae-teian-1-4 (comprehensive edition, 176 words).pdf` (2.6 MB) and
  `iikaegotou-dic (alphabetical dictionary list).html` (71 KB) - two top-level reference copies of
  the comprehensive edition, sitting alongside the mirror rather than inside it.
- `offline copy/www2.ninjal.ac.jp/gairaigo/` (250 files, 6.3 MB) - the site mirror itself:
  - `index.html`, `gaira.css`, `member2.html`, `syuisyo.html` - the section's landing page,
    stylesheet, committee roster and founding charter.
  - `Teian1/`, `Teian2/`, `Teian3/` - the first three rounds' proposals as individual PDFs
    (2003, 2003, 2004).
  - `Teian1_4/` - the four-round comprehensive edition (2006): the combined PDF, a usage guide
    (`tebiki.html`), a legend (`hanrei.html`), an index of proposed terms (`iikaego.html`), and
    `Words/` - 175 individual per-word HTML pages, each giving the loanword, its proposed
    paraphrase(s), example sentences with the paraphrase shown as furigana over the loanword,
    a plain-language definition, usage notes, and separate comprehension-rating stars for the
    general public versus readers 60 and over.
  - `Book/` - an excerpt of the print edition of the proposals (『分かりやすく伝える 外来語 言い換え
    手引き』, ぎょうせい, 2006).
  - `Haifu/` - committee meeting-minutes PDFs. Rounds 1-19 are present; the index page's own
    archive additionally lists rounds 20 and 21 (August and December 2005), which are not present
    in this mirror.
  - `enq/` - results of the committee's questionnaires to local governments and national ministries
    on loanword use in public communication.

## What was excluded

The mirror deliberately omits `Report126/` - 国立国語研究所報告126, "公共媒体の外来語 ―「外来語」言い換え
提案を支える調査研究―" (NINJAL Research Report 126, the survey methodology behind the paraphrase
proposals). That report is a separate research document consisting mostly of figure and chart
images, not further loanword-paraphrase material - the paraphrase proposals were the target of this
mirror, not the report plates behind them. Report126 alone accounted for the bulk of an earlier,
much larger pull of this section (roughly 1,565 files and 80 MB); it has been removed, and this
copy should not be extended to include it.

One consequence of the removal is visible in `index.html`: under the heading
"「外来語」にかかわる調査" ("Research relating to loanwords"), the first link
(`Report126/report126.html`) points to a file that is not present in this mirror. That is the
expected result of the deliberate exclusion, not a crawl failure - every other link on that page,
and the rest of the section's navigation, resolves within the mirror.

## Caveat

The proposals reflect Japanese public-communication norms and reader-comprehension survey data from
2002-2006; some of the loanwords judged difficult at the time (e.g. early IT and finance terms) may
read as far more familiar today. The per-word comprehension-rating stars are the committee's own
survey results, not an independently reproducible measurement.
