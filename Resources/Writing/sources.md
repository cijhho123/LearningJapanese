# Sources and provenance

Everything in this folder that was fetched from the internet, where it came from, and what
you are actually allowed to do with it. Retrieved dates are when the file was downloaded,
not when the document was published.

## Official Japanese orthography (`Official orthography/`)

These are the **primary sources** - the actual cabinet notifications and Agency for Cultural
Affairs reports that define correct written Japanese. They replace guesswork about okurigana,
kana usage and kanji-vs-kana with the text of the rule itself.

| Resource | Source URL | Retrieved | License / status | Why it is worth having |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [常用漢字表 (2010)](<./Official orthography/joyo kanji table 2010 - 常用漢字表.pdf>) | [bunka.go.jp](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf) | 2026-09-17 | Japanese government publication (文化庁). Officially and freely published; copyright held by the agency, governed by the MEXT website terms of use, which adopt the 政府標準利用規約 (reuse with source attribution). Not a formal CC licence - see the note below. | The 2,136-character list **as promulgated**, 164 pages: every character with its official on/kun readings, the 付表 of irregular readings (熟字訓), and the 字体 notes. | [常用漢字表 別紙](<./Official orthography/joyo kanji table appendix - 常用漢字表 別紙.pdf>) | [bunka.go.jp](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyobesi_20101130.pdf) | 2026-09-17 | Same as above | 4-page appendix to the above: the list of characters added and removed in the 2010 revision. Okurigana is the single most-guessed-at part of written Japanese - 行う vs 行なう, 表す vs 表わす - and this is the document that decides it. |
| [現代仮名遣い](<./Official orthography/modern kana usage - 現代仮名遣い.pdf>) | [bunka.go.jp](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/gendaikana/pdf/94380801_01.pdf) | 2026-09-17 | Same as above | Cabinet notification, 1986. 11 pages defining modern kana spelling: when を/は/へ are particles, the ぢ/じ and づ/ず cases, long vowels, and the 本則/許容 split. | [外来語の表記](<./Official orthography/loanword transcription - 外来語の表記.pdf>) | [bunka.go.jp](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/gairai/pdf/94380801_03.pdf) | 2026-09-17 | Same as above | Cabinet notification, 1991. 14 pages on writing loanwords in katakana: the standard table, the second table of less-established forms (ヴ, ティ, ツィ...), and rules for the long-vowel mark ー. Directly useful because katakana loanwords are where learners' spelling goes wrong most often. |
| [公用文作成の考え方 (2022)](<./Official orthography/official document writing guidance 2022 - 公用文作成の考え方.pdf>) | [bunka.go.jp](https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/bunkashingikai/kokugo/hokoku/pdf/93651301_01.pdf) | 2026-09-17 | Same as above | 文化審議会建議, 2022, 63 pages including the 解説. The modern replacement for the 1952 公用文作成の要領. Covers formal **written register** directly: when to write a word in kanji vs kana, 、and 。 usage, brackets, numerals, full-width vs half-width, and how to write for a public audience. The closest thing to an official Japanese style guide, and the best single answer to "is this sentence written correctly, not just grammatically". |
| [公用文における漢字使用等について (2010)](<./Official orthography/kanji usage in official documents 2010 - 公用文における漢字使用等について.pdf>) | [bunka.go.jp](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/sanko/koyobun/pdf/kunrei.pdf) | 2026-09-17 | Same as above | 内閣訓令第1号 (2010) plus its 別紙, 5 pages. The short **operational memo** that turns 常用漢字表 and 送り仮名の付け方 into concrete word-by-word decisions: exactly which pronouns, adverbs and conjunctions to write in kanji versus kana in an official document (俺・彼・誰 in kanji; かなり・ふと in kana; とき・ところ・わけ in kana even though joyo kanji forms exist), plus the specific list of words where okurigana is conventionally dropped (取扱い→取扱, 打合せ, 見積り...). The closest thing in this folder to a one-page orthography cheat sheet - the government's own distillation, not somebody's summary of one. |
| [常用漢字表の字体・字形に関する指針 (2016) - 抄](<./Official orthography/joyo kanji forms guidance excerpt 2016 - 常用漢字表の字体・字形に関する指針(抄).pdf>) | [moj.go.jp](https://www.moj.go.jp/content/001249387.pdf) | 2026-09-17 | Japanese government publication (法務省 hosting a 文化審議会国語分科会 report). Covered by 公共データ利用規約 (PDL1.0), the current name for the same government reuse policy described in the note below. | The full 文化審議会報告 is 35.6 MB and the press-release version 20.5 MB, both too large to include - see the "Not included here" table below. This is a 7-page **抄 (excerpt)**, hosted by the Ministry of Justice for its own 戸籍 kanji-shape purposes, covering the load-bearing chapter: the difference between 字体 (character identity, which matters) and 明朝体 デザイン差 / 手書き楷書 variation (which does not), with the 硬/吸/頃 and 睡/異/挨 comparison diagrams that answer "my handwriting doesn't match the textbook form - is that wrong?". Not the whole report, but the part that settles the question, at roughly 1/20th the size. |

> **Honest note on the licence column.** These are official government documents, published
> free of charge on the agency's own website, and reproducing them for private study is
> plainly fine. But bunka.go.jp does *not* stamp them with a Creative Commons licence - the
> site asserts copyright and defers to the MEXT website terms of use, which adopt the
> Government Standard Terms of Use. That standard normally permits copying, redistribution
> and adaptation with source attribution, which is why they are here. If you ever want to
> republish these, read the current terms yourself rather than trusting this row.

## Practice paper (`Practice paper/`)

All five sheets come from 方眼紙ネット (houganshi.net), which states its terms as: anyone,
corporate or individual, may use the PDFs free of charge, and **modification and
redistribution are permitted without restriction** (a link back is appreciated, not
required). That is the cleanest licence of anything in this folder, which is why these were
chosen over the many prettier Japanese printable sites whose terms forbid redistribution.

Print with page scaling set to **none**, or the squares come out the wrong size.
