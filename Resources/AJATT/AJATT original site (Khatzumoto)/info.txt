Full site mirror (wget --mirror --convert-links --adjust-extension --page-requisites --no-parent,
--reject-regex "replytocom" to skip WordPress comment-reply URL variants which are not distinct
content). The live site itself is a static HTTrack export of the original alljapaneseallthetime.com
WordPress blog, hosted on GitHub Pages under the successor domain - the WordPress markup
(wp-content, wp-includes paths) and the "Added by HTTrack" comment in the page source are both
original-site artifacts preserved in that export, not something this mirror added.

Two orphan pages were not reachable by crawling alone (not linked from any page wget visited) and
were fetched by their known URLs instead:

    all-japanese-all-the-time-ajatt-how-to-learn-japanese-on-your-own-having-fun-and-to-fluency/
  - the-best-well-least-crappy-of-ajatt/ (the "Best of AJATT" curated index)

completed into a full mirror; the other four (aim-to-fail/, showing-up/,
