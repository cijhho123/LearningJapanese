## Honest licence caveats

1. **Only one item here is unambiguously open-licence: the Nguyen and Doan (2025) Frontiers paper (CC BY 4.0).** Everything else is either "free to read, author's copyright, no reuse licence" (the AJATT site, both Krashen books, the ERIC paper) or has no licence statement at all (lazyguidejp's *content*, i.e. the guide's prose - though the *repository/code* is MIT; morg.systems). Read on the source site or cite properly if this repo is ever made public.
2. **lazyguidejp has the cleanest licence status of the material here.** MIT-licensed repository, genuinely redistributable, no caveats.
3. **The AJATT site and Krashen's books are both explicitly offered for free by their respective authors**, which is a stronger footing than a plain "no licence stated" grey area, even without a formal Creative Commons tag.
4. **morg.systems has no stated licence at all** - treat it the same as the no-licence personal sites elsewhere in this folder (e.g. the podcast transcripts in `../Listening/`): personal study copy, not for redistribution, source always attributed.
